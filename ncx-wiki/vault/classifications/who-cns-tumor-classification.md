# WHO CNS Tumor Classification

The 5th edition WHO Classification of CNS Tumors (2021) integrates molecular markers alongside histopathology for tumor classification. This represents a fundamental shift from purely histological to molecular-informed diagnosis.

## Grading System

WHO grades reflect biological behavior and prognosis:

| Grade | Biological behavior | Growth | Example |
|-------|-------------------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Low proliferative, potentially curable by surgery | Slow | Pilocytic astrocytoma, meningioma |
| 2 | Infiltrative, tendency to recur | Slow-moderate | Diffuse astrocytoma IDH-mutant, atypical meningioma |
| 3 | Histologic evidence of malignancy | Moderate-fast | Anaplastic astrocytoma, anaplastic meningioma |
| 4 | Cytologically malignant, rapid growth, necrosis | Rapid | Glioblastoma, medulloblastoma, AT/RT |

## Major Categories

### Adult-type Diffuse Gliomas

| Tumor | Key molecular marker | WHO grade |
|-------|---------------------|-----------|
| Astrocytoma, IDH-mutant | IDH1/2 mutation, no 1p/19q codeletion | 2, 3, or 4 |
| Oligodendroglioma, IDH-mutant | IDH1/2 mutation + 1p/19q codeletion | 2 or 3 |
| [[glioblastoma\|Glioblastoma]], IDH-wildtype | IDH-wildtype + TERT/EGFR/+7/-10 | 4 |

### Circumscribed Astrocytic Gliomas

| Tumor | WHO grade |
|-------|-----------|
| Pilocytic astrocytoma | 1 |
| Pleomorphic xanthoastrocytoma (PXA) | 2 or 3 |
| Subependymal giant cell astrocytoma (SEGA) | 1 |
| Chordoid glioma | 2 |
| High-grade astrocytoma with piloid features | N/A |

### Glioneuronal and Neuronal Tumors

| Tumor | WHO grade |
|-------|-----------|
| Ganglioglioma | 1 |
| Dysembryoplastic neuroepithelial tumor (DNT) | 1 |
| Central neurocytoma | 2 |
| Diffuse leptomeningeal glioneuronal tumor (DLGNT) | 2 or 3 |

### Ependymal Tumors

| Tumor | WHO grade | Key molecular |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| Supratentorial ependymoma, ZFTA fusion-positive | 2 or 3 | ZFTA (C11orf95) fusion |
| Posterior fossa group A (PFA) | 2 or 3 | H3K27me3 loss |
| Posterior fossa group B (PFB) | 2 or 3 | |
| Myxopapillary ependymoma | 2 | (upgraded from grade 1) |
| Subependymoma | 1 | |

### Embryonal Tumors

| Tumor | WHO grade |
|-------|-----------|
| [[medulloblastoma\|Medulloblastoma]], WNT-activated | 4 |
| Medulloblastoma, SHH-activated, TP53-wildtype | 4 |
| Medulloblastoma, SHH-activated, TP53-mutant | 4 |
| Medulloblastoma, non-WNT/non-SHH | 4 |
| Atypical teratoid/rhabdoid tumor (AT/RT) | 4 |

### [[meningioma\|Meningiomas]]

| Subtype | WHO grade |
|---------|-----------|
| Meningothelial, fibrous, transitional, psammomatous, angiomatous, microcystic, secretory, lymphoplasmacyte-rich, metaplastic | 1 |
| Chordoid, clear cell, atypical | 2 |
| Papillary, rhabdoid, anaplastic (malignant) | 3 |

### Cranial and Paraspinal Nerve Tumors

| Tumor | WHO grade |
|-------|-----------|
| Schwannoma (all subtypes) | 1 |
| Neurofibroma (all subtypes) | 1 |
| MPNST | 2, 3, or 4 |

### Tumors of the Sellar Region

| Tumor | Key feature |
|-------|------------|
| Adamantinomatous craniopharyngioma | CTNNB1 mutation |
| Papillary craniopharyngioma | BRAF V600E |
| PitNET (pituitary neuroendocrine tumors) | Replaces "pituitary adenoma" terminology |

## Key Changes from 4th Edition

1. **Molecular integration mandatory** — IDH status defines adult glioma types
2. **Grading within type** — astrocytoma IDH-mutant can be grade 2, 3, or 4 (replaces "glioblastoma IDH-mutant")
3. **Layered diagnosis** — integrated diagnosis, histological diagnosis, CNS WHO grade, molecular information
4. **PitNET replaces adenoma** — recognizes neuroendocrine tumor biology
5. **Myxopapillary ependymoma upgraded** — now grade 2 (was grade 1)
6. **Medulloblastoma molecular subtypes** — WNT, SHH, non-WNT/non-SHH define prognosis
