# Tag Taxonomy — Controlled Vocabulary

Canonical tags for the NCX Neurosurgery Wiki. New pages MUST use tags from this list.

Entity-identifying information (specific arteries, nerves, cisterns, etc.) lives in the page filename and title — NOT in tags. Tags are categorical and topical.

## Domain Tags
- `ncx` — NCX wiki page
- `anatomy` — general anatomical structure
- `rhoton-bridge` — cross-referenced with Rhoton wiki content

## Region Tags
- `skull-base` — cranial base structures
- `anterior-fossa` — anterior cranial fossa
- `middle-fossa` — middle cranial fossa
- `posterior-fossa` — infratentorial compartment
- `foramen-magnum` — craniocervical junction bone
- `craniocervical-junction` — occipitoatlantoaxial complex
- `cerebrum` — supratentorial hemisphere
- `deep-structures` — basal ganglia, thalamus, diencephalon, internal capsule
- `sellar-region` — sella turcica and parasellar
- `cavernous-sinus` — cavernous sinus and lateral wall
- `tentorial-incisura` — incisural spaces
- `temporal-bone` — petrous, mastoid, squamous
- `brainstem` — midbrain, pons, medulla
- `cerebellopontine-angle` — CPA cistern region
- `pineal-region` — pineal and quadrigeminal region
- `orbit` — orbital contents and walls
- `infratemporal-fossa` — infratemporal anatomy
- `supratentorial` — above tentorium cerebelli
- `infratentorial` — below tentorium cerebelli
- `spinal` — spinal cord and column
- `intraventricular` — within ventricular system

## System Tags
- `vasculature` — arterial structures (generic)
- `venous-system` — venous and dural sinus structures
- `cranial-nerves` — CN I-XII
- `white-matter` — fiber tracts, capsule, radiation, fiber dissection
- `cisterns` — subarachnoid cisterns and spaces
- `ventricles` — ventricular system (lateral, third, fourth, aqueduct)
- `perforators` — perforating arteries
- `meninges` — dura, arachnoid, pia
- `visual-pathway` — optic nerve/chiasm/tract/radiation
- `motor-pathway` — corticospinal and related tracts
- `limbic-system` — hippocampus, amygdala, memory circuit

## Circulation Tags
- `anterior-circulation` — ICA/MCA/ACA territory
- `posterior-circulation` — vertebrobasilar territory
- `circle-of-willis` — arterial circle and variants
- `external-carotid` — ECA branches

## Surgical Tags
- `approaches` — surgical approach/corridor pages

- `craniectomy` — bone removal without replacement
- `osteotomy` — bone cuts for skull base exposures
- `microsurgery` — microsurgical technique and principles
- `endoscopic` — endoscopic/endonasal techniques
- `minimally-invasive` — minimally invasive approaches
- `keyhole` — keyhole craniotomy variants
- `surgical-technique` — operative methodology
- `surgical-corridor` — anatomical corridor used in approaches
- `surgical-landmark` — intraoperative landmarks
- `decompression` — microvascular decompression, decompressive craniectomy
- `hemostasis` — bleeding control
- `csf-diversion` — shunts and drainage
- `positioning` — patient positioning and setup
- `instrumentation` — surgical instruments and equipment
