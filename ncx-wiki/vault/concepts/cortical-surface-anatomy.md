# Cortical Surface Anatomy

Understanding cerebral sulci and gyri is fundamental for neurosurgical planning — identifying eloquent cortex, planning craniotomies, and navigating to deep-seated lesions.

## Superolateral Surface

### Key Sulci

| Sulcus | Identification | Significance |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| **Central sulcus (Rolando)** | Deepest sulcus, runs from interhemispheric fissure to [[sylvian-fissure]], omega-shaped "hand knob" on axial MRI | Separates motor (precentral) from sensory (postcentral) cortex |
| **Sylvian fissure** | Most prominent sulcus, horizontal | See [[sylvian-fissure]] |
| **Superior frontal sulcus** | Parallel to interhemispheric fissure | Separates SFG from MFG |
| **Inferior frontal sulcus** | Below SFG sulcus | Separates MFG from IFG |
| **Intraparietal sulcus** | Posterior continuation of postcentral sulcus | Divides SPL from IPL |

### How to Differentiate Central Sulcus

The central sulcus (CS) can be identified by multiple criteria:
1. **Omega sign** on axial MRI — "hand knob" shape at the CS
2. CS typically does not reach the Sylvian fissure (separated by subcentral gyrus)
3. Precentral gyrus is wider than postcentral gyrus
4. Superior frontal sulcus points to the precentral sulcus (not CS)
5. CS connects to interhemispheric fissure (postcentral does not always)

### Frontal Lobe Gyri

| Gyrus | Location | Function |
|-------|----------|----------|
| **Precentral** | Between precentral sulcus and CS | Primary motor cortex (M1) |
| **Superior frontal (SFG)** | Above superior frontal sulcus | Supplementary motor area (medial), executive function |
| **Middle frontal (MFG)** | Between superior and inferior frontal sulci | Frontal eye field, working memory |
| **Inferior frontal (IFG)** | Below inferior frontal sulcus | Pars opercularis, triangularis, orbitalis — [[broca-area\|Broca's area]] (dominant hemisphere) |

### Temporal Lobe Gyri

| Gyrus | Location | Function |
|-------|----------|----------|
| **Superior temporal (STG)** | Below Sylvian fissure | Heschl's gyrus (primary auditory), Wernicke's area (posterior STG, dominant) |
| **Middle temporal (MTG)** | Between STS and ITS | Language processing |
| **Inferior temporal (ITG)** | Below ITS | Visual processing, object recognition |
| **Fusiform** | Basal surface | Face recognition (fusiform face area) |

### Parietal Lobe Gyri

| Gyrus | Location | Function |
|-------|----------|----------|
| **Postcentral** | Between CS and postcentral sulcus | Primary somatosensory cortex (S1) |
| **Superior parietal lobule (SPL)** | Above intraparietal sulcus | Visuospatial processing, hand-eye coordination |
| **Inferior parietal lobule (IPL)** | Below intraparietal sulcus | Supramarginal gyrus (caps Sylvian fissure end), angular gyrus (caps STS) |

## Medial Surface

| Structure | Key landmarks |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Cingulate gyrus** | Above corpus callosum, below cingulate sulcus |
| **Paracentral lobule** | Medial extension of pre+postcentral gyri — leg motor/sensory area |
| **Precuneus** | Between marginal sulcus and parieto-occipital sulcus |
| **Cuneus** | Between parieto-occipital and calcarine sulci |
| **Calcarine sulcus** | Primary visual cortex (V1) along its banks |

## Basal Surface

| Structure | Location | Clinical relevance |
|-----------|----------|-------------------|
| **Orbital gyri** | Frontal lobe base | Olfactory groove [[meningioma]] approaches |
| **Gyrus rectus** | Medial to olfactory sulcus | Sacrifice portion for frontal basal approaches |
| **Parahippocampal gyrus** | Medial temporal base | Contains uncus (hooks over tentorium) — [[herniation-syndromes\|uncal herniation]] |
| **Occipitotemporal/fusiform** | Between collateral and OT sulci | Resection zone for temporal lobe epilepsy |

## Craniometric Landmarks

| Craniometric point | Cortical correlation |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Bregma** | Near supplementary motor area |
| **Pterion** | Overlies anterior branch of MCA, Sylvian fissure |
| **Asterion** | Near transverse-sigmoid junction |
| **Stephanion** | Superior frontal sulcus-coronal suture junction |
| **Lambda** | Near superior margin of visual cortex |
