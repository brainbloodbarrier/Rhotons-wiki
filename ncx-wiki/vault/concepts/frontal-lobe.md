# Frontal Lobe

The frontal lobe is the largest cerebral lobe, bounded posteriorly by the central sulcus (Rolando), inferiorly by the Sylvian fissure, and medially by the [[interhemispheric-fissure]].

## Gyri and Sulci

### Lateral Surface

| Gyrus | Function |
|-------|----------|
| **Precentral gyrus** | Primary motor cortex (M1, BA 4) — somatotopic homunculus |
| **Superior frontal gyrus** | Supplementary motor area (SMA, posterior third), frontal eye field |
| **Middle frontal gyrus** | Dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (working memory, executive function) |
| **Inferior frontal gyrus** | Pars orbitalis, triangularis, opercularis (Broca's area — dominant hemisphere) |

### Medial Surface

- Superior frontal gyrus (medial portion) including SMA proper and pre-SMA
- Cingulate gyrus
- Paracentral lobule (medial motor cortex — leg representation)

### Basal Surface (orbital)

- Gyrus rectus (medial, bordered by olfactory sulcus)
- Orbital gyri (lateral, bordered by H-shaped orbital sulci)
- Olfactory tract in olfactory sulcus

## Key Functional Regions

- **Primary motor cortex (M1)**: contralateral somatotopy — medial leg, middle arm (dominant in precentral hand knob), lateral face/tongue
- **Premotor and SMA**: motor planning, bimanual coordination, proximal postural control
- **Broca's area** (dominant IFG pars opercularis/triangularis): motor speech, grammar
- **Frontal eye fields** (posterior MFG/SFG): contralateral saccadic gaze
- **Dorsolateral prefrontal cortex**: working memory, executive function
- **Orbitofrontal cortex**: decision-making, social cognition, emotional regulation

## Vascular Supply

| Region | Supply |
|--------|--------|
| Lateral (including M1, premotor, DLPFC, Broca) | MCA M3-M4 branches |
| Medial | [[anterior-cerebral-artery]] (A2-A5, callosomarginal) |
| Basal (orbital) | ACA (orbitofrontal) + MCA (lateral orbital) |

## Surgical Relevance

### Eloquent Cortex Mapping
Frontal lesions adjacent to M1, Broca, or SMA require:
- **Awake craniotomy** with cortical and subcortical language mapping (dominant IFG, arcuate fasciculus)
- **Motor mapping** (direct cortical / subcortical stimulation, MEP)
- **Preoperative fMRI + DTI** of corticospinal tract, arcuate fasciculus, frontal aslant tract

### Approaches
- **Pterional / frontotemporal**: sphenoid ridge + inferior frontal lobe retraction for anterior-circulation vascular targets
- **Bifrontal**: midline skull-base tumors (olfactory-groove meningioma, tuberculum sella)
- **[[anterior-interhemispheric-approach]]**: medial frontal lesions, pericallosal AVMs/aneurysms

### SMA Syndrome
Resection of dominant SMA proper produces transient (days to weeks) mutism + contralateral hemineglect / hemiparesis. Patient recovers via contralateral SMA / ipsilateral pre-SMA compensation. Key to counsel pre-operatively.
