# Foramen of Monro (Interventricular Foramen)

The foramen of Monro is the paired aperture through which each [[lateral-ventricle]] communicates with the third ventricle. It marks the junction of the anterior body of the lateral ventricle with the roof of the third ventricle.

## Boundaries

| Border | Structure |
|--------|-----------|
| **Anterior** | Column of the fornix |
| **Posterior** | Choroid plexus (tail), superior thalamostriate vein, anterior caudate vein |
| **Superior / medial** | Septum pellucidum insertion |
| **Lateral / inferior** | Body of thalamus |

The **choroidal fissure** begins at the foramen of Monro and extends posteriorly along the roof of the third ventricle and into the body → atrium → temporal horn of the lateral ventricle.

## Key Adjacent Veins

- **Superior thalamostriate vein** — runs in the thalamocaudate groove, entering internal cerebral vein immediately posterior to foramen of Monro (the "venous angle" of neuroradiology).
- **Septal vein** — runs on septum pellucidum, joins thalamostriate at the venous angle.
- **Anterior caudate vein** — drains head of caudate into thalamostriate.

## Surgical Relevance

### Approaches Traversing or Targeting the Foramen

| Approach | Route | Use case |
|----------|-------|----------|
| **Transcortical-transventricular** | Middle frontal gyrus → frontal horn | Colloid cyst, SEGA, intraventricular tumors |
| **Anterior transcallosal** | [[interhemispheric-fissure]] → callosotomy → body → foramen | Colloid cyst, third ventricle tumors |
| **Transforaminal** | Direct through enlarged foramen | Small lesions within 3rd ventricle anterior |
| **Transchoroidal** | Through tela choroidea, extending foramen posteriorly | Expanded access to 3rd ventricle |
| **Interforniceal** | Through septum pellucidum / between fornices | Posterior 3rd ventricle, pineal region |

### Colloid Cyst
Classic pathology. Arises at the roof of the 3rd ventricle, obstructs one or both foramina of Monro → acute obstructive hydrocephalus. Presentation: positional headache, sudden loss of consciousness, risk of sudden death. Treatment: endoscopic or microsurgical resection via transcortical / transcallosal corridor.

### Endoscopic Third Ventriculostomy (ETV)
Entry through the foramen of Monro to perform fenestration of the floor of the third ventricle (tuber cinereum) anterior to mammillary bodies and posterior to infundibular recess — bypasses aqueductal obstruction. Key landmark identification: both foramina on either side of the massa intermedia.

### Damage Considerations
- **Fornix column injury** → amnesia (especially bilateral or dominant)
- **Thalamostriate vein sacrifice** → thalamic venous infarct, decreased consciousness
- **Septal vein** can typically be sacrificed
