# Caudate Nucleus

The caudate nucleus is a C-shaped deep gray nucleus that parallels the curvature of the [[lateral-ventricle]]. It is a component of the basal ganglia (together with the putamen, globus pallidus, subthalamic nucleus, and substantia nigra) and the striatum (together with the putamen, anatomically separated by the anterior limb of the internal capsule but functionally unified).

## Anatomy

| Part | Location | Ventricular relation |
|------|----------|---------------------|
| **Head** | Frontal horn | Lateral floor + wall |
| **Body** | Body of lateral ventricle | Floor, lateral to thalamus |
| **Tail** | Temporal horn | Roof, adjacent to amygdala at its tip |

The **stria terminalis** runs along the thalamocaudate groove (between body of caudate and thalamus) from the amygdala tail to the septum / anterior commissure.

## Connections (simplified)

- **Input** (corticostriatal): frontal cortex (especially prefrontal, dorsolateral prefrontal, orbitofrontal) → caudate
- **Output**: caudate → globus pallidus internus / substantia nigra pars reticulata → thalamus → cortex
- **Functional role**: cognitive/associative basal ganglia loop (vs. putamen's motor loop)

## Vascular Supply

| Region | Supply |
|--------|--------|
| Head | Recurrent artery of Heubner (from [[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]] A1-A2 junction); medial lenticulostriate perforators from A1 |
| Body | Lateral lenticulostriates from MCA M1; anterior choroidal artery |
| Tail | Anterior choroidal artery |

Head-of-caudate infarct (classic Heubner territory): contralateral face-arm weakness, frontal-subcortical cognitive syndrome (abulia, perseveration).

## Surgical Relevance

### Transventricular Approaches
The caudate forms the floor / lateral wall of every lateral-ventricle approach. Key considerations:
- **Transcortical-frontal horn**: exposes caudate head as floor
- **Transcallosal-body**: exposes caudate body between [[foramen-of-monro]] and atrium
- **Temporal horn approaches**: caudate tail roofs the temporal horn

### Subependymal Giant Cell Astrocytoma (SEGA)
Arises from caudate head / septum region in tuberous sclerosis. Resection via transcortical or endoscopic route.

### Deep Brain Stimulation
Caudate (not putamen) is a target in investigational DBS for OCD and Tourette syndrome; body and ventral head.

### Hemorrhage
Caudate-head hemorrhages (from lenticulostriate microaneurysms) typically rupture into the lateral ventricle and present with intraventricular hemorrhage + acute hydrocephalus. May mimic SAH clinically.
