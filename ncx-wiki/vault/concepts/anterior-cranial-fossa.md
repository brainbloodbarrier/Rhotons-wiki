# Anterior Cranial Fossa (ACF)

The anterior cranial fossa is the rostral compartment of the cranial base, housing the orbital gyri of the [[frontal-lobe]]. It is bounded posteriorly by the posterior edge of the lesser sphenoid wing and the anterior clinoid processes.

## Bony Anatomy

| Region | Composition |
|--------|-------------|
| **Roof of orbits** | Orbital plates of the frontal bone |
| **Midline** | Cribriform plate + crista galli (ethmoid); frontal crest (frontal bone) |
| **Posterior midline** | Planum sphenoidale + tuberculum sellae + anterior clinoids (sphenoid) |
| **Posterior lateral** | Lesser wings of sphenoid; optic canals |
| **Anterior** | Frontal sinus posterior wall |

### Key Foramina and Landmarks

- **Cribriform plate**: olfactory fila (CN I) passing between olfactory bulb and nasal mucosa
- **Anterior ethmoidal foramen**: anterior ethmoidal artery (branch of ophthalmic), entering cranium from orbit
- **Posterior ethmoidal foramen**: posterior ethmoidal artery
- **Foramen cecum**: vestigial; emissary vein to nasal cavity (infection route)
- **Optic canal**: transmits CN II + ophthalmic artery (posterior-lateral ACF)

## Contents

- Orbital gyri of frontal lobes (above)
- Olfactory bulbs and tracts (along cribriform plate)
- Ethmoidal arteries and nerves (midline dural supply)

## Vascular Supply of the Dura

- Anterior and posterior ethmoidal arteries → anterior ACF dura
- Anterior meningeal branch of ophthalmic → midline ACF dura
- Middle meningeal artery branches → lateral posterior ACF dura

## Surgical Relevance

### Pathologies

| Lesion | Location |
|--------|----------|
| Olfactory-groove meningioma | Cribriform plate region |
| Tuberculum sellae meningioma | Planum sphenoidale / tuberculum |
| Esthesioneuroblastoma (olfactory neuroblastoma) | Cribriform, with transnasal extension |
| Frontal-base epidermoid / dermoid | Inferior frontal lobe |
| Frontoethmoidal encephalocele | Cribriform plate defect |
| Anterior communicating aneurysm | At ACF floor / posterior edge (accessed via subfrontal or pterional) |

### Approaches

- **Subfrontal (unilateral or bilateral frontal craniotomy)**: midline anterior fossa pathology (meningioma, encephalocele, anterior CSF leak repair)
- **Bifrontal craniotomy ± orbital osteotomy**: large midline or sinonasal-extending lesions
- **Pterional (subfrontal)**: lateral and posterior ACF — optic chiasm, ACoA, tuberculum
- **Endoscopic endonasal transcribriform/transplanum**: ventral approach, avoids brain retraction; requires multilayer skull-base reconstruction
- **Supraorbital keyhole (eyebrow)**: minimally invasive subfrontal for small tuberculum/planum lesions

### Risks and Reconstruction
- **CSF rhinorrhea**: cribriform or planum defect; multilayer repair (pericranial flap, fascia lata, fat, abdominal fat, nasoseptal flap if endoscopic)
- **Olfaction**: cribriform mobilization risks anosmia (counsel pre-op; bilateral sacrifice permanent)
- **Frontal sinus management**: cranialization vs obliteration when posterior table breached
