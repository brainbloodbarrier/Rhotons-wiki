# Lateral Ventricle

The lateral ventricle is the paired C-shaped cerebrospinal-fluid (CSF) cavity within each cerebral hemisphere. Each communicates with the third ventricle through the foramen of Monro.

## Compartments

| Compartment | Location | Key walls |
|-------------|----------|-----------|
| **Frontal (anterior) horn** | Frontal lobe | Roof: corpus callosum; floor/lateral: caudate head; medial: septum pellucidum |
| **Body** | Parietal region | Roof: corpus callosum (body); floor: thalamus, caudate body; medial: septum |
| **Atrium (trigone)** | Parieto-occipital junction | Contains glomus of choroid plexus; lateral wall: tapetum (optic radiation) |
| **Occipital horn** | Occipital lobe | Walls: calcar avis (below), bulb of corpus callosum (medial), tapetum (lateral) |
| **Temporal horn** | Temporal lobe | Roof: tail of caudate, stria terminalis; floor: hippocampus, collateral eminence |

## CSF Flow

Lateral ventricle → foramen of Monro → third ventricle → cerebral aqueduct → fourth ventricle → foramina of Luschka + Magendie → subarachnoid space → arachnoid granulations → superior sagittal sinus.

## Choroid Plexus

Produces CSF. Lies along the choroidal fissure from foramen of Monro → body → atrium → temporal horn. Blood supply:

- **Anterior choroidal artery** (from supraclinoid ICA) — supplies temporal horn plexus
- **Posterior choroidal arteries** (from PCA) — medial and lateral; supply atrium and body plexus

## Surgical Relevance

### Approaches to Lateral Ventricle

| Corridor | Entry point | Target |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| **Transcortical (middle frontal gyrus)** | Kocher's point | Frontal horn, foramen of Monro |
| **Transcallosal anterior** | Coronal suture region via [[interhemispheric-fissure]] | Body, foramen of Monro, 3rd ventricle |
| **Transcallosal posterior (interforniceal / subchoroidal)** | Atrium via parietal callosotomy | Pineal region, 3rd ventricle posterior |
| **Transtemporal** | Middle / inferior temporal gyrus | Temporal horn, hippocampus |
| **Occipital transventricular** | Superior parietal lobule | Atrium, occipital horn |

Kocher's point: 1 cm anterior to coronal suture, 2.5 cm lateral to midline — standard ventricular drain and anterior horn access.

### Intraventricular Pathology

- **Colloid cyst** of the foramen of Monro → obstructive hydrocephalus, sudden death
- **Central neurocytoma** — anterior body / septum, most common intraventricular tumor in adults
- **Choroid plexus papilloma/carcinoma** — atrium (adults) or temporal horn (children)
- **Subependymal giant cell astrocytoma (SEGA)** — tuberous sclerosis, near foramen of Monro
- **Meningioma** — atrial location most common

### External Ventricular Drain (EVD)
Target: frontal horn, ipsilateral to pathology or dominant-hemisphere side if none. Insertion at Kocher's point, aimed at the contralateral medial canthus in coronal plane and tragus in sagittal plane.
