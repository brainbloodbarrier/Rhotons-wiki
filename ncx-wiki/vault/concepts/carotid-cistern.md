# Carotid Cistern

The carotid cistern is a component of the [[basal-cisterns|basal cistern system]], surrounding the supraclinoid internal carotid artery (ICA) from the dural ring to the ICA bifurcation.

## Boundaries

| Wall | Structure |
|------|-----------|
| Medial | Optic nerve, chiasmatic cistern |
| Lateral | Uncus, medial temporal lobe |
| Superior | Anterior perforated substance, A1 origin |
| Inferior | Oculomotor triangle / roof of cavernous sinus |
| Anterior | Distal dural ring, clinoid segment of ICA |
| Posterior | Interpeduncular and crural cisterns |

## Contents

- Supraclinoid ICA (C6–C7 of Bouthillier)
- ICA branches in sequence:
  - Ophthalmic artery (just above dural ring, medial)
  - Superior hypophyseal artery (medial, perforators to pituitary stalk and chiasm)
  - Posterior communicating artery (PCoA) origin (posterolateral)
  - Anterior choroidal artery origin (just distal to PCoA, posterior)
  - ICA bifurcation → A1 + M1
- Oculomotor nerve (CN III) in adjacent cistern, entering oculomotor triangle
- Arachnoid trabeculae and Lilliequist's membrane (posteriorly, separating from interpeduncular)

## Surgical Relevance

### Pterional / Transsylvian Access
Sequential cistern opening during pterional craniotomy follows:
1. **Sylvian cistern** — arachnoid split along the Sylvian fissure, release superficial Sylvian vein
2. **Carotid cistern** — incise arachnoid lateral to ICA, release CSF
3. **Chiasmatic / optico-carotid cistern** — expose optic nerve medially
4. **Lamina terminalis cistern** — access 3rd-ventricle anteriorly
5. **Interpeduncular** — via optico-carotid window, for basilar apex

Opening the carotid cistern is the **single step that most improves brain relaxation** in anterior-circulation aneurysm surgery.

### Relevant Aneurysms
- ICA bifurcation
- PCoA
- AChA
- Ophthalmic segment
- [[acoa-aneurysm|ACoA]] (via lamina terminalis, but carotid cistern must be opened first)

### Anatomical Windows
- **Opticocarotid window**: between optic nerve and ICA — access to basilar apex from lateral view
- **Carotid-oculomotor window**: between ICA and CN III — alternate basilar view
- **Supracarotid triangle**: bounded by ICA laterally, A1 inferiorly, optic tract superiorly — access to anterior perforated substance
