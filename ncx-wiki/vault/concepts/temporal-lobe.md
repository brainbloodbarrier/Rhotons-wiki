# Temporal Lobe

The temporal lobe is the lateral cerebral lobe inferior to the Sylvian fissure and anterior to the occipital lobe. It is divided into lateral, basal, and mesial surfaces, each with distinct functional and surgical significance.

## Surfaces

| Surface | Key structures |
|---------|---------------|
| **Lateral** | Superior (STG), middle (MTG), inferior (ITG) temporal gyri; superior and inferior temporal sulci |
| **Basal** | Fusiform gyrus, inferior temporal gyrus, parahippocampal gyrus |
| **Mesial** | Uncus, amygdala, hippocampus, parahippocampal gyrus, collateral sulcus |

## Key Functional Regions

- **Primary auditory cortex (Heschl's gyrus)** — posterior STG, transverse
- **Wernicke area** — posterior STG (dominant hemisphere, BA 22 posterior)
- **Fusiform face area** — lateral fusiform gyrus
- **Hippocampal formation** — declarative/episodic memory consolidation
- **Amygdala** — emotional learning, threat evaluation
- **Meyer's loop** (optic radiation) — swings anteriorly into the temporal stem before reaching V1; lesioning causes contralateral superior quadrantanopia ("pie in the sky")

## Vascular Supply

- **Lateral surface**: MCA M3-M4 branches (anterior temporal, middle temporal, posterior temporal, temporo-occipital)
- **Basal surface**: PCA P4 branches (hippocampal, anterior temporal, middle/posterior temporal from PCA)
- **Mesial (uncus, hippocampus)**: anterior choroidal artery + PCA perforators (P2)

## Surgical Relevance

### Temporal Lobectomy (Epilepsy)
Standard anterior temporal lobectomy for medically refractory temporal lobe epilepsy:
- **Lateral resection**: 4.5 cm from temporal tip on dominant side, 5.5 cm on non-dominant, sparing STG posterior to vein of Labbé drainage.
- **Mesial resection**: selective amygdalohippocampectomy via transcortical (middle T gyrus) or trans-Sylvian corridor.
- **Meyer's loop**: anterior extent of temporal-horn opening limits visual-field deficit.

### Tumor Surgery
Gliomas in temporal lobe — awake craniotomy with language mapping on dominant side. Key eloquent structures: Wernicke (posterior STG), inferior longitudinal fasciculus, uncinate fasciculus (ventral language stream).

### Vascular
Temporal lobe is traversed during trans-Sylvian, sub-temporal, and petrosal approaches to reach the basilar apex, cavernous sinus, and upper clivus.
