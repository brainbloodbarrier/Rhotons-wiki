# Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS)

Neuromodulatory technique using implanted electrodes to deliver continuous electrical stimulation to specific deep brain targets. Revolutionized treatment of movement disorders, with expanding indications in epilepsy, psychiatric disorders, and pain.

## DBS Targets and Indications

| Target | Abbreviation | Primary Indication | Secondary Indications |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **Ventral intermediate nucleus** | VIM | Essential tremor | Cerebellar outflow tremors, MS tremor |
| **Subthalamic nucleus** | STN | Parkinson's disease | — |
| **Globus pallidus internus** | GPi | Parkinson's disease | Dystonia (primary generalized) |
| **Anterior thalamic nuclei** | ANT | Epilepsy (medically refractory) | — |
| **Ventral capsule/ventral striatum** | VC/VS | OCD | Depression |
| **Subcallosal cingulate** | SCC | Treatment-resistant depression | — |
| **Periventricular/periaqueductal gray** | PVG/PAG | Medically intractable pain | — |

## STN vs GPi for Parkinson's Disease

| Feature | STN | GPi |
|---------|-----|-----|
| **Medication reduction** | Significant (~50% levodopa reduction) | Minimal |
| **Motor improvement** | Comparable | Comparable |
| **Dyskinesia** | Improved via medication reduction | Direct suppression |
| **Cognitive/mood effects** | Higher risk depression, impulsivity | Fewer cognitive effects |
| **Battery life** | Longer (lower energy needed) | Shorter (higher energy) |
| **Target size** | Smaller (~8mm) — more demanding | Larger — more forgiving |
| **Preferred for** | Younger patients, high medication burden | Cognitive concerns, dyskinesia-dominant |

## Surgical Technique (Frame-Based STN DBS)

### Preoperative Planning

- High-resolution MRI: T1, T2, FLAIR, QSM (quantitative susceptibility mapping)
- Direct targeting on MRI (visualize STN on T2/QSM)
- Indirect targeting from AC-PC coordinates: STN at 12mm lateral, 2mm posterior, 4mm inferior to MCP
- Frame placement: Leksell or CRW stereotactic frame

### Microelectrode Recording (MER)

Start 15 mm above anatomical target. Structures encountered in order:

| Structure | MER Signature |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Thalamus** | Bursting units (15±19 Hz) + irregular tonic firing (~28 Hz) |
| **Zona incerta/Fields of Forel** | Decreased background; low firing rates |
| **STN** | **Dramatic increase in background activity**; 34-47 Hz firing; kinesthetic-responsive units in dorsolateral motor territory |
| **Substantia nigra pars reticulata** | Higher firing (50-70 Hz); tonic/regular pattern; few kinesthetic responses |

**Adequate STN target:** ≥5 mm of STN span with kinesthetic-responsive units in dorsolateral region.

### Electrode Implantation

- **Electrodes:** 4 contacts (1.5mm height, 1.27mm diameter)
  - Model 3387: 1.5mm spacing (10.5mm span)
  - Model 3389: 0.5mm spacing (7.5mm span)
- Contact 0 at ventral STN border; remaining contacts span dorsally
- **Macrostimulation test:** 1-5V, 90µs PW, 130Hz
  - Therapeutic benefit with wide side-effect threshold = good position
  - Low-threshold motor phenomena → too lateral
  - Persistent paresthesias → too posterior
  - Ocular deviation/mydriasis → too medial

### IPG (Neurostimulator) Implantation

- Second stage under general anesthesia (1 week after leads)
- Infraclavicular pocket: 2cm below clavicle, 4cm from midline
- Tunneled extension lead connects DBS lead to IPG
- Available: single-channel (Activa SC), dual-channel (Activa PC), rechargeable (Activa RC)

## Programming

- Initial programming: 4 weeks post-implant (allow edema to subside)
- PD patients: program in off-medication state first
- Start: single monopolar stimulation, 1.0V, dorsal STN contact
- Typical therapeutic parameters: 1.5-4V, 60-120µs PW, up to 185Hz
- Gradually increase stimulation as medication is titrated down

## DBS Platform Technologies

| Approach | Method | Advantage |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **Frame-based** | Leksell/CRW stereotactic frame | Gold standard accuracy |
| **Frameless (O-arm)** | Intraoperative CT registration | No frame discomfort; real-time imaging |
| **iMRI-guided** | Real-time MRI verification | Direct visualization of lead in target |
| **3D-printed platforms** | Patient-specific stereotactic guides | Reduced setup time; no frame |
| **CT-guided (awake)** | Intraoperative CT targeting | Versatile; frameless option |

## Complications

| Complication | Incidence | Management |
|-------------|-----------|------------|
| Hemorrhage | 1-3% | Intraoperative CT; evacuate if symptomatic |
| Infection | 3-8% | Hardware removal if deep; antibiotics |
| Hardware malfunction | 5% over 5 years | Lead revision |
| Lead migration | Rare | Reimplantation |
| Pneumocephalus | Common; usually benign | Air resorbs; may affect MER accuracy |
| Stimulation side effects | Dose-dependent | Reprogramming; lead repositioning |

## Expanding Indications

- **Epilepsy:** ANT-DBS (SANTE trial); responsive neurostimulation (RNS)
- **OCD:** VC/VS stimulation
- **Depression:** SCC (Cg25) and VC/VS
- **Tourette syndrome:** GPi or centromedian-parafascicular complex
- **Cluster headache:** Posterior hypothalamus
- **Anorexia nervosa:** SCC
- **Alzheimer's:** Fornix/nucleus basalis of Meynert (investigational)
