# Ventricular Shunt Reference

The layout of this chapter is broken down by the manufacturer of the shunt, listed in alphabetical order. Images of each shunt valve on standard radiography are provided. Images showing the various shunt settings are also available for reference.

## **Delta Valve**

![Cross-sectional diagram of a Delta Valve with labels for its various components.](49ee89a1d5852ab005dbbab6de09a8a6_img.jpg)

The diagram shows a cross-section of a Delta Valve with the following labeled components:

- Silicone dome:** The top, bulbous part of the valve.
- Reservoir:** The internal chamber within the silicone dome.
- Proximal occluder:** Located at the inlet side.
- Integral inlet connector:** The connection point at the proximal end.
- Radiopaque marker:** A marker for visibility on X-rays.
- Firm polypropylene plastic base:** The structural base of the valve.
- Radiopaque performance/flow direction indicators:** Indicators for flow direction and performance.
- Distal occluder:** Located at the outlet side.
- Delta chamber:** The internal chamber near the distal end.
- Silicone membrane valve:** The valve mechanism itself.
- Integral outlet connector:** The connection point at the distal end.

Cross-sectional diagram of a Delta Valve with labels for its various components.
