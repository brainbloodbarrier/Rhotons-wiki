# Infratentorial Operative Anatomy

For additional illustrations of surgical anatomy, please refer to the Jackler Atlas by clicking on the image below:

This anatomical illustration depicts the middle ear and facial nerve (FN) anatomy. Key structures labeled include: Horizontal SCC (Semicircular Canal), Labyrinthine Segment (of the FN), Geniculate Ganglion, Greater Superficial Petrosal Nerve, Tympanic FN, Tensor Tympani Muscle, Posterior SCC, 2nd Genu, Stapes, Cochleariform Process, Eustachian Tube, Mastoid FN, Round Window, Tympanic Plexus, Chorda Tympani, and Stylomastoid Foramen. The illustration is a detailed cross-section showing the relationship between the bony structures and the soft tissue components of the ear.
