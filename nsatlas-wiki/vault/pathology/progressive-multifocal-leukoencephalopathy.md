# Progressive Multifocal Leukoencephalopathy (PML)

*Source: NSAtlas, Cohen-Gadol (contributor: Sean Dodson, MD)*

**JC polyomavirus** reactivation in the immunosuppressed brain. Virus infects **oligodendrocytes** → progressive white-matter demyelination. Not primarily a neurosurgical disease — but neurosurgeons see it as **biopsy referrals** mimicking infiltrative glioma.

## Pathogenesis

- **Hematogenous JC virus spread** or reactivation of latent CNS infection
- Target: **oligodendrocytes** (myelin-producing)
- Demyelinating, not inflammatory — minimal mass effect
- Classically in **AIDS** (CD4 < 200 typical, often < 100)
- **Transplant recipients** on immunosuppression
- **Biologic agents**: **natalizumab** (anti-α4 integrin for MS/Crohn's) is the paradigm; also rituximab, fingolimod, efalizumab historically
- Chemotherapy-induced immunosuppression

## Clinical Features

- **Subacute, progressive neurologic decline**: altered mental status, focal deficits by white-matter territory involved, headache, lethargy
- **No fever, no leukocytosis** (not meningitis-like)
- **30–50% early mortality** in the first few months
- Prognosis improves with immune reconstitution (HAART in AIDS, withdraw natalizumab + plasmapheresis in MS-PML)

## Imaging

### General

- **Asymmetric, multifocal demyelinating plaques** in **subcortical + deep white matter**
- **Juxtacortical** (hugging U-fibers) **without cortical** involvement — important DDx clue vs glioma
- **Mass effect is unusual**
- **Enhancement is atypical** — when present, may indicate **immune reconstitution inflammatory syndrome (IRIS)** and is paradoxically associated with **improved survival**

### CT

- Asymmetric focal low-attenuation periventricular + subcortical white matter

### MRI — the workhorse

- **T1WI**: hypointense (**striking** T1 hypointensity is a useful glioma-distinguishing feature)
- **T2WI / FLAIR**: hyperintense
- **DWI**: peripheral reduced diffusion in acute/advancing edge (leading edge of demyelination)
- **Post-contrast T1**: **no enhancement**, or faint peripheral enhancement; any new enhancement → IRIS
- **MRS**: **↓ NAA, ↑ lactate, ↑ choline, ↑ lipids** — overlaps with glioma pattern

### Imaging recommendations

- **Standard MRI + DWI + contrast**
- **Serial imaging** — expansion without mass effect is characteristic
- **CSF JCV PCR** — preferred noninvasive confirmation

## Differential

- **Infiltrative glioma** — the hardest mimic. Favor glioma with: mass effect, cortical involvement, avid enhancement, mono-regional. Favor PML with: striking T1 hypointensity, juxtacortical without cortical, multifocal asymmetric, absent enhancement, known immunosuppression
- **HIV encephalopathy** — symmetric, periventricular, deep gray involvement
- **Acute demyelination (ADEM, MS)** — enhancing ring/open-ring; different clinical tempo
- **CMV encephalitis** — periventricular, ependymal enhancement
- **Chronic small-vessel ischemia** — symmetric, different distribution

## Biopsy Considerations

- **CSF JCV PCR** is usually tried first; sensitivity 70–90%
- **Stereotactic biopsy** when PCR is negative and diagnosis remains uncertain — targets the advancing edge (DWI-bright rim), **not the T1-hypointense burned-out center**
- Histology: JCV inclusions in bizarre oligodendrocytes; reactive gemistocytic astrocytes can mimic glioma
- **Immunohistochemistry for SV40 / JCV antigens** confirms

## Management

- **Restore immunity** — the only meaningful intervention
  - HAART in AIDS
  - Withdraw natalizumab + plasmapheresis / immunoadsorption in MS
  - Taper immunosuppression in transplant when possible
- **Pembrolizumab** has shown case-series benefit (off-label, experimental)
- **No proven antiviral** — mefloquine, cytarabine, mirtazapine tried without convincing efficacy
- **Supportive care**, seizure management, rehabilitation

## Pearls and Pitfalls

- **In any rapidly evolving non-enhancing T1-hypointense white-matter lesion**, ask about HIV, transplant, natalizumab — PML until proven otherwise
- **Juxtacortical without cortical involvement** distinguishes PML from glioma
- **Mass effect absence** in a growing lesion argues against glioma
- **New enhancement during therapy** = IRIS, not progression — paradoxically associated with survival
- **Biopsy the advancing DWI-bright rim**, not the T1-dark core
- **CSF JCV PCR first** before stereotactic biopsy
- **No surgical cure** — immune reconstitution is the treatment
