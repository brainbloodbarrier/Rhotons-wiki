# Instrumentation

*Principles of Cranial Surgery — Instrumentation*
*DOI: 10.18791/nsatlas.v2.05*

**Source:** NSAtlas, Cohen-Gadol

> *"Simplicity is the key to technical adaptability and proficiency."*

## The "Special Set" Philosophy

- An **abbreviated set** of multifunctional instruments
- Each instrument **exploited** for multiple maneuvers
- The **way an instrument is used** defines its success — not the instrument itself
- As experience grows, the set shrinks (see also [[subarachnoid-dissection]])
- Virtuosity = movements that meld together without hesitation, like a concerto

## Head Fixation

| Device | Use |
|---|---|
| **Doughnut headrest** | Non-rigid cases: hematoma evacuation, emergencies |
| **Horseshoe headrest** | Non-rigid cases |
| **Mayfield skull clamp** (3-pin, Integra) | Most microsurgical and stereotactic cases — workhorse |
| **Sugita frame** (4-pin) | Alternative rigid fixation with self-retaining retractor system |
| **Budde Halo Retractor System** | Couples to skull clamp; platform for retractor blades, also armrest/handrest |

See [[skull-clamp-placement]] and [[patient-positioning]].

## Operating Room Microscope

See [[microscope-and-mouthpiece]].

- **Illumination, magnification, stereoscopic imaging** expand the surgeon's vision
- Enables retractorless microsurgery (see [[brain-mobilization-and-protection]])
- **Do NOT macrosurgery under the microscope**
- **Magnification must match the operative stage** — too high = disorientation and fatigue, too low = missed vessels or residual tumor
- **Mouth switch** — eliminates hand-based focus/zoom, dramatically advances efficiency
- "With enough experience the microscope becomes virtually part of the surgeon's face"

## Cranial and Dural Opening Instruments

### Scalp / soft tissue

- **10-blade scalpel** — skin
- **Bipolar electrocautery** — hemostasis
- **Raney clips** — scalp-edge bleeding control
- **Frazier suction** — multiple sizes, finger-controlled level
- **Periosteal elevator** — reflects muscle/galea off bone
- **Weitlaner retractors** — flat-surface scalp retraction
- **Adson cerebellar retractors** — curved scalp (midline suboccipital)
- **Fishhooks** — pterional and anterior skull base — precise force along subfrontal corridor

### Burr holes — Cohen-Gadol's bit choice

- **Perforator clutch drill bit** (14 mm, auto-stop on inner table) — standard
- **Acorn-shaped bit** (preferred) — burr hole size more controllable; **perforator's auto-stop not always reliable, especially over venous sinuses** → acorn used routinely

### Bone work

- **#1 Penfield dissector / bone curette** — removes inner-table fragments after burr hole
- **#3 Penfield dissector** — thorough dural stripping from inner table; separates adherent dura at bone flap center
- **High-speed side-cutting drill + footplate** — craniotomy cuts ([[burr-holes-and-bone-flaps]])
- **Leksell rongeurs** — lateral sphenoid wing removal; coarser bone
- **Kerrison rongeurs** — burr hole expansion; shelling bone over venous dural sinuses (retromastoid)
- **Straight drill bit** — dural tackup suture holes

### Dural opening

- **15-blade scalpel** — initial durotomy
- **Metzenbaum scissors** — extend the durotomy

## Microsurgical Instruments

- **Arachnoid knife** (sharp and round) — opening arachnoid membranes
- **Microscissors** — sharp dissection
- **Rhoton microdissectors** — wide range of tip shapes and angles for variable operative angles and small spaces
- **Bayonetted bipolar forceps** — variable tip sizes; functions as forceps, scissors, dissector, coagulator
- **Sharp dissection protects against blunt injury** to cerebrovascular structures
- **10-cc syringe with bulb** — irrigation (clears field, hydrates tissue, prevents microscope heat injury)

See [[subarachnoid-dissection]] for the "special set" of microsurgical instruments.

## Tumor Debulking / Lesion Removal

- **Tumor forceps, pituitary rongeurs** — mobilization and debulking
- **Ultrasonic tissue aspirator (CUSA)** — fibrous skull base tumors near critical structures; aggressive internal debulking without traction injury to CNs/brainstem
- **Suction tips** — dual function as dissector and dynamic retractor (airflow modulation critical — see [[brain-mobilization-and-protection]])
- **Moist cotton patty strips** — brain protection, hemostasis, tamponade, maintaining tumor borders, perforator tamponade (see [[intraoperative-hemostasis]])

## Cranial Closing

- **Pericranium** — duraplasty when native dura insufficient (harvested at start of operation)
- **Synthetic dura substitutes / pericardium** — **Cohen-Gadol avoids** due to aseptic inflammation and infection risk
- **Plating system**: burr hole covers + plates + **4-mm screws**
- **Cranioplasty** — reconstruction to avoid cosmetic defects
- **Galea**: 3-0 Vicryl
- **Skin**: staples or nylon (Cohen-Gadol's preference is 3-0 Monocryl — see [[scalp-incisions]] and [[suturing-and-closure]])

## Pearls and Pitfalls

- **Master the capabilities AND limitations** of each instrument
- **Abbreviated standardized set** → operative efficiency + minimized OR staff confusion
- **Acorn bit > perforator** for controlled burr holes, especially over sinuses
- **Mouth switch** is an efficiency multiplier
- **Avoid synthetic dura substitutes** — pericranium preferred
- **Ultrasonic aspirator** for fibrous skull base tumors near critical structures
