# Ventricular Shunt Reference

*Principles of Cranial Surgery — Ventricular Shunt Reference*
*Source:* NSAtlas, Cohen-Gadol

This chapter is a **reference catalog** of commercially available ventricular shunt valves, organized by manufacturer. For each valve: radiographic appearance, pressure setting range, and unique features.

## Aesculap

### proGAV Valve

- **Adjustable range:** 0-20 cm H₂O
- **Non-adjustable gap:** between 0 and 3 cm H₂O
- **Settings numerically correspond to opening pressure**
- Radiograph: central rotating disc aligned with setting mark
- "Flat side of valve" marks the disc indicator

### proGAV 2.0 Valve

- **Adjustable range:** 0-20 cm H₂O (in cm H₂O)
- Proximal indicator at top, distal at bottom
- **Inlet indicator, orientation indicator, triangle tip** for X-ray setting identification
- **Rotor coding with magnets + virtual triangle** overlay for programmer interpretation
- **Settings numerically correspond to opening pressure**

### proSA Gravitational Unit

- **Range:** 0-40 cm H₂O
- **Gravitational** = opens to different pressure depending on patient position (upright vs supine)
- **Example settings:** 35 cm H₂O, 31 cm H₂O, 40 cm H₂O
- **Non-adjustable range between 40 and 0**
- **Settings correspond numerically to opening pressure when NOT combined with a differential pressure unit in the shunt system**

## Codman

### Certas Plus Valve

- **8 settings including "Virtual Off"**
- **MRI-resistant up to 3 Tesla**
- Integrated reservoir
- **Optional integrated SIPHONGUARD® anti-siphon device**
- **Optional unitized BACTISEAL® antimicrobial catheter**
- Ruby bushing (precision rotor bearing)

### Hakim Programmable Valve

- **8 discrete settings** with distinct internal configuration
- Right-hand-side X-ray marker + setting indicator on radiograph

| Valve Setting | Opening Pressure (cm H₂O) |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2.0-2.5 |
| 2 | 4.4-5.5 |
| 3 | 7.5-8.0 |
| 4 | 11-12 |
| 5 | 14-14.5 |
| 6 | 17-18 |
| 7 | 21-22.5 |
| 8 | >40 |

### Codman Hakim Precision (fixed)

- **30-200 mm H₂O** in 10 mm increments
- Settings numerically correspond to opening pressure

## Medtronic

### PS Medical CSF-Flow Control Valve

- **Four discrete settings** marked by chevrons + dots
- Silicone dome reservoir with radiopaque pressure dots
- Radiopaque flow direction indicator
- Occluders at inlet/outlet
- Polypropylene base

| Valve Setting | Opening Pressure (cm H₂O) |
|---|---|
| Low-Low (1 dot) | 1 |
| Low (2 dots) | 3 |
| Medium (3 dots) | 8.5 |
| High (4 dots) | 14.5 |

### Delta Valve

- Silicone dome with reservoir
- **Proximal and distal occluders**
- **Integral inlet connector**
- Firm polypropylene base
- Radiopaque marker

### Strata Programmable Valve (Medtronic)

- Magnetically programmable
- **5 settings** covering low through high opening pressure
- Radiopaque indicator visible on X-ray

## Reading a Shunt Valve on X-ray — General

1. **Identify the manufacturer** (shape, chevron dots, rotor design)
2. Use manufacturer's X-ray atlas (Hakim = 8 settings, proGAV = 0-20 dial, Certas Plus = 1-8 with Virtual Off)
3. Confirm orientation (proximal vs distal indicator)
4. Note any anti-siphon or gravitational unit — these affect pressure interpretation

## Anti-Siphon Technology

- **SIPHONGUARD® (Codman)** — passive anti-siphon chamber
- **Delta valve (Medtronic)** — built-in siphon control
- **proSA (Aesculap)** — gravitational unit adjusts opening pressure based on posture
- **Purpose:** prevent overdrainage in upright posture

## MRI Safety

- **Certas Plus**: 3T compatible
- Most modern programmable valves are **MRI-conditional** — but settings can shift after MRI exposure
- **Confirm valve setting on X-ray after any MRI**
- Radiograph protocols required before discharging MRI patient with programmable valve

## Clinical Use

Choose valve based on:

- **Patient age and expected longevity** — programmable for children and long-term
- **Body habitus + mobility** — gravitational units for ambulatory patients
- **MRI frequency** — MRI-resistant valves for tumor surveillance
- **Infection risk** — BACTISEAL® antimicrobial catheter option
- **Overdrainage risk** — anti-siphon-equipped valves
- See [[external-ventricular-drain]] for acute drainage before shunting

## Pearls and Pitfalls

- **Know the valve before imaging** — every ER doctor asks the neurosurgeon to identify a valve on X-ray
- **Settings may change after MRI** — always verify with X-ray
- **Gravitational valves** require understanding that opening pressure depends on body position
- **Virtual Off (Certas Plus)** is not truly "off" — minimal flow still possible
- **Combined differential + anti-siphon** — numerical valve setting may NOT equal effective opening pressure
