# Posterior Cranial Fossa

The posterior cranial fossa is the largest and deepest of the three cranial fossae, containing the most complex intracranial anatomy. In approximately one-eighth the intracranial space, it houses the pathways regulating consciousness, vital autonomic functions, motor and sensory systems, and the centers for balance and gait. Ten of the 12 cranial nerve pairs have segments within it.

## Boundaries

- **Superior** — [[tentorium-cerebelli]] (communicates with supratentorial space via [[tentorial-incisura]])
- **Inferior** — foramen magnum (communicates with spinal canal)
- **Anterior** — dorsum sellae, posterior [[sphenoid-bone|sphenoid]] body, [[clivus]]
- **Posterior** — squamosal occipital bone
- **Lateral** — petrous and mastoid [[temporal-bone]], lateral occipital bone, mastoid angle of parietal bone

Foramina: [[jugular-foramen]], [[internal-auditory-canal|internal acoustic meatus]], hypoglossal canal, vestibular and cochlear aqueducts.

## Contents

### Neural
- **[[cerebellum]]** — three surfaces (tentorial, petrosal, suboccipital), two fissures (cerebellomesencephalic, cerebellomedullary)
- **Brainstem** — midbrain, pons, medulla oblongata
- **[[fourth-ventricle]]** — tent-shaped midline cavity between cerebellum and brainstem
- **Cranial nerves** — [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] through XII all have posterior fossa segments

### Arterial
- **Vertebral arteries** — enter through foramen magnum
- **Basilar artery** — formed by vertebral junction on ventral pons
- **Superior cerebellar artery (SCA)** — from basilar; supplies tentorial cerebellar surface
- **Anterior inferior cerebellar artery (AICA)** — from basilar; supplies petrosal surface, [[facial-nerve|CN VII]]/VIII region
- **Posterior inferior cerebellar artery (PICA)** — from vertebral; supplies suboccipital surface, tonsils

### Venous
- Posterior fossa veins drain into transverse, sigmoid, superior/inferior petrosal sinuses, and [[vein-of-galen]] system

## Fourth Ventricle

A broad, tent-shaped midline cavity:
- **Connections** — rostrally via aqueduct to third ventricle; caudally via foramen of Magendie to cisterna magna; laterally via foramina of Luschka to cerebellopontine angles
- **Roof** — upper half: superior medullary velum + lingula; lower half: nodule (midline) + inferior medullary velum (laterally) + tela choroidea
- **Floor** — dorsal surface of pons and medulla (rhomboid fossa); most cranial nerves arise near here
- **Lateral recesses** — extend laterally around brainstem to the foramina of Luschka

### Cerebellar-Brainstem Fissures
Two critical fissures provide natural surgical corridors to the fourth ventricle:
- **Cerebellomesencephalic fissure** — between cerebellum and midbrain; SCA courses within it; approach to upper fourth ventricle ([[telovelar-approach]] from above)
- **[[cerebellomedullary-fissure]]** — between cerebellum and medulla; PICA courses within it; provides the **telovelar approach** to the fourth ventricle floor without splitting the vermis

## Surgical Approaches

| Approach | Target | Key Relations |
|---|---|---|
| Suboccipital midline | Fourth ventricle, vermian tumors | PICA, cerebellar tonsils |
| Retrosigmoid | Cerebellopontine angle, [[trigeminal-nerve|CN V]]-XI | AICA, CN VII/VIII |
| Telovelar | Fourth ventricle floor | Cerebellomedullary fissure, PICA, tela choroidea |
| Far lateral | Foramen magnum, lower clivus | Vertebral artery, CN IX-XII |
| Presigmoid | Petroclival region | Superior petrosal sinus, CN V |

## Relations

- Separated from supratentorial ([[cerebrum]]) space by tentorium cerebelli
- Contains [[brainstem]] (midbrain, pons, medulla), cerebellum, CN III-XII
- Arterial supply: [[vertebral-artery]] → [[basilar-artery]] system ([[superior-cerebellar-artery]], [[aica]], [[pica]])
- Venous drainage: [[posterior-fossa-veins]] → [[dural-venous-sinuses]] (petrosal, transverse, sigmoid sinuses)
- Fourth ventricle communicates with [[lateral-and-third-ventricles]] via aqueduct
- [[tentorial-incisura]] is the gateway between supra- and infratentorial compartments
- [[cerebellopontine-cistern]] is a major surgical target within the posterior fossa
- [[foramen-magnum]] is the inferior boundary (transmits [[vertebral-artery]], [[lower-cranial-nerves|CN XI]])
- Approaches: [[retrosigmoid-approach]], [[far-lateral-approach]], [[subtemporal-approach]], [[supracerebellar-infratentorial-approach]], suboccipital midline
- See [[surgical-approaches-overview]] for approach selection by target
