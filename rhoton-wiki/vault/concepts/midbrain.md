# Midbrain (Mesencephalon)

The midbrain (mesencephalon) is the short superior segment of the [[brainstem]] connecting the pons below to the diencephalon above. In the supratentorial–infratentorial axis it sits at the level of the [[tentorial-incisura]], making it the central structure of any approach to the incisural space. Small in volume but anatomically dense, it contains the motor fibers of the [[cerebral-peduncles]], the oculomotor nuclear complex, the red nucleus, the substantia nigra, the periaqueductal gray, and the dorsal tectal plate (quadrigeminal bodies).

## External Anatomy

### Boundaries

- **Superior:** mammillothalamic groove (ventral) and posterior commissure (dorsal)
- **Inferior:** pontomesencephalic sulcus (ventral) and inferior colliculus (dorsal)
- **Lateral:** the two lateral mesencephalic sulci (anterolateral sulcus and lateral mesencephalic sulcus)

### Surfaces

| Surface | Structures |
|---------|------------|
| **Anterior** | Interpeduncular fossa + [[cerebral-peduncles]]; CN III emergence |
| **Posterior (tectum)** | Quadrigeminal plate — superior + inferior colliculi; CN IV emergence below inferior colliculus |
| **Lateral** | Cerebral peduncle continuing into ambient cistern |

## Internal Zones (From Ventral → Dorsal)

| Zone | Contents |
|------|----------|
| **Basis pedunculi** ([[cerebral-peduncles]]) | Corticospinal, corticobulbar, corticopontine fibers |
| **Substantia nigra** | Pars compacta (dopaminergic → striatum), pars reticulata (GABAergic → thalamus) |
| **Tegmentum** | Red nucleus, oculomotor and trochlear nuclei, medial lemniscus, medial longitudinal fasciculus (MLF), reticular formation, periaqueductal gray |
| **Cerebral aqueduct** | CSF channel between 3rd and 4th ventricles |
| **Tectum (quadrigeminal plate)** | Superior colliculus (visuomotor), inferior colliculus (auditory) |

## Cranial Nerves

- **CN III (oculomotor)** — nuclei in rostral midbrain at superior colliculus level; fascicles cross the red nucleus and MLF, exit at the interpeduncular fossa (medial peduncle); see [[oculomotor-nerve]]
- **CN IV (trochlear)** — the only CN to decussate and the only one to exit dorsally; emerges below the inferior colliculus; wraps around the cerebral peduncle in the ambient cistern

## Vascular Supply

- **Paramedian branches** from the basilar apex and P1 segments — supply interpeduncular fossa and medial midbrain (CN III fascicle)
- **Short circumflex branches** (from P1, SCA) — supply lateral peduncle
- **Long circumflex branches** (thalamoperforating, thalamogeniculate from P2; SCA circumflex) — supply tegmentum, colliculi
- **Posterior choroidal arteries** — dorsal tectum, quadrigeminal plate
- **Anterior choroidal artery** — variable contribution to the optic tract and lateral peduncle

## Cisternal Relations

The midbrain is ringed by the [[basal-cisterns]]:

| Cistern | Surface |
|---------|---------|
| **Interpeduncular** | Anterior (between peduncles) |
| **[[crural-cistern]]** | Between peduncle and uncus |
| **[[ambient-cistern]]** | Between peduncle/tegmentum and [[parahippocampal-gyrus]] |
| **Quadrigeminal** | Posterior (behind tectum) |

These cisterns are the principal operative corridors for all midbrain surgery and are defined around CN III, CN IV, and the PCA/SCA course.

## Classical Syndromes

| Syndrome | Lesion | Signs |
|----------|--------|-------|
| **Weber** | Ventral midbrain (basis + CN III) | Ipsilateral CN III palsy + contralateral hemiparesis |
| **Benedikt** | Tegmentum + red nucleus + CN III | Weber + contralateral tremor/ataxia |
| **Claude** | Tegmentum (red nucleus, SCN) + CN III | CN III palsy + ipsilateral limb ataxia |
| **Parinaud (dorsal midbrain)** | Tectum | Upgaze paresis, light-near dissociation, convergence-retraction nystagmus |
| **Top-of-the-basilar** | Thalamoperforators + midbrain | Vertical gaze, pupillary, consciousness disturbances |

## Surgical Relevance

### Incisural Approaches

- **Anterior incisural space**: via pterional / orbitozygomatic transsylvian — ICA, optic apparatus, [[basilar-artery]] apex, medial midbrain
- **Middle incisural space**: subtemporal — ambient cistern, lateral midbrain, CN IV
- **Posterior incisural space**: supracerebellar-infratentorial or occipital-transtentorial — tectum, pineal region, dorsal midbrain

### Safe Entry Zones (for intrinsic cavernomas / gliomas)

- **Perioculomotor** zone — medial peduncle, sparing CN III fascicle
- **Anterior mesencephalic sulcus** — between peduncle and substantia nigra
- **Lateral mesencephalic sulcus** — between peduncle and tegmentum laterally
- **Supracollicular** / **intercollicular** — for tectal and periaqueductal lesions

### Endoscopic Third Ventriculostomy (ETV)

- The floor of the third ventricle sits just rostral to the midbrain — the **tuber cinereum** and **mammillary bodies** are the targets, with the **basilar apex** immediately beneath; perforating artery territory includes midbrain perforators

### Brainstem Cavernous Malformation Surgery

- Two-point rule: draw a line from the long axis of the lesion to the surface; approach along that trajectory, using the nearest safe entry zone
- Diffusion-tensor imaging of corticospinal tract informs peduncle entry

## Midbrain and Herniation

- **Transtentorial (uncal) herniation** compresses the midbrain from the side — lateral peduncle injury, contralateral hemiparesis, and CN III palsy (ipsilateral blown pupil)
- **Central (diencephalic) herniation** produces rostro-caudal midbrain deterioration with progressive loss of brainstem reflexes
- **Duret hemorrhages** — small midbrain/pons hemorrhages from perforator avulsion in transtentorial herniation — often agonal
