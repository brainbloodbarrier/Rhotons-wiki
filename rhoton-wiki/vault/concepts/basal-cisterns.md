# Basal Cisterns

The basal cisterns are subarachnoid spaces at the base of the brain containing CSF, arteries, veins, and cranial nerves. They are the natural surgical corridors through which deep structures are accessed. Arachnoid membranes separate adjacent cisterns.

## Supratentorial Cisterns

### Sylvian Cistern
Located within the [[sylvian-fissure]]. Contains the [[middle-cerebral-artery]] and its branches. Divided into sphenoidal and operculoinsular compartments.

### Carotid Cistern
Surrounds the [[internal-carotid-artery]] as it enters the subarachnoid space. Bounded laterally by the lateral carotid membrane, medially by the medial carotid membrane (separating it from the chiasmatic cistern).

### Chiasmatic Cistern
Surrounds the [[optic-chiasm]]. Contains the [[anterior-cerebral-artery]] (A1), [[anterior-communicating-artery]], and superior hypophyseal arteries.

### Interpeduncular Cistern
Between the cerebral peduncles. Contains the basilar apex, [[posterior-cerebral-artery]] (P1), [[oculomotor-nerve]] (CN III), and perforating arteries to the posterior perforated substance. Part of the anterior incisural space in the [[tentorial-incisura]].

### Crural Cistern
Between the cerebral peduncle and uncus/parahippocampal gyrus. Contains the [[posterior-cerebral-artery]] (P2A), [[anterior-choroidal-artery]], and basal vein. Part of the middle incisural space.

### Ambient Cistern
Continuation of the crural cistern posteriorly, between the midbrain and parahippocampal gyrus. Contains [[posterior-cerebral-artery]] (P2P), [[superior-cerebellar-artery]], basal vein of Rosenthal, and trochlear nerve (CN IV).

### Quadrigeminal Cistern
Behind the midbrain tectum. Contains the vein of Galen, internal cerebral veins, pineal gland, [[posterior-cerebral-artery]] (P3), and [[superior-cerebellar-artery]] branches. Part of the posterior incisural space.

### Lamina Terminalis Cistern
Above the [[optic-chiasm]], anterior to the lamina terminalis. Contains [[anterior-communicating-artery]] perforators. Can be opened for CSF drainage during aneurysm surgery.

## Posterior Fossa Cisterns

### Prepontine Cistern
Between the anterior pons and clivus. Contains the basilar artery and abducens nerve (CN VI).

### [[cerebellopontine-cistern|Cerebellopontine Cistern]]
See [[cerebellopontine-cistern]]. Contains CN V–VIII, [[superior-cerebellar-artery]], [[aica]].

### Cerebellomedullary Cistern (Cisterna Magna)
Between the medulla and cerebellum, behind the fourth ventricle. Contains [[pica]], vertebral arteries, and CN IX–XII. Communicates with the fourth ventricle via the foramen of Magendie.

### Premedullary Cistern
Between the anterior medulla and lower clivus. Contains the vertebral arteries and anterior spinal artery.

## Surgical Significance

The cisterns are accessed through standard neurosurgical approaches:
- [[pterional-approach]] → sylvian, carotid, chiasmatic, interpeduncular, crural cisterns
- Subtemporal → ambient, crural cisterns
- [[retrosigmoid-approach]] → [[cerebellopontine-cistern]]
- [[far-lateral-approach]] → cerebellomedullary, premedullary cisterns
- [[transsphenoidal-approach]] → chiasmatic cistern (from below)
