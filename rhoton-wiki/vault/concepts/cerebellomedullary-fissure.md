# Cerebellomedullary Fissure

The cerebellomedullary fissure is the deep cleft between the inferior surface of the [[cerebellum]] (tonsils and biventer) and the dorsal surface of the medulla oblongata. It is the anatomical gateway for the [[telovelar-approach]], providing a natural corridor to the [[fourth-ventricle]] without cerebellar incision.

## Anatomy

### Boundaries
- **Anterior (deep)**: dorsal surface of medulla oblongata
- **Posterior (superficial)**: cerebellar tonsils and biventer lobule
- **Superior**: inferior medullary velum and tela choroidea (roof of fourth ventricle)
- **Lateral**: extends to the lateral recesses of the fourth ventricle
- **Inferior**: foramen of Magendie opens into the cisterna magna

### Contents

| Structure | Relationship |
|---|---|
| **[[pica]] (telovelotonsillar segment)** | Courses through the fissure; loops around the tonsil; guides the surgical trajectory |
| **Tela choroidea** | Thin membrane forming the lower part of the fourth ventricle roof |
| **Inferior medullary velum** | Thin neural tissue connecting the nodulus to the flocculus, forming the upper boundary of the fissure |
| **Choroid plexus** | Extends from the fourth ventricle through the foramen of Magendie into the fissure |
| **Foramen of Magendie** | Midline aperture in the tela choroidea; connects fourth ventricle to cisterna magna |

### PICA Relationship
The [[pica]] has an intimate relationship with the fissure through its **telovelotonsillar segment**:

1. PICA loops inferiorly around the cerebellar tonsil
2. Ascends into the cerebellomedullary fissure
3. Courses along the inferior medullary velum and tela choroidea
4. Exits the fissure to supply the suboccipital cerebellar surface

The PICA serves as a **guide artery** for opening the fissure — following the artery leads directly to the tela choroidea and inferior medullary velum.

## Telovelar Approach

The [[telovelar-approach]] exploits the cerebellomedullary fissure to access the [[fourth-ventricle]]:

### Steps
1. **Suboccipital craniotomy/craniectomy** — expose the cerebellar tonsils and cisterna magna
2. **Tonsillar retraction** — gently separate the tonsils laterally
3. **Fissure opening** — dissect into the cerebellomedullary fissure, following the PICA
4. **Tela choroidea opening** — incise the tela choroidea and inferior medullary velum to enter the fourth ventricle
5. **Fourth ventricle exposure** — the entire floor (rhomboid fossa) from obex to aqueduct is visible

### Advantages Over Transvermian Approach
| Feature | Telovelar | Transvermian |
|---|---|---|
| Cerebellar incision | None | Vermis split required |
| Cerebellar mutism risk | Lower | Higher |
| Fourth ventricle floor exposure | Excellent | Good |
| Lateral recess access | Good (via lateral extension) | Limited |
| PICA preservation | Guided by the artery | PICA not followed |

## Structures Visible Through the Fissure

After opening the tela and velum, the surgeon can see:

- **Rhomboid fossa** — floor of the [[fourth-ventricle]] with the facial colliculus, striae medullaris, hypoglossal and vagal trigones
- **Obex** — inferior apex of the fourth ventricle, calamus scriptorius
- **[[cerebral-aqueduct]]** — visible superiorly through the fourth ventricle
- **Lateral recesses** — extend laterally to the [[cerebellopontine-cistern]], containing the foramen of Luschka

## Clinical Significance

### Fourth Ventricle Tumors
The cerebellomedullary fissure/telovelar approach is the preferred corridor for:
- **Ependymomas** — arise from the fourth ventricle floor
- **Medulloblastomas** — arise from the roof (vermis/velum)
- **Choroid plexus tumors** — arise from the [[choroid-plexus]] within the ventricle
- **Epidermoids** — extend through the foramen of Magendie

### Obstructive Hydrocephalus
Tumors within the cerebellomedullary fissure or fourth ventricle can obstruct the foramen of Magendie and/or the [[cerebral-aqueduct]], causing obstructive hydrocephalus.

## Connections

- Between: [[cerebellum]] (tonsils) and medulla of [[brainstem]]
- Contains: [[pica]] (telovelotonsillar segment), tela choroidea, [[choroid-plexus]]
- Opens into: cisterna magna (foramen of Magendie)
- Accesses: [[fourth-ventricle]] via [[telovelar-approach]]
- Superior: inferior medullary velum → [[fourth-ventricle]] roof
- Lateral: lateral recesses → [[cerebellopontine-cistern]]
- Related: [[foramen-magnum]] (below), [[cerebral-aqueduct]] (above)
