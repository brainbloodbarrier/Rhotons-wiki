# Meckel's Cave

Meckel's cave (cavum trigeminale) is a dural and arachnoidal recess that houses the trigeminal (gasserian) ganglion and the posterior root of the [[trigeminal-nerve|CN V]]. Located at the apex of the petrous part of the [[temporal-bone]], it creates a communication between the [[posterior-fossa]] subarachnoid space and the middle cranial fossa. It is a critical surgical landmark in approaches to the [[cavernous-sinus]], petroclival region, and trigeminal neuralgia.

## Anatomy

### Walls
- **Superior** — the [[dural-venous-sinuses|superior petrosal sinus]] passes above the posterior trigeminal root, forming the upper margin of the cave ostium
- **Medial** — the posterior wall of the [[cavernous-sinus]]; the cave communicates with the cavernous sinus venous channels
- **Lateral** — the dura of the middle cranial fossa floor ([[temporal-bone]] anterior petrous surface)
- **Inferior** — the [[petrous-apex]] bone
- **Posterior (ostium)** — opens into the [[cerebellopontine-cistern]] (posterior fossa subarachnoid space)

### Contents
- **Trigeminal (gasserian) ganglion** — the sensory ganglion; located in the anterior part of the cave
- **Posterior trigeminal root** — enters the cave from the [[cerebellopontine-cistern]] through the porus trigeminus
- **Motor root** — courses on the medial side of the sensory fibers within the cave
- **CSF** — the cave is a subarachnoid extension; CSF from the posterior fossa fills it
- **Meningeal branches** — small dural arteries from the meningohypophyseal trunk of the [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]]

### Trigeminal divisions exit
From the ganglion, the three divisions diverge:
- **V1 (ophthalmic)** — enters the lateral wall of the [[cavernous-sinus]], exits via the superior orbital fissure to the [[orbit]]
- **V2 (maxillary)** — exits through the foramen rotundum ([[sphenoid-bone]])
- **V3 (mandibular)** — exits through the foramen ovale ([[sphenoid-bone]])

## Surgical Significance

### Trigeminal neuralgia
- **Percutaneous procedures** — balloon compression, radiofrequency rhizotomy, and glycerol injection access the ganglion in Meckel's cave through the foramen ovale
- **Microvascular decompression** — via [[retrosigmoid-approach]]; the [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]] (most common offending vessel) is mobilized off the posterior root at or near the porus trigeminus
- **Gamma knife radiosurgery** — targets the root entry zone at the porus

### Trigeminal schwannomas
May arise within Meckel's cave, the posterior root, or any division. Dumbbell-shaped tumors extend from the posterior fossa through Meckel's cave into the middle fossa. Require combined approaches or extended [[subtemporal-approach]] with petrosectomy.

### Cavernous sinus surgery
Meckel's cave is exposed by retracting or dividing the [[trigeminal-nerve|trigeminal nerve]] posteriorly. The posteromedial (Kawase) triangle of the middle fossa is bounded by the trigeminal nerve and the [[greater-petrosal-nerve]] — drilling here exposes the lateral [[clivus]] and the cave.

### [[middle-fossa-approach]]
The trigeminal impression on the petrous apex is the landmark for Meckel's cave from above. The [[subtemporal-approach]] with [[cerebral-lobes|temporal lobe]] elevation exposes the middle fossa floor over the cave.

## Paraclival Triangles

Two triangles on the posterior clival/petrous surface relate to Meckel's cave:
- **Inferolateral paraclival triangle** — bounded by [[trochlear-nerve|CN IV]] and [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]] dural entry sites; the porus trigeminus (Meckel's cave entrance) is at the center
- **Inferomedial paraclival triangle** — bounded by CN IV, CN VI, and the posterior clinoid; the dura here forms the posterior wall of the [[cavernous-sinus]]

## Relations

- Houses the [[trigeminal-nerve]] ganglion and posterior root
- Located at the apex of the [[temporal-bone]] (petrous part)
- Communicates posteriorly with the [[cerebellopontine-cistern]] via the porus trigeminus
- Communicates medially with the [[cavernous-sinus]]
- [[dural-venous-sinuses|Superior petrosal sinus]] crosses above the cave entrance
- [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]] courses near the posterior root (neurovascular compression site)
- [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]] crosses the petrous apex below the cave — see [[cranial-nerves-overview]]
- Accessed via [[retrosigmoid-approach]] (posterior root), [[subtemporal-approach]] (middle fossa), percutaneous (foramen ovale)
- [[sphenoid-bone]] (foramen rotundum, foramen ovale) transmits V2 and V3 divisions
- See [[surgical-approaches-overview]]
