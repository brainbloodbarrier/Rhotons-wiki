# Substantia Nigra

The **substantia nigra** (SN) is a laminar, heavily pigmented nucleus of the rostral mesencephalic tegmentum that appears black on gross section due to neuromelanin in the dopaminergic neurons of its compact part. It sits as a wafer between the ventral [[cerebral-peduncles|basis pedunculi]] and the tegmental [[red-nucleus]], and is divided into two functionally distinct parts: the **pars compacta (SNc)** and the **pars reticulata (SNr)**. The substantia nigra is the defining target of Parkinson's disease pathology and an essential internal landmark for midbrain surgery and deep brain stimulation planning.

## Location and Boundaries

| Plane | Relation |
|-------|----------|
| **Ventral** | [[cerebral-peduncles]] (basis pedunculi) — the SN defines the dorsal limit of the peduncle on axial section |
| **Dorsal** | Midbrain tegmentum, medial lemniscus, [[red-nucleus]] (immediately posteromedial) |
| **Medial** | CN III fascicles exiting the midbrain toward the interpeduncular fossa |
| **Lateral** | Lateral mesencephalic sulcus and the surface of the peduncle |
| **Rostral** | Subthalamic nucleus (STN) — separated by a thin capsule; the two nuclei are in direct functional continuity |
| **Caudal** | Ventral tegmental area (medial) and the pontine-midbrain junction |

## Subdivisions

1. **Pars compacta (SNc)** — dorsal, densely pigmented dopaminergic neurons giving rise to the **nigrostriatal pathway** → putamen and caudate; degeneration here causes Parkinson's disease.
2. **Pars reticulata (SNr)** — ventral, GABAergic; one of the two **output nuclei of the [[basal-ganglia]]** (the other is the globus pallidus internus). SNr inhibits the superior colliculus and thalamus, gating eye movements and motor plans.
3. **Pars lateralis** — a small laterally displaced cell group.

## Connections

- **Nigrostriatal tract** (SNc → striatum) — modulates direct and indirect basal ganglia pathways via D1 and D2 receptors.
- **Striatonigral projections** (striatum → SNr) — GABAergic inhibitory input.
- **SNr → thalamus (VA/VL)** — GABAergic output closing the basal-ganglia–thalamocortical loop.
- **SNr → superior colliculus** — saccadic control; loss produces oculomotor disinhibition.
- **STN → SNr** — glutamatergic drive from the subthalamic nucleus.

## Surgical Relevance

- **STN deep brain stimulation (DBS) for Parkinson's disease** — the STN lies immediately superior to the SN; dorsoventral trajectory planning uses the SN as a landmark to avoid ventral current spread and mood side effects. Susceptibility-weighted MRI sequences visualize the SN as a dark laminar band.
- **MR-guided focused ultrasound and thalamotomy** — the DRTT passes medial to the SN en route to the [[red-nucleus]]; the SN bounds the safe lateral margin of ventral lesioning.
- **Midbrain safe entry zones** — the SN defines the ventral limit of the perimesencephalic tegmentum; the **anterior mesencephalic zone** (between CN III rootlets medially and the SN laterally) is used for selected midbrain cavernomas.
- **Orientation on axial MR** — at the level of the superior colliculus, the SN is the **black band** between the peduncle (anterior) and the [[red-nucleus]] (posteromedial) — a cardinal internal landmark for surgery of the midbrain via the [[subtemporal-approach]] or supracerebellar-infratentorial route.

## Clinical Significance

- **Parkinson's disease** — progressive loss of SNc dopaminergic neurons produces the classical triad of resting tremor, rigidity, and bradykinesia; pigmented cell loss gives the SN a pale appearance on gross pathology.
- **Multiple system atrophy (MSA) and progressive supranuclear palsy (PSP)** — also involve the SN but with additional striatal and tegmental pathology.
- **Neuromelanin MRI** — a dedicated sequence that visualizes pigment loss in the SNc and locus coeruleus, emerging as a Parkinson biomarker.
- **Iron-sensitive sequences** — SWI and QSM show the SN as a prominent low-signal band, and loss of the dorsolateral nigral hyperintensity ("swallowtail sign") is a diagnostic feature of Parkinson's disease.

## Relations

- Ventral midbrain tegmentum, immediately dorsal to the [[cerebral-peduncles|basis pedunculi]]
- Anterolateral to the [[red-nucleus]]
- Ventral to the subthalamic nucleus and the [[thalamus]]
- Medial to the lateral mesencephalic sulcus
- A component of the [[basal-ganglia]] output circuitry (SNr) and the dopaminergic ascending system (SNc)
