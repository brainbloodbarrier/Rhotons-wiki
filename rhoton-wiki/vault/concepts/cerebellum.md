# Cerebellum

The cerebellum occupies the [[posterior-fossa]] below the [[tentorium-cerebelli]]. It consists of two hemispheres connected by the vermis and is attached to the [[brainstem]] by three paired cerebellar peduncles. It has three surfaces (tentorial, petrosal, suboccipital) separated by two major fissures, and is supplied by three paired arteries — each defining one of the three neurovascular complexes of the posterior fossa.

## Surfaces

### Tentorial surface
Faces the tentorium cerebelli and slopes downward from the apex of the vermis. Supplied by the [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]]. The apex lies just behind the cerebellomesencephalic fissure. Exposed via the [[supracerebellar-infratentorial-approach]].

### Petrosal surface
Faces the [[temporal-bone|petrous temporal bone]] and the [[cerebellopontine-cistern]]. Supplied by the [[aica|AICA]]. The flocculus projects from this surface into the cerebellopontine angle near [[facial-nerve|CN VII]] and [[vestibulocochlear-nerve|CN VIII]]. Exposed via the [[retrosigmoid-approach]].

### Suboccipital surface
Faces the occipital bone posteriorly. Supplied by the [[pica|PICA]]. The tonsils and the inferior vermis form the most posterior and inferior parts. The tonsils sit above the [[foramen-magnum]] and are the structures involved in **[[herniation-syndromes|tonsillar herniation]]** (Chiari malformation). Exposed via midline suboccipital craniectomy and the [[far-lateral-approach]].

## Fissures

### Cerebellomesencephalic fissure
Between the cerebellum and the midbrain ([[brainstem]]). Contains the [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]] branches and precerebellar arteries passing to the dentate nucleus. The [[cerebellar-peduncles|superior cerebellar peduncle]] forms its anterior wall. This fissure lies in the [[posterior-incisural-space|posterior incisural space]] of the [[tentorial-incisura]].

### [[cerebellomedullary-fissure]]
Between the cerebellum and the medulla. Contains the [[pica|PICA]] (telovelotonsillar segment), the tela choroidea, and the inferior medullary velum. The **[[telovelar-approach]]** opens this fissure to access the [[fourth-ventricle]] floor without splitting the vermis.

### Cerebellopontine fissure
Between the cerebellum and the pons. Contains [[aica|AICA]] branches coursing along the middle cerebellar peduncle. Related to the lateral recess and foramen of Luschka.

## Cerebellar Peduncles

Three paired fiber bundles connect the cerebellum to the [[brainstem]]:

| Peduncle | Connects to | Primary tracts | Neurovascular complex |
|---|---|---|---|
| Superior | Midbrain | Efferent (dentatorubrothalamic) | Upper ([[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]]) |
| Middle | Pons | Afferent (pontocerebellar) | Middle ([[aica|AICA]]) |
| Inferior | Medulla | Afferent/efferent (spinocerebellar, olivocerebellar) | Lower ([[pica|PICA]]) |

## Deep Nuclei

The dentate nucleus is the largest and most lateral deep cerebellar nucleus. The [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]] sends precerebellar branches along the superior peduncle to supply it. It is a surgical landmark in the cerebellomesencephalic fissure, forming the **dentate tubercle** near the fastigium.

## Vascular Supply — Three Neurovascular Complexes

Each cerebellar surface is paired with a major artery, a brainstem segment, a peduncle, a fissure, and a group of cranial nerves (see [[brainstem]] for the complete table):

- **Upper complex** — [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]], tentorial surface, midbrain, [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]]/[[trochlear-nerve|IV]]/[[trigeminal-nerve|V]]
- **Middle complex** — [[aica|AICA]], petrosal surface, pons, [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]]/[[facial-nerve|VII]]/[[vestibulocochlear-nerve|VIII]]
- **Lower complex** — [[pica|PICA]], suboccipital surface, medulla, [[lower-cranial-nerves|CN IX-XII]]

## Surgical Significance

### Telovelar approach
Opens the cerebellomedullary fissure between the tonsil and the inferior medullary velum/tela choroidea. Follows the [[pica|PICA]] to access the fourth ventricle floor without vermian splitting. Used for fourth ventricle tumors (ependymomas, medulloblastomas).

### Tonsillar herniation
The cerebellar tonsils descend through the [[foramen-magnum]], compressing the medulla, [[vertebral-artery]], and [[pica|PICA]]. Chiari I malformation = tonsils >5 mm below the foramen magnum.

### Cerebellar retraction
- Tentorial surface retraction in [[supracerebellar-infratentorial-approach]] — risk of vermian contusion
- Hemispheric retraction in [[retrosigmoid-approach]] — risk of bridging vein avulsion

## Relations

- Occupies the [[posterior-fossa]] below the tentorium
- Attached to [[brainstem]] by three peduncles
- Three surfaces supplied by [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]], [[aica|AICA]], [[pica|PICA]]
- Drained by [[posterior-fossa-veins]] → [[dural-venous-sinuses]]
- [[foramen-magnum]] below (tonsillar herniation pathway)
- [[tentorial-incisura]] above (ascending herniation pathway)
- [[basal-cisterns]] (cisterna magna, [[quadrigeminal-cistern]]) surround the cerebellum
- Fourth ventricle ([[lateral-and-third-ventricles]]) is embedded between cerebellum and [[brainstem]]
- See [[surgical-approaches-overview]] for approach selection
