# Cerebellar Peduncles

The cerebellar peduncles are three paired fiber bundles that connect the [[cerebellum]] to the [[brainstem]]. They carry all afferent and efferent pathways of the cerebellum and serve as critical surgical landmarks for posterior fossa approaches and brainstem safe entry zones.

## Three Peduncles

### Superior Cerebellar Peduncle (Brachium Conjunctivum)
- **Connects**: [[cerebellum]] ↔ [[brainstem|midbrain]]
- **Primary direction**: efferent (cerebellum → midbrain)
- **Contents**: dentatorubrothalamic tract (major cerebellar outflow to red nucleus and [[thalamus]]), anterior spinocerebellar tract (afferent)
- **Course**: exits the cerebellum superiorly, forms the lateral wall of the upper [[fourth-ventricle]], then decussates in the midbrain tegmentum
- **Fissure**: the cerebellomesencephalic fissure separates the superior peduncle from the midbrain

### Middle Cerebellar Peduncle (Brachium Pontis)
- **Connects**: [[cerebellum]] ↔ pons
- **Primary direction**: afferent (pons → cerebellum)
- **Contents**: pontocerebellar fibers (corticopontocerebellar pathway) — the largest of the three peduncles
- **Course**: wraps around the lateral surface of the pons as a broad band of fibers
- **Relation**: the [[trigeminal-nerve|CN V]] root emerges from the lateral pons at the junction of the middle peduncle and the pons

### Inferior Cerebellar Peduncle (Restiform Body)
- **Connects**: [[cerebellum]] ↔ medulla oblongata
- **Primary direction**: mixed (afferent dominant)
- **Contents**: posterior spinocerebellar tract, olivocerebellar fibers, vestibulocerebellar fibers, cuneocerebellar fibers
- **Course**: ascends from the posterolateral medulla into the cerebellum; forms the lateral wall of the lower [[fourth-ventricle]]
- **Relation**: the [[cerebellomedullary-fissure]] lies between the inferior peduncle and the medulla

## Relationships to the Fourth Ventricle

The cerebellar peduncles form the lateral walls of the [[fourth-ventricle]]:

| Peduncle | Fourth Ventricle Relation |
|---|---|
| Superior | Lateral wall of upper (rostral) fourth ventricle |
| Middle | Lateral wall at the level of the lateral recess |
| Inferior | Lateral wall of lower (caudal) fourth ventricle |

The floor of the fourth ventricle (rhomboid fossa) lies medial to the peduncles, and the cerebellar surface lies lateral/posterior to them.

## Surgical Significance

### Brainstem Safe Entry Zones
The peduncles define several safe entry zones into the [[brainstem]]:

| Safe Entry Zone | Location | Access Via |
|---|---|---|
| **Peritrigeminal zone** | Lateral to CN V at middle peduncle-pons junction | [[retrosigmoid-approach]] |
| **Suprafacial triangle** | Medial to superior peduncle, above facial colliculus | [[telovelar-approach]] |
| **Infrafacial triangle** | Between facial colliculus and striae medullaris | [[telovelar-approach]] |
| **Lateral mesencephalic sulcus** | Between cerebral peduncle and superior peduncle | [[subtemporal-approach]] |

### Approach Context

| Approach | Peduncle Encountered |
|---|---|
| [[telovelar-approach]] | Inferior peduncle (lateral wall of lower fourth ventricle) |
| [[supracerebellar-infratentorial-approach]] | Superior peduncle (at cerebellomesencephalic fissure) |
| [[retrosigmoid-approach]] | Middle peduncle (at CPA, CN V root entry) |
| [[subtemporal-approach]] | Superior peduncle (lateral mesencephalic sulcus) |

### Tumor Surgery
Intrinsic brainstem tumors (gliomas) may expand a peduncle, and their resection must respect the fiber tracts within:
- **Superior peduncle injury** → cerebellar ataxia (dentatorubrothalamic outflow interrupted)
- **Middle peduncle injury** → limb ataxia (corticopontocerebellar pathway)
- **Inferior peduncle injury** → truncal ataxia, vestibular dysfunction

## Cerebellar Fissures

The peduncles are related to the three major cerebellar fissures:

| Fissure | Between | Related Peduncle |
|---|---|---|
| **Cerebellomesencephalic** | [[cerebellum]] and midbrain | Superior peduncle |
| **Cerebellopontine** | [[cerebellum]] and pons | Middle peduncle |
| **[[cerebellomedullary-fissure]]** | [[cerebellum]] and medulla | Inferior peduncle |

## Connections

- Superior: [[cerebellum]] ↔ [[brainstem|midbrain]] (dentatorubrothalamic tract → [[thalamus]])
- Middle: [[cerebellum]] ↔ pons (corticopontocerebellar)
- Inferior: [[cerebellum]] ↔ medulla (spinocerebellar, olivocerebellar)
- Form walls of: [[fourth-ventricle]]
- Define: brainstem safe entry zones
- Related fissure: [[cerebellomedullary-fissure]]
- CN landmark: [[trigeminal-nerve]] at middle peduncle-pons junction
