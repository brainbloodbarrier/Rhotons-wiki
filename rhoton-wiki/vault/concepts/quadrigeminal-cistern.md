# Quadrigeminal Cistern

The quadrigeminal cistern (cistern of the great cerebral vein) occupies the posterior incisural space behind the [[brainstem|midbrain]] tectum, between the [[cerebellum]] (superior vermis) and the splenium of the [[corpus-callosum]]. It is the convergence point of the [[deep-venous-system]]: the [[internal-cerebral-veins]] and [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal]] join to form the [[vein-of-galen]] within this cistern. The [[pineal-region|pineal gland]] projects into it. The [[supracerebellar-infratentorial-approach]] reaches this cistern from below.

## Boundaries
- **Anterior** — [[brainstem|tectal plate]] (superior and inferior colliculi)
- **Posterior** — splenium of [[corpus-callosum]] and [[velum-interpositum]]
- **Superior** — [[tentorium-cerebelli|tentorium]] and [[cerebral-lobes|occipital lobe]]
- **Inferior** — superior surface of [[cerebellum]] (culmen, declive)
- **Lateral** — continuous with [[ambient-cistern]] on each side

## Contents
### Veins
- **[[vein-of-galen]]** — formed here by junction of [[internal-cerebral-veins]] and [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal]]
- **[[internal-cerebral-veins]]** — exit [[velum-interpositum]] into the cistern
- **[[basal-vein-of-rosenthal]]** — arrives from [[ambient-cistern]]
- **Precentral cerebellar vein** — drains superior vermis into [[vein-of-galen]]

### Arteries
- **[[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] P3 segment** — courses around the splenium
- **[[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]]** branches — supply the tentorial cerebellar surface
- **[[posterior-choroidal-arteries]]** — course through the cistern to the [[choroid-plexus]]

### Structures
- **[[pineal-region|Pineal gland]]** — projects posteriorly into the cistern
- **[[trochlear-nerve|CN IV]]** — emerges from the dorsal midbrain into this cistern

## Surgical Significance
### Supracerebellar Infratentorial Approach
- **[[supracerebellar-infratentorial-approach]]** is the primary route — cerebellum falls away with gravity (sitting/prone position)
- Reaches the [[pineal-region]], tectal plate, and posterior [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|third ventricle]]
- **[[vein-of-galen]]** is the dominant obstacle — must not be sacrificed
- Precentral cerebellar vein may need division (usually tolerated)

### Occipital Transtentorial Approach
- Alternative superior route through occipital lobe retraction + tentorial division
- Useful for lesions extending above the [[vein-of-galen]]

### Pineal Tumors
- Germinomas, pineocytomas, pineoblastomas arise in this cistern
- Tumor biopsy may be endoscopic (through third ventricle) or open (SCIT)
- [[cerebral-aqueduct]] compression → obstructive hydrocephalus
