# Brainstem

The brainstem comprises the midbrain, pons, and medulla oblongata — a continuous column connecting the [[cerebrum]] to the spinal cord. It passes through the [[tentorial-incisura]] and occupies the central core of the [[posterior-fossa]]. All cranial nerves except CN I and CN II originate from or traverse the brainstem, and all corticospinal, sensory, and cerebellar pathways pass through it.

## Midbrain (Mesencephalon)

The midbrain occupies the tentorial notch and is surrounded by the anterior, middle, and posterior incisural spaces of the [[tentorial-incisura]].

### Surface anatomy
- **Anterior surface (cerebral peduncles)** — paired, converging crura cerebri separated by the interpeduncular fossa; the tentorial free edges either touch or are separated from the peduncles by a narrow interval
- **Lateral surface** — the ambient cistern wraps around between the peduncles and the medial temporal lobe
- **Posterior surface (tectum/quadrigeminal plate)** — superior and inferior colliculi; the [[trochlear-nerve|CN IV]] emerges below the inferior colliculus (only CN with dorsal exit)

### Key relations
- [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] — emerges from the interpeduncular fossa between the [[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] and [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]]
- [[trochlear-nerve|CN IV]] — emerges dorsally below the inferior colliculus
- Cerebral peduncles transmit the corticospinal and corticopontine tracts
- The cerebellomesencephalic fissure separates the midbrain from the cerebellum, containing [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]] branches

### Cisterns
- **Interpeduncular cistern** — contains the [[basilar-artery]] apex, [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] origin
- **Crural cistern** — between peduncle and uncus, contains [[anterior-choroidal-artery]]
- **Ambient cistern** — lateral to the midbrain, contains [[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]], [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]], basal vein
- **Quadrigeminal cistern** — behind the tectum, contains pineal gland, vein of Galen

## Pons

The pons is the middle segment, situated between the midbrain and medulla. Its ventral surface bulges forward (the "belly" of the pons) and rests against the clivus of the [[cranial-base]].

### Surface anatomy
- **Anterior surface** — the basilar sulcus houses the [[basilar-artery]]; the [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]] emerges at the pontomedullary junction
- **Lateral surface** — the [[trigeminal-nerve|CN V]] exits the lateral mid-pons; the middle cerebellar peduncle connects the pons to the cerebellum
- **Posterior surface** — forms the superior half of the fourth ventricle floor (rhomboid fossa)

### Key relations
- [[basilar-artery]] — ascends on the ventral pons, giving off pontine perforating branches, [[aica]], and [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]]
- [[trigeminal-nerve|CN V]] — exits between the SCA (above) and AICA (below)
- [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]] — emerges at the pontomedullary sulcus, ascending along the clivus
- [[facial-nerve|CN VII]] and [[vestibulocochlear-nerve|CN VIII]] — emerge at the cerebellopontine angle, closely related to the [[aica]]

### Cisterns
- **Prepontine cistern** — between the clivus and the ventral pons, containing the [[basilar-artery]]
- **[[cerebellopontine-cistern]]** — lateral to the pons, containing CN V–VIII and AICA

## Medulla Oblongata

The medulla extends from the pontomedullary junction to the [[foramen-magnum]], where it blends indistinguishably into the upper spinal cord at the level of the C1 nerve roots.

### Surface anatomy
- **Anterior surface** — medullary pyramids (corticospinal tracts), facing the clivus and the odontoid process
- **Lateral surface** — the inferior olives are the dominant surface feature; the [[lower-cranial-nerves|CN IX, X, XI]] emerge dorsal to the olives
- **Posterior surface** — superior part forms the inferior half of the fourth ventricle floor and the inferior cerebellar peduncles; inferior part contains the gracile and cuneate fasciculi and tubercles

### Key relations
- [[vertebral-artery]] — ascends through the [[foramen-magnum]] along the anterolateral medulla, joined at the pontomedullary junction to form the [[basilar-artery]]
- [[pica]] — the largest vertebral artery branch; courses around the medulla among CN IX–XII rootlets
- [[lower-cranial-nerves|CN XII]] — rootlets emerge between the pyramids and olives (premedullary cistern)
- [[lower-cranial-nerves|CN IX, X, XI]] — emerge dorsal to the olives in the cerebellomedullary cistern

### Cisterns
- **Premedullary cistern** — between the anterior medulla and clivus; contains the [[vertebral-artery|vertebral arteries]] and CN XII rootlets
- **Cerebellomedullary cistern** — between the medulla and cerebellum; contains CN IX–XI, [[pica]], choroid plexus from the foramen of Luschka
- **Cisterna magna** ([[basal-cisterns]]) — dorsal to the medulla and vermis, behind the [[foramen-magnum]]

## Three Neurovascular Complexes

The brainstem organizes the [[posterior-fossa]] into three neurovascular complexes, each centered on one brainstem segment:

| Complex | Brainstem | Artery | Peduncle | Fissure | Surface | Cranial Nerves |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Upper | Midbrain | [[superior-cerebellar-artery\|SCA]] | Superior | Cerebellomesencephalic | Tentorial | III, IV, V |
| Middle | Pons | [[aica\|AICA]] | Middle | Cerebellopontine | Petrosal | VI, VII, VIII |
| Lower | Medulla | [[pica\|PICA]] | Inferior | Cerebellomedullary | Suboccipital | IX, X, XI, XII |

## Surgical Significance

### Safe entry zones
The brainstem has recognized "safe entry zones" where surgical access causes minimal damage:
- **Anterior mesencephalic** — between the peduncles (interpeduncular fossa)
- **Lateral mesencephalic** — lateral sulcus of the midbrain
- **Peritrigeminal zone** — around the CN V exit point on the lateral pons
- **Median sulcus of fourth ventricle** — posterior midline approach
- **Lateral medullary zone** — between the inferior olive and posterior lateral sulcus

### Approaches
- [[pterional-approach]] — anterior and middle incisural spaces, basilar apex
- [[subtemporal-approach]] — middle incisural space, ambient cistern
- [[supracerebellar-infratentorial-approach]] — posterior midbrain and tectum
- [[retrosigmoid-approach]] — cerebellopontine angle, lateral pons
- [[far-lateral-approach]] — anterolateral medulla, [[foramen-magnum]]
- [[transsphenoidal-approach|Transclival]] — ventral pons and clivus

### Herniation syndromes
- **Transtentorial (uncal) herniation** — uncus compresses midbrain and [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] against the tentorial edge
- **Tonsillar herniation** — cerebellar tonsils descend through [[foramen-magnum]], compressing medulla
- **Upward (ascending) herniation** — infratentorial mass pushes cerebellum upward through the [[tentorial-incisura]]

## Relations

- Occupies the central core of the [[posterior-fossa]]
- Passes through the [[tentorial-incisura]]
- Continuous with the [[cerebrum]] above and spinal cord below
- Surrounded by [[basal-cisterns]] (interpeduncular, prepontine, ambient, quadrigeminal, cerebellomedullary)
- All posterior circulation arteries closely related: [[basilar-artery]], [[vertebral-artery]], [[superior-cerebellar-artery]], [[aica]], [[pica]]
- All cranial nerves III–XII arise from or traverse the brainstem
- Fourth ventricle ([[lateral-and-third-ventricles|ventricles]]) lies dorsal to the pons and medulla
- [[cranial-base]] (clivus) forms the anterior bony wall
