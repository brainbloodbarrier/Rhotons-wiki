# Suboccipital Triangle

The suboccipital triangle is the deep muscular triangle in the posterior craniocervical region that contains the V3 (atlantic) segment of the [[vertebral-artery]] and the C1 nerve (suboccipital nerve). It is the key surgical landmark in the [[far-lateral-approach]] — identification of the triangle allows safe exposure of the vertebral artery before its dural entry at the [[foramen-magnum]].

## Boundaries

| Border | Muscle | Origin | Insertion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Superomedial** | Rectus capitis posterior major | Spine of the axis (C2) | Lateral part of inferior nuchal line (occipital bone) |
| **Superolateral** | Superior oblique | Transverse process of atlas (C1) | Between superior and inferior nuchal lines |
| **Inferolateral** | Inferior oblique | Spine and lamina of axis (C2) | Transverse process of atlas (C1) |

## Floor

The posterior arch of the atlas (C1) and the posterior atlanto-occipital membrane form the floor. The [[vertebral-artery]] V3 segment courses along this floor in the sulcus arteriosus — a groove on the superior surface of the posterior arch of C1.

## Contents

### Vertebral artery V3 segment
The V3 segment emerges from the C2 transverse foramen, curves posteriorly behind the C1 lateral mass, and courses medially along the floor of the suboccipital triangle in the groove on the atlas. Key features:
- The groove may be converted into a **complete bony ring** (ponticulus posticus/arcuate foramen) in 28% of specimens, partially bridged in 24%, and in a shallow groove in 48%
- The artery is surrounded by a **venous plexus** (anastomoses between deep cervical and epidural veins)
- It penetrates the posterior atlanto-occipital membrane and dura to enter the [[posterior-fossa]] as the V4 (intradural) segment

### C1 nerve (suboccipital nerve)
Passes between the [[vertebral-artery]] and the groove on the posterior arch of the atlas. It is a predominantly motor nerve supplying the suboccipital muscles. The dorsal root (when present) may arise from the spinal accessory nerve ([[lower-cranial-nerves|CN XI]]).

### Posterior meningeal artery
Arises from the V3 segment near the dural entry. Ascends to supply the dura on the posterior aspect of the [[foramen-magnum]] and [[posterior-fossa]].

### Posterior spinal artery
May arise from V3 (extradural) or V4 (intradural). Enters the dura with the [[vertebral-artery]] and C1 nerve through a common dural funnel.

## Overlying Muscles (superficial to deep)

The suboccipital triangle is deep to multiple muscle layers:
1. **Trapezius** — most superficial; from superior nuchal line to scapula
2. **Sternocleidomastoid** — from mastoid process to sternum/clavicle
3. **Splenius capitis** — from inferior nuchal line to lower cervical/upper thoracic spinous processes
4. **Semispinalis capitis** — between superior and inferior nuchal lines
5. **Longissimus capitis** — to posterior mastoid process
6. **Suboccipital muscles** — the three muscles forming the triangle (deepest layer)

## Surgical Significance

### Far-lateral approach ([[far-lateral-approach]])
The suboccipital triangle is the surgical roadmap:
1. **Identify the triangle** — dissect through trapezius, splenius, semispinalis to expose the suboccipital muscles
2. **Find the inferior oblique** — it runs between the C2 spinous process and the C1 transverse process, spanning the interval where the [[vertebral-artery]] transitions from V2 to V3
3. **Expose the V3 segment** — the artery sits on the posterior arch of C1, in the floor of the triangle; carefully separate it from the venous plexus
4. **Trace to dural entry** — follow V3 medially to where it penetrates the dura at the lateral edge of the [[foramen-magnum]]
5. **Continue intradurally** — open the dura to expose the V4 segment, [[pica]], and [[lower-cranial-nerves|CN IX-XII]]

### Ponticulus posticus
The bony ring over the V3 segment (when present) must be identified on preoperative CT. Drilling the ring is required to mobilize the artery during the [[far-lateral-approach]].

### C1 lateral mass screw placement
The V3 segment courses over the C1 lateral mass — screw placement for occipitocervical fixation must avoid the artery. The suboccipital triangle landmarks guide safe screw trajectories.

## Relations

- Contains the [[vertebral-artery]] V3 segment — see [[posterior-circulation]]
- Floor formed by the posterior arch of C1 (atlas) — related to [[foramen-magnum]]
- C1 nerve courses with the [[vertebral-artery]] through the dural funnel
- Posterior meningeal artery arises from V3 to supply [[meninges]] of the [[posterior-fossa]]
- Deep to multiple muscle layers of the posterior cervical region
- Overlies the [[foramen-magnum]] and cervicomedullary junction ([[brainstem]])
- [[pica]] may arise from V3 in an extradural location (rare)
- Key landmark for [[far-lateral-approach]] — see [[surgical-approaches-overview]]
- Related to [[lower-cranial-nerves|CN XI]] (spinal portion ascends through the foramen magnum behind V4)
