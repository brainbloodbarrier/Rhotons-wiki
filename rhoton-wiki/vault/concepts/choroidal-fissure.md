# Choroidal Fissure

The choroidal fissure is the cleft between the [[fornix]] (hippocampal lip) and the [[thalamus]] (thalamic lip) through which the [[choroid-plexus]] invaginates into the [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|lateral ventricle]]. It extends as a C-shaped arc from the [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|foramen of Monro]] to the [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|inferior choroidal point]] at the temporal horn tip. Opening this natural cleavage plane — the **transchoroidal approach** — provides wide access to the third ventricle, [[ambient-cistern]], and medial temporal structures while preserving the [[fornix]] and [[internal-capsule]].

## Anatomy

### Course
The fissure follows the C-shaped curve of the [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|lateral ventricle]]:
1. **Body segment** — between the body of the [[fornix]] (floor) and the superior surface of the [[thalamus]] (roof of third ventricle)
2. **Atrial segment** — at the trigone, between the crus of the [[fornix]] and the [[thalamus|pulvinar]]
3. **Temporal segment** — between the [[hippocampus|fimbria of the hippocampus]] and the thalamic peduncle, along the floor of the temporal horn

### Two Lips
- **Forniceal (hippocampal) lip** — formed by the [[fornix]] body/crus superiorly and the [[hippocampus|fimbria]] in the temporal horn
- **Thalamic lip** — formed by the [[thalamus|tenia thalami]] superiorly and the stria terminalis/thalamic peduncle in the temporal horn

### Choroidal Points
- **Anterior choroidal point** — where the [[anterior-choroidal-artery]] enters the temporal horn through the fissure; located at the inferior choroidal point (temporal horn tip)
- **Posterior choroidal point** — where the [[posterior-choroidal-arteries|lateral posterior choroidal artery]] enters at the atrium

## Contents of the Fissure

### Arteries
- [[anterior-choroidal-artery]] — enters at the inferior choroidal point, supplies temporal horn [[choroid-plexus]]
- [[posterior-choroidal-arteries|Lateral posterior choroidal artery]] — enters at atrium, supplies atrial/body [[choroid-plexus]]
- [[posterior-choroidal-arteries|Medial posterior choroidal artery]] — courses in the [[velum-interpositum]] above the body segment

### Veins
- [[internal-cerebral-veins]] — course in the velum interpositum (body segment)
- Inferior ventricular vein — drains along the temporal segment into the [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal]]
- Atrial veins — drain into the [[internal-cerebral-veins]] or [[vein-of-galen]]

### Neural Structures
- [[choroid-plexus]] — attached along both lips by the tenia
- Tela choroidea — the double arachnoid layer carrying the vessels between the lips

## Surgical Significance

### Transchoroidal Approach
- **The single most important natural corridor to the third ventricle**
- Opened by dividing the tenia along the forniceal lip (preserves [[fornix]] fibers) or the thalamic lip
- **Forniceal-side opening** is safer — the [[thalamus]] side risks the [[thalamoperforating-arteries|thalamoperforating]] and [[internal-cerebral-veins|thalamostriate]] veins
- Provides access to: [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|third ventricle]], [[hypothalamus]], [[pineal-region]], velum interpositum

### Temporal Horn Access
- Opening the temporal segment exposes the [[hippocampus]], ambient cistern, and mesial temporal structures
- The [[anterior-choroidal-artery]] must be identified and preserved at the inferior choroidal point
- Used in selective [[hippocampus|amygdalohippocampectomy]] and temporal horn tumor surgery

### Critical Vascular Risks
- [[anterior-choroidal-artery]] injury → hemiplegia, hemianopia, hemianesthesia (AChA syndrome)
- [[posterior-choroidal-arteries]] injury → visual field deficits, thalamic infarction
- [[internal-cerebral-veins]] injury → bilateral thalamic venous infarction (devastating)
- The [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal]] courses just outside the temporal segment

### Approach-Specific Applications

| Approach | Fissure segment | Target |
|---|---|---|
| [[interhemispheric-approach]] transchoroidal | Body | [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|Third ventricle]] tumors |
| Transcortical-transventricular | Body/atrium | Colloid cysts, [[pineal-region]] |
| [[subtemporal-approach]] transtentorial | Temporal | Ambient cistern, [[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] |
| Selective amygdalohippocampectomy | Temporal | [[hippocampus]], mesial temporal |
