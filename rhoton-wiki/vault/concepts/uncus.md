# Uncus

The uncus is the hook-shaped medial projection of the [[cerebral-lobes|temporal lobe]] that overhangs the [[tentorial-incisura|tentorial edge]] and forms the medial wall of the [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|temporal horn]]. It contains the [[hippocampus|amygdala]] anteriorly and the hippocampal head posteriorly, and is the structure that herniates through the [[tentorial-incisura]] in uncal herniation — the most common and clinically urgent form of [[herniation-syndromes|transtentorial herniation]].

## Anatomy

### Segments
1. **Anterior segment (semilunar gyrus)** — overlies the [[hippocampus|amygdala]]; cortical surface of the uncus
2. **Posterior segment (uncinate gyrus/intralimbic gyrus)** — overlies the hippocampal head; continuous with the [[hippocampus|dentate gyrus]] and [[hippocampus|parahippocampal gyrus]]
3. **Apex** — the most medial point; overhangs the [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] as it crosses the [[tentorial-incisura]]

### Uncal Notch (Uncal Sulcus)
- Groove on the medial uncal surface that separates the anterior and posterior segments
- Marks the boundary between amygdala (anterior) and hippocampal head (posterior)
- The [[anterior-choroidal-artery]] courses along or just above this notch

### Internal Structure
- **Amygdala** — fills the anterior uncus; connects to the [[basal-ganglia|substantia innominata]] and [[hypothalamus]] via stria terminalis
- **Hippocampal head** — fills the posterior uncus; characterized by the digitationes hippocampi (hippocampal digitations)
- The two structures are partially separated by the uncal recess of the [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|temporal horn]]

## Relationships

### Superior
- [[tentorial-incisura|Tentorial edge]] — the uncus lies just above and medial to the free edge
- [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] — crosses the incisura between the [[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] and [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]], directly below the uncal apex

### Medial
- **Crural cistern** — lies between the uncus and the [[brainstem|cerebral peduncle]]
- [[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] P2 segment — courses in the crural and ambient [[basal-cisterns|cisterns]] along the uncal surface
- [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal]] — passes along the medial uncal surface posteriorly

### Lateral
- [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|Temporal horn]] — the uncus forms the medial wall; the [[hippocampus|amygdala]] and hippocampal head are exposed by opening the temporal horn
- [[choroidal-fissure]] — begins at the inferior choroidal point behind the uncal posterior segment

### Inferior
- [[tentorial-incisura|Tentorium cerebelli]] — the uncus overhangs the tentorial edge
- [[oculomotor-nerve]] — lies in the cistern below the uncus

### Vascular
- [[anterior-choroidal-artery]] — courses along the uncal sulcus before entering the [[choroidal-fissure]]
- [[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] inferior temporal branches — supply the lateral uncal surface
- [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]] bifurcation — lies just anterior and lateral to the uncus

## Uncal Herniation

### Mechanism
- Expanding supratentorial mass → mesial temporal lobe displaced medially → uncus herniates over [[tentorial-incisura|tentorial edge]]
- The uncal apex compresses the [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] against the [[anterior-clinoid-process|petroclinoid ligament]]
- Progressive herniation compresses the [[brainstem|cerebral peduncle]] (ipsilateral or contralateral Kernohan's notch)

### Clinical Progression
1. **Ipsilateral pupil dilation** — [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] compression (parasympathetic fibers on nerve surface)
2. **Contralateral hemiparesis** — [[brainstem|cerebral peduncle]] compression
3. **Ipsilateral hemiparesis** (Kernohan's notch) — contralateral peduncle compressed against opposite tentorial edge (false localizing sign)
4. **[[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] infarction** — PCA trapped against tentorial edge → occipital infarct, homonymous hemianopia
5. **Duret hemorrhage** — [[brainstem]] perforator stretch/tear → pontine hemorrhage (irreversible)

### Surgical Response
- Emergency temporal decompression or hemicraniectomy
- The [[pterional-approach]] or decompressive craniectomy exposes the temporal tip/uncus
- [[subtemporal-approach]] for direct access to the medial uncal surface

## Surgical Significance

### Amygdalohippocampectomy
- The uncus is resected in selective amygdalohippocampectomy for mesial temporal epilepsy
- The [[anterior-choroidal-artery]] along the uncal sulcus must be preserved
- The [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] lies just medial — excessive medial retraction risks CN III palsy

### Temporal Lobe Approaches
- [[sylvian-fissure|Transsylvian]] amygdalohippocampectomy approaches the uncus from the lateral side
- [[subtemporal-approach]] accesses the uncal surface from below
- The uncus is a landmark for the [[optic-tract]] (courses just above the uncal apex)
