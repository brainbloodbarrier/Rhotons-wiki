# Prepontine Cistern

The prepontine cistern is the midline CSF space anterior to the [[brainstem|pons]], posterior to the [[clivus]], and inferior to the [[interpeduncular-cistern]]. It contains the [[basilar-artery]] trunk, pontine perforating arteries, and the [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]] as it ascends to [[cavernous-sinus|Dorello's canal]]. The [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach]] accesses the prepontine cistern through the [[clivus]] for clival tumors and basilar trunk pathology. Opening the [[liliequist-membrane]] connects prepontine to [[interpeduncular-cistern|interpeduncular]] cisterns.

## Boundaries
- **Anterior** — [[clivus]] (basisphenoid above, basiocciput below)
- **Posterior** — ventral surface of the [[brainstem|pons]]
- **Superior** — [[liliequist-membrane]] (mesencephalic leaf) → [[interpeduncular-cistern]]
- **Inferior** — continuous with the premedullary cistern at the pontomedullary junction
- **Lateral** — [[cerebellopontine-cistern]] on each side

## Contents
- **[[basilar-artery]]** — trunk courses vertically through the full length
- **Pontine perforators** — short circumferential and paramedian branches supplying the pons (NO collaterals)
- **[[abducens-nerve|CN VI]]** — exits pontomedullary junction, ascends through cistern to [[petrous-apex|Dorello's canal]]
- **[[anterior-spinal-artery]]** — visible at the inferior extent
- **[[aica]]** origin — arises from lower basilar within or just below the cistern

## Surgical Significance
- **[[endoscopic-endonasal-approach]]** — transclival route accesses the prepontine cistern directly for [[clivus|clival chordomas]], petroclival meningiomas
- **[[retrosigmoid-approach]]** — lateral access to the prepontine cistern for [[trigeminal-nerve|CN V]] and [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]] pathology
- **Pontine perforators are inviolable** — injury causes devastating pontine stroke
- **[[liliequist-membrane]]** must be opened to connect prepontine cistern to [[interpeduncular-cistern]] for CSF drainage and basilar apex access
