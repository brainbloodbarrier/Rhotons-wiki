# Chiasmatic Cistern

The chiasmatic cistern (suprasellar cistern) is the CSF space surrounding the [[optic-chiasm]] above the [[sellar-region|sella turcica]]. It is the crossroads of anterior skull base surgery — the [[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]] A1 segments, [[anterior-communicating-artery|ACoA]] complex, [[lamina-terminalis]], [[pituitary-gland|pituitary stalk]], and [[superior-hypophyseal-artery|superior hypophyseal arteries]] all converge here. Opening the [[lamina-terminalis]] from this cistern drains the [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|third ventricle]] and provides a corridor to its interior.

## Boundaries
- **Superior** — [[optic-chiasm]], [[lamina-terminalis]], [[anterior-cerebral-artery|A1]] segments
- **Inferior** — [[sellar-region|diaphragma sellae]], [[pituitary-gland|pituitary stalk]]
- **Anterior** — planum sphenoidale, [[olfactory-nerve|olfactory tracts]]
- **Posterior** — [[interpeduncular-cistern]] (separated by [[liliequist-membrane]] diencephalic leaf)
- **Lateral** — [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]] supraclinoid segments, carotid cisterns

## Contents
- **[[optic-chiasm]]** — central landmark; crossing fibers produce bitemporal hemianopia when compressed
- **[[optic-nerve|CN II]]** — pre-chiasmatic segments enter from the [[optic-canal|optic canals]]
- **[[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]] A1 segments** — course above the chiasm to the [[anterior-communicating-artery|ACoA]]
- **[[anterior-communicating-artery|ACoA]] complex** — most common aneurysm site; sits above the chiasm
- **[[recurrent-artery-of-heubner]]** — arises from A1-A2 junction, courses back along A1
- **[[superior-hypophyseal-artery]]** — perforators to stalk, chiasm undersurface
- **[[lamina-terminalis]]** — anterior wall of [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|third ventricle]]
- **[[pituitary-gland|Pituitary stalk]]** — descends from [[hypothalamus]] through the cistern

## Surgical Significance

### Pterional Approach
- [[pterional-approach]] provides direct access after splitting the [[sylvian-fissure]]
- The opticocarotid triangle (between [[optic-nerve|CN II]] and [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]]) is the first window into the cistern
- [[anterior-communicating-artery|ACoA]] aneurysm clipping: dissect A1 → ACoA → contralateral A1; preserve [[recurrent-artery-of-heubner|Heubner]] and [[perforating-arteries|hypothalamic perforators]]

### Endoscopic Endonasal
- [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach]] reaches the chiasmatic cistern through the [[transsphenoidal-approach|transsphenoidal]] corridor
- Craniopharyngiomas, pituitary macroadenomas with suprasellar extension
- The [[superior-hypophyseal-artery]] perforators are at risk during stalk manipulation

### Lamina Terminalis Opening
- Opening the [[lamina-terminalis]] from the chiasmatic cistern drains CSF from the [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|third ventricle]]
- Reduces need for external ventricular drain in subarachnoid hemorrhage
- Standard step during [[anterior-communicating-artery|ACoA]] aneurysm surgery
