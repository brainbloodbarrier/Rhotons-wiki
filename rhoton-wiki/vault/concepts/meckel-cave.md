# Meckel Cave

**Meckel cave** (cavum trigeminale) is a dural pouch in the posteromedial floor of the middle cranial fossa that transmits the [[trigeminal-nerve]] from the [[posterior-fossa]] into the middle fossa and houses the Gasserian (trigeminal) ganglion. It is defined by a double layer of dura that herniates posteriorly from the middle-fossa dura through the porus trigeminus, creating an arachnoid-lined cistern continuous with the [[prepontine-cistern]]. Understanding Meckel cave is foundational for trigeminal neuralgia procedures, middle-fossa skull-base approaches, and schwannoma surgery.

## Boundaries

| Boundary | Structure |
|----------|-----------|
| **Posterior** | **Porus trigeminus** — dural opening at the petrous apex through which CN V enters |
| **Anterior** | Posterior wall of the [[cavernous-sinus]]; V1, V2, V3 emerge from the ganglion here |
| **Medial** | [[clivus]], dorsum sellae, and the posterior clinoid process |
| **Lateral** | Middle-fossa dura overlying the [[petrous-apex|petrous apex]] |
| **Superior** | Tentorial edge and the attachment of the [[tentorial-incisura]] dura |
| **Inferior** | Petrous ridge and the superior petrosal sinus |
| **Floor** | Thin dural layer overlying the [[petrous-apex|petrous apex]] — the target of anterior petrosectomy |

## Contents

1. **Trigeminal (Gasserian) ganglion** — the cell bodies of the general somatic afferent fibers of CN V.
2. **Proximal V1, V2, V3** — the ophthalmic, maxillary, and mandibular divisions emerge from the anterior pole of the ganglion.
3. **Cisternal cerebrospinal fluid** — Meckel cave is an arachnoid-lined extension of the [[basal-cisterns]] and freely communicates with the [[prepontine-cistern]] — this allows retrograde contrast filling on cisternography.
4. **Dural venous plexus** — surrounding the ganglion, connecting the [[cavernous-sinus]] with the superior and inferior petrosal sinuses.

## Surgical Relevance

- **Percutaneous trigeminal procedures** — glycerol injection, balloon compression, and radiofrequency rhizotomy for [[trigeminal-nerve|trigeminal]] neuralgia all access the ganglion via the foramen ovale, advancing the cannula into the CSF of Meckel cave.
- **[[anterior-petrosectomy|Anterior petrosectomy (Kawase)]]** — the floor of Meckel cave overlies the petrous apex; the greater petrosal nerve and the trigeminal depression mark the lateral and medial boundaries of the Kawase rhomboid.
- **[[middle-fossa-approach]]** — extradural elevation of the middle-fossa dura reveals Meckel cave as a bulge medial to the greater petrosal nerve; careful dural incision allows exposure of the ganglion for schwannoma resection.
- **[[subtemporal-approach]]** — trans-tentorial exposure of the upper petroclival region passes just superior to Meckel cave.
- **Trigeminal schwannomas** — Samii's type B (middle-fossa), type C (dumbbell middle + posterior fossa), and type D (extracranial extension) all center on Meckel cave.

## Clinical Significance

- **Trigeminal neuralgia** — most cases are caused by vascular compression at the root entry zone (CN V meets pons), but some result from compression along the cisternal segment en route to Meckel cave; see [[neurovascular-compression-syndromes]].
- **Perineural tumor spread** — head and neck squamous cell carcinoma can track retrograde along V2/V3 into Meckel cave and then into the [[cavernous-sinus]] and brainstem — imaging surveillance of the cave is mandatory.
- **Meckel cave meningiomas** — arise from the dura lining the cave, present with V1–V3 numbness, and extend into the cavernous sinus.
- **MR cisternography** — high-resolution T2 sequences visualize CN V within the CSF of the cave, critical for surgical planning.

## Relations

- Dural continuation of the [[posterior-fossa]] dura through the porus trigeminus
- Immediately posterolateral to the [[cavernous-sinus]] and medial to the [[petrous-apex]]
- Inferior to the [[tentorial-incisura]]
- CSF continuous with the [[prepontine-cistern]] and [[basal-cisterns]]
