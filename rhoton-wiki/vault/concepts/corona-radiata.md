# Corona Radiata

The corona radiata is the fan-shaped expanse of [[white-matter-tracts|projection fibers]] radiating between the [[cerebral-lobes|cerebral cortex]] and the [[internal-capsule]]. All corticofugal (descending motor) and corticopetal (ascending sensory) fibers pass through this structure as they converge into the compact [[internal-capsule]] below. The corona radiata is a watershed zone between the [[middle-cerebral-artery|MCA]] and [[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]] territories, making it vulnerable to hemodynamic infarction.

## Anatomy

### Fiber Organization
- **Motor fibers** — descend from the precentral gyrus ([[cerebral-lobes|frontal lobe]]) through the corona radiata → [[internal-capsule|posterior limb]] → [[brainstem|cerebral peduncle]] → corticospinal tract
- **Sensory fibers** — ascend from the [[thalamus]] through the corona radiata → postcentral gyrus ([[cerebral-lobes|parietal lobe]])
- **[[optic-radiation]]** — courses through the posterior corona radiata from the [[thalamus|lateral geniculate body]] to the [[cerebral-lobes|occipital cortex]]
- **Frontopontine fibers** — descend from frontal lobe → [[internal-capsule|anterior limb]] → pons

### Relationships
- **Superior** — continuous with subcortical [[white-matter-tracts|U-fibers]] and cortical gray matter
- **Inferior** — converges into the [[internal-capsule]]
- **Medial** — [[corpus-callosum]] commissural fibers intersect the corona radiata
- **Lateral** — [[insula|insular cortex]] and extreme capsule lie deep to the opercula

### Vascular Supply
- **[[middle-cerebral-artery|MCA]]** — lateral corona radiata (convexity territory)
- **[[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]]** — medial corona radiata (parasagittal territory)
- **Watershed zone** — the boundary between MCA and ACA supply is within the corona radiata; hemodynamic infarction (e.g., cardiac arrest, carotid stenosis) preferentially affects this zone

## Surgical Significance
- **Transcortical approaches** to the [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|lateral ventricle]] traverse the corona radiata
- **[[insula|Insular gliomas]]** — the corona radiata is the superior resection boundary; subcortical stimulation identifies the corticospinal tract
- **Stereotactic biopsy** — the corona radiata is traversed during needle biopsy of deep lesions
- **Watershed infarction** — hemodynamic stroke produces characteristic "rosary bead" pattern in the corona radiata on diffusion MRI
