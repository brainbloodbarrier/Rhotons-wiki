# Hypothalamus

The hypothalamus is a small but functionally critical region forming the floor and inferior lateral walls of the [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|third ventricle]]. It controls neuroendocrine function, autonomic regulation, circadian rhythms, and thermoregulation. Surgically, it is intimately related to the [[pituitary-gland]], [[optic-chiasm]], [[lamina-terminalis]], and the perforating arteries of the anterior and posterior circulation.

## Anatomical Boundaries

| Boundary | Structure |
|---|---|
| **Superior** | Hypothalamic sulcus (separates hypothalamus from thalamus on the third ventricle wall) |
| **Anterior** | [[lamina-terminalis]], [[optic-chiasm]] |
| **Posterior** | Mammillary bodies, posterior perforated substance |
| **Inferior** | Tuber cinereum, median eminence, [[pituitary-gland|pituitary stalk]] (infundibulum) |
| **Lateral** | Subthalamus, internal capsule, [[optic-nerve|optic tracts]] |
| **Medial** | Third ventricle cavity |

## Key Structures

### Tuber Cinereum and Median Eminence
The tuber cinereum is the gray matter elevation on the ventral hypothalamic surface between the [[optic-chiasm]] and the mammillary bodies. The median eminence is its central portion, where hypothalamic releasing hormones enter the portal capillary system to control the [[pituitary-gland|anterior pituitary]].

### Infundibulum (Pituitary Stalk)
The infundibulum descends from the tuber cinereum to the [[pituitary-gland]], carrying the hypothalamo-hypophyseal tract (axons to the posterior pituitary) and the portal vessels (to the anterior pituitary). The [[superior-hypophyseal-artery|circuminfundibular anastomosis]] surrounds it.

### Mammillary Bodies
Paired eminences on the posterior hypothalamic surface, at the posterior edge of the interpeduncular cistern. They receive input from the hippocampus via the fornix and project to the anterior thalamic nuclei (mammillothalamic tract) — part of the Papez circuit for memory.

## Vascular Supply

The hypothalamus receives a rich but delicate perforating supply:

| Artery | Territory |
|---|---|
| [[superior-hypophyseal-artery]] | Infundibulum, tuber cinereum, median eminence, inferior optic chiasm |
| [[posterior-communicating-artery]] (premamillary artery) | Anterior and ventrolateral hypothalamus, mamillary bodies |
| [[thalamoperforating-arteries]] | Posterior hypothalamus (from P1/basilar apex) |
| [[anterior-communicating-artery]] perforators | Anterior hypothalamus via [[lamina-terminalis]] region |
| [[anterior-choroidal-artery]] | Lateral hypothalamus (minor contribution) |

These perforators are **end-arteries** — their injury produces irreversible hypothalamic dysfunction.

## Surgical Significance

### Craniopharyngioma Surgery
Craniopharyngiomas arise from Rathke's pouch remnants at the infundibulum/tuber cinereum. They are the most common tumors directly involving the hypothalamus. Surgery risks:
- **Hypothalamic obesity** — damage to ventromedial nucleus (satiety center)
- **Diabetes insipidus** — disruption of supraoptic/paraventricular nuclei or stalk
- **Memory deficits** — mamillary body injury
- **Endocrine failure** — portal vessel disruption

### ACoA Aneurysm Surgery
[[anterior-communicating-artery|ACoA]] perforators supply the anterior hypothalamus through the [[lamina-terminalis]] region. Their injury during [[aneurysm-surgery|ACoA aneurysm clipping]] can cause memory deficits and personality changes.

### Endoscopic Third Ventriculostomy
ETV fenestrates the floor of the third ventricle (tuber cinereum) to bypass aqueductal obstruction. The fenestration is made just anterior to the mammillary bodies, avoiding the basilar apex behind the [[liliequist-membrane]].

### Approach Considerations

| Approach | Hypothalamic Access |
|---|---|
| [[pterional-approach]] | Via carotid-chiasmatic corridor; inferior hypothalamic surface |
| [[interhemispheric-approach]] | Via [[lamina-terminalis]] opening; anterior hypothalamus |
| [[transsphenoidal-approach]] / [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach]] | Via sellar floor; infundibulum and tuber cinereum |
| Transcallosal-transforaminal | Via foramen of Monro; lateral hypothalamic wall |

## Connections

- Forms floor of: [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|third ventricle]]
- Anterior wall: [[lamina-terminalis]]
- Connected to: [[pituitary-gland]] via infundibulum (stalk)
- Above: [[optic-chiasm]], [[anterior-communicating-artery]]
- Below: tuber cinereum, interpeduncular cistern, [[liliequist-membrane]]
- Perforators from: [[superior-hypophyseal-artery]], [[posterior-communicating-artery]], [[anterior-communicating-artery]], [[thalamoperforating-arteries]]
- Memory circuit: mammillary bodies → [[thalamus]] (mammillothalamic tract) → [[hippocampus]] (via fornix)
