# Vidian Canal

The Vidian canal (pterygoid canal) is a bony channel through the base of the [[sphenoid-bone|pterygoid process]] connecting the [[pterygopalatine-fossa]] anteriorly to the foramen lacerum at the petrous [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]] posteriorly. It transmits the Vidian nerve (nerve of the pterygoid canal) and Vidian artery (artery of the pterygoid canal, a branch of the [[maxillary-artery]]). In [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach|endoscopic endonasal surgery]], the Vidian canal is the most reliable landmark for identifying the petrous [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]] during transpterygoid approaches.

## Anatomy

### Course
- **Anterior opening** — in the posterior wall of the [[pterygopalatine-fossa]]
- **Course** — runs posterolaterally through the [[sphenoid-bone|pterygoid process]] base
- **Posterior opening** — at foramen lacerum, where the petrous [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]] crosses the cartilage-filled foramen

### Contents
- **Vidian nerve** — formed by union of the [[greater-petrosal-nerve|GSPN]] (parasympathetic) and deep petrosal nerve (sympathetic); carries lacrimation and nasal secretion fibers to the [[pterygopalatine-fossa|pterygopalatine ganglion]]
- **Vidian artery** — branch of the [[maxillary-artery]] (third part); anastomoses with the petrous [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]] branches posteriorly

### Relationship to ICA
- The Vidian canal points directly to the **horizontal petrous ICA**
- The canal floor lies 1-2 mm above the [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]] in the petrous bone
- Following the Vidian canal posteriorly is the **safest method** to identify the petrous ICA during [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach|endoscopic]] transpterygoid approaches

## Surgical Significance

### Endoscopic Endonasal Surgery
- **Transpterygoid approach** — drilling the pterygoid process exposes the Vidian canal; following it posteriorly leads to the petrous [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]]
- [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach|Type III/IV expanded endonasal]] approaches to the [[petrous-apex]], [[meckels-cave]], and [[infratemporal-fossa]] require Vidian canal identification
- The Vidian nerve is sacrificed during transpterygoid drilling → ipsilateral dry eye (loss of lacrimation)

### Relationship to GSPN
- The [[greater-petrosal-nerve|GSPN]] joins the deep petrosal nerve at the foramen lacerum to form the Vidian nerve
- The GSPN can be followed from the [[greater-petrosal-nerve|geniculate ganglion]] to the Vidian canal — this is the [[anterior-petrosectomy|Kawase triangle]] dissection pathway

### Juvenile Nasopharyngeal Angiofibroma
- Angiofibromas often extend into or through the Vidian canal
- [[maxillary-artery]] embolization targets the Vidian artery among other feeders
- Preoperative CT defines tumor extension relative to the canal
