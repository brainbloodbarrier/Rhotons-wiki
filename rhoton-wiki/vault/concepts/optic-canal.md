# Optic Canal

The optic canal is a bony channel through the lesser wing of the [[sphenoid-bone]] that transmits the [[optic-nerve]] and [[ophthalmic-artery]] from the middle cranial fossa to the [[orbit]]. It is separated from the superior orbital fissure laterally by the optic strut, a bridge of bone connecting the [[anterior-clinoid-process]] to the sphenoid body.

## Anatomy

### Dimensions
- **Length**: 8–10 mm
- **Diameter**: ~5 mm (oval cross-section, wider horizontally)

### Boundaries
| Wall | Formed By |
|---|---|
| **Superior (roof)** | Lesser wing of [[sphenoid-bone]], anterior root of [[anterior-clinoid-process]] |
| **Inferior (floor)** | Body of [[sphenoid-bone]], roof of sphenoid sinus |
| **Medial** | Body of [[sphenoid-bone]] (lateral wall of [[paranasal-sinuses\|sphenoid sinus]]) |
| **Lateral** | Optic strut (separates optic canal from superior orbital fissure) |

### Openings
- **Intracranial (posterior)**: opens into the middle cranial fossa, medial to the [[anterior-clinoid-process]]
- **Orbital (anterior)**: opens into the orbital apex, within the annulus of Zinn ([[extraocular-muscles]])

## Contents

| Structure | Position | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [[optic-nerve]] | Central | Surrounded by dural and arachnoid sheaths; CSF extends into the canal |
| [[ophthalmic-artery]] | Inferolateral, then shifts to inferior | Courses within the dural sheath of the optic nerve; enters orbit below the nerve |
| Falciform process (dural fold) | Roof, intracranial opening | Dural fold from [[anterior-clinoid-process]] to tuberculum sellae; covers the nerve at the canal entrance |

## Relationship to Optic Strut

The **optic strut** is the bony bridge connecting the inferior aspect of the [[anterior-clinoid-process]] to the sphenoid body. It separates:

- **Medially**: optic canal
- **Laterally**: superior orbital fissure

Drilling the optic strut is a key step in clinoidectomy, as it connects the optic canal exposure to the superior orbital fissure and allows mobilization of the [[optic-nerve]].

## Sphenoid Sinus Relationship

The medial wall of the optic canal forms a prominence in the lateral wall of the [[paranasal-sinuses|sphenoid sinus]] — the **optic prominence**. In well-pneumatized sphenoid sinuses, the bone between the sinus and the optic nerve may be extremely thin or even dehiscent, creating risk during endonasal surgery and providing a window for endoscopic optic nerve decompression.

The **opticocarotid recess** is the lateral pocket of the sphenoid sinus between the optic prominence above and the carotid prominence below — a landmark for [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach]] surgery.

## Surgical Significance

### Optic Nerve Decompression
Unroofing the optic canal by drilling the superior wall is performed for:
- **Traumatic optic neuropathy** — canal fracture compresses the nerve
- **Meningiomas** — tumor encasing the nerve within the canal
- **Fibrous dysplasia** — bony overgrowth narrowing the canal

### Clinoidectomy
[[Anterior-clinoid-process|Clinoidectomy]] includes drilling the optic strut and unroofing the optic canal to:
- Mobilize the [[optic-nerve]] for paraclinoid aneurysm surgery
- Expose the clinoid segment of the [[internal-carotid-artery]]
- Divide the falciform process

### Approach Context

| Approach | Optic Canal Access |
|---|---|
| [[pterional-approach]] | Direct view of intracranial opening; canal unroofing with drill |
| [[orbitozygomatic-approach]] | Enhanced angle for canal decompression |
| [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach]] | Medial canal decompression through sphenoid sinus (optic prominence) |
| Supraorbital approach | Limited canal access from above |

### Risks
- [[ophthalmic-artery]] injury during canal drilling
- CSF leak (subarachnoid space extends into canal)
- Thermal injury to [[optic-nerve]] from drilling

## Connections

- Through: lesser wing of [[sphenoid-bone]]
- Transmits: [[optic-nerve]], [[ophthalmic-artery]]
- Separated from superior orbital fissure by: optic strut → [[anterior-clinoid-process]]
- Covered by: falciform process
- Medial wall: [[paranasal-sinuses|sphenoid sinus]] (optic prominence)
- Opens into: [[orbit]] (annulus of Zinn), middle cranial fossa
- Accessed via: [[pterional-approach]], [[orbitozygomatic-approach]], [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach]]
