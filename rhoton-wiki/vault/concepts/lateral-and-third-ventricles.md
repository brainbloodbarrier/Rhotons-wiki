# Lateral and Third Ventricles

The lateral and third ventricles are deep CSF-filled cavities at the center of the [[cerebrum]]. Their walls contain critical motor, sensory, and visual pathways, as well as vital autonomic and endocrine centers. Surgical approaches are challenging due to deep position, complete neural encasement, and narrow communicating foramina.

## Lateral Ventricle

Each lateral ventricle is a C-shaped cavity wrapping around the [[thalamus]] with five parts:

- **Frontal horn** — anterior to foramen of Monro; deep to inferior frontal gyrus
- **Body** — above the thalamus; floor formed by thalamic superior surface
- **Atrium (trigone)** — junction of body, temporal horn, and occipital horn; deep to supramarginal gyrus
- **Temporal horn** — inferolateral to thalamus; deep to middle temporal gyrus; contains [[hippocampus]] in floor
- **Occipital horn** — posterior to thalamus; variable in size

Each part has medial and lateral walls, roof, and floor — formed predominantly by thalamus, septum pellucidum, [[corpus-callosum]], and two C-shaped structures:

### [[basal-ganglia|Caudate Nucleus]]
C-shaped cellular mass wrapping around the thalamus. Head bulges into frontal horn lateral wall, body forms part of atrial lateral wall, tail extends into temporal horn roof and is continuous with the amygdala anteriorly.

### [[fornix]]
C-shaped fiber tract (hippocampomamillary fibers) wrapping around the thalamus. Fimbria arises on hippocampal surface in temporal horn floor → becomes crus → paired crura meet under splenium → body runs forward along thalamic superomedial border → columns arch around foramen of Monro.

The body of the fornix separates the roof of the third ventricle from the floor of the lateral ventricle bodies.

## Third Ventricle

A narrow midline slit between the paired thalami. Communicates with the lateral ventricles via the **foramina of Monro** (interventricular foramina) and with the fourth ventricle via the [[cerebral-aqueduct]].

Key walls:
- **Lateral walls** — thalami
- **Roof** — fornix body, [[choroid-plexus]], tela choroidea
- **Floor** — [[hypothalamus]], tuber cinereum, mammillary bodies, infundibulum
- **Anterior wall** — [[lamina-terminalis]], anterior commissure, columns of fornix
- **Posterior wall** — pineal gland, posterior commissure, habenular commissure

## [[choroidal-fissure]]

The choroidal fissure is the cleft between the fornix and thalamus through which the choroid plexus is attached. It extends in a C-shape from the foramen of Monro through the body and temporal horn. Opening the choroidal fissure is a key surgical technique for accessing the third ventricle (transchoroidal approach) and for medial temporal disconnection during epilepsy surgery.

## Vascular Relations

### Arterial
- [[anterior-choroidal-artery]] — enters temporal horn through choroidal fissure
- Lateral [[posterior-choroidal-arteries]] — from [[posterior-cerebral-artery]], enter atrium
- Medial posterior choroidal arteries — supply choroid plexus of third ventricle

### Venous
- **Thalamostriate vein** — courses along caudate-thalamic groove → joins septal vein at venous angle (behind foramen of Monro); part of the deep [[cerebral-veins]] system
- **[[internal-cerebral-veins]]** — course in roof of third ventricle → drain into [[vein-of-galen]]
- **Venous angle** — landmark for foramen of Monro location

## Relations

- Located within the [[cerebrum]], wrapping around the thalamus
- Arterial supply: [[anterior-choroidal-artery]], [[posterior-cerebral-artery]] (choroidal branches)
- Venous drainage: deep [[cerebral-veins]] (internal cerebral veins → vein of Galen)
- Communicates with [[fourth-ventricle]] in [[posterior-fossa]] via cerebral aqueduct
- [[optic-chiasm]] and [[sellar-region]] are below the anterior third ventricle
- Choroidal fissure provides surgical corridor (transchoroidal approach)

## Surgical Approaches

| Approach | Target | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Transcortical-transventricular | Frontal horn, body lesions | Through frontal cortex |
| Transcallosal | Foramen of Monro, colloid cysts | Interhemispheric → through corpus callosum |
| Transchoroidal | Third ventricle | Open choroidal fissure between fornix and thalamus |
| Subtemporal-transtentorial | Posterior third ventricle | Below temporal lobe |
| Occipital-transtentorial | Atrium, [[pineal-region]] | Through [[cerebral-lobes|occipital lobe]] |
