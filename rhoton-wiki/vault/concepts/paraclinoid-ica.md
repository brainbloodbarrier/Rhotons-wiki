# Paraclinoid Internal Carotid Artery

The paraclinoid region of the [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]] is the segment between the roof of the [[cavernous-sinus]] and the ICA bifurcation, encompassing the clinoid, ophthalmic, and communicating segments. It is defined by two dural rings — the proximal (lower) and distal (upper) — that surround the ICA as it exits the cavernous sinus to become intradural. The [[anterior-clinoid-process]] and [[optic-canal|optic strut]] form the bony framework. This region is the site of paraclinoid aneurysms (ophthalmic, superior hypophyseal, carotid cave) and requires anterior clinoidectomy for surgical access.

## Anatomy

### Dural Rings
1. **Proximal dural ring (caroticooculomotor membrane)** — the lower ring that separates the [[cavernous-sinus]] from the clinoid space; surrounds the ICA loosely
2. **Distal dural ring** — the upper ring that forms a tight fibrous collar around the ICA at the level of the [[anterior-clinoid-process]] base; this is the true boundary between extradural and intradural ICA
3. **Clinoid space (carotid cave)** — the small extradural space between the two rings; aneurysms arising here (carotid cave aneurysms) are technically extradural despite projecting intracranially

### ICA Segments in the Paraclinoid Region
- **Clinoid segment** — between the two dural rings; within the clinoid space
- **Ophthalmic segment** — from the distal dural ring to the origin of the [[posterior-communicating-artery|PCoA]]; gives rise to the [[ophthalmic-artery]] and [[superior-hypophyseal-artery]]
- **Communicating segment** — from the PCoA origin to the ICA bifurcation; gives rise to the [[posterior-communicating-artery|PCoA]] and [[anterior-choroidal-artery]]

### Bony Framework
- **[[anterior-clinoid-process]]** — the tip of the lesser wing of the [[sphenoid-bone]]; overhangs the clinoid segment
- **Optic strut** — bony bridge between the anterior clinoid and sphenoid body; separates the [[optic-canal]] from the superior orbital fissure
- **Tuberculum sellae** — anterior wall of the sella; the [[optic-chiasm]] sits just above
- **[[cavernous-sinus]] roof** — dural layer forming the proximal ring

## Paraclinoid Aneurysms

### Classification by Wall of Origin
| Type | Wall | Direction | Key relationship |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ophthalmic | Superior | Superomedial | Compresses [[optic-nerve]] against [[optic-canal\|falciform ligament]] |
| Superior hypophyseal | Medial | Medial/inferior | Projects toward [[pituitary-gland\|pituitary stalk]], may compress [[optic-chiasm]] |
| Carotid cave | Inferomedial | Inferomedial | Arises between dural rings (partially extradural) |
| Dorsal wall (blister) | Dorsal | Superior | Fragile, fusiform; high rupture risk, difficult clipping |

### Surgical Approach
- **[[pterional-approach]]** with **anterior clinoidectomy** is the standard
- **Extradural clinoidectomy** — removes the anterior clinoid process and opens the optic strut before dural opening, exposing the clinoid space and both dural rings
- **Intradural clinoidectomy** — removes the clinoid after dural opening; higher risk of ICA injury
- The distal dural ring must be incised to mobilize the ICA for proximal control
- The [[optic-nerve]] is unroofed by opening the [[optic-canal|falciform ligament]] and optic canal roof

### Proximal Control
- The clinoid segment ICA (between the rings) provides **proximal control** for paraclinoid aneurysm clipping
- If the aneurysm is at or below the distal ring, the cervical ICA must be prepared for proximal control
- Endovascular treatment (coiling, flow diversion) has become the primary treatment for most paraclinoid aneurysms

## Surgical Significance

### Anterior Clinoidectomy
- Required for exposure of the clinoid segment and paraclinoid aneurysms
- Exposes the [[optic-nerve|CN II]] in the optic canal, [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] in the oculomotor triangle, and the ICA between the dural rings
- Risk of ICA injury if the clinoid process is pneumatized and the ICA wall is adherent

### Optic Nerve Decompression
- The [[optic-nerve]] is bounded by the [[optic-canal|falciform ligament]] superiorly and the optic strut inferolaterally
- Clinoidectomy and unroofing the optic canal decompresses CN II for optic nerve sheath meningiomas and compressive lesions
- The [[ophthalmic-artery]] enters the canal inferolateral to the nerve

### Cavernous-Clinoid Interface
- Tumors extending from the [[cavernous-sinus]] to the intradural space (e.g., meningiomas) cross the paraclinoid region
- The dural rings determine whether the tumor is resectable or requires cavernous sinus surgery
- [[cavernous-sinus-triangles|Dolenc triangle]] (clinoidal triangle) provides access to this transition zone
