# Cavernous Sinus Contents

The [[cavernous-sinus]] contains a critical concentration of neurovascular structures packed into a small dural envelope lateral to the [[sellar-region]]. Understanding the spatial arrangement of these contents is essential for surgical approaches.

## Lateral Wall (superior to inferior)

1. **[[oculomotor-nerve]] (CN III)** — enters through oculomotor triangle in roof, courses superolaterally
2. **[[trochlear-nerve]] (CN IV)** — below CN III in the lateral wall
3. **Ophthalmic nerve (CN V1)** — lower lateral wall; first division of [[trigeminal-nerve]]
4. **Maxillary nerve (CN V2)** — courses briefly in the inferior lateral wall before exiting through foramen rotundum

## Within the Sinus (medial to lateral wall)

5. **[[abducens-nerve]] (CN VI)** — unique position: NOT in the lateral wall but between V1 and the [[internal-carotid-artery]]; most vulnerable nerve during surgery
6. **[[internal-carotid-artery]] (C3 segment)** — central structure; courses through the venous space surrounded by sympathetic plexus

## Mnemonic: Lateral Wall Order

**O TOM CAT** — Oculomotor (III), Trochlear (IV), Ophthalmic (V1), Maxillary (V2) in the lateral wall; Carotid Artery and abducens (T=VI=6) medially.

## Arterial Branches Within the Sinus

From the intracavernous [[internal-carotid-artery]]:
- **Meningohypophyseal trunk** — inferior hypophyseal artery (posterior [[pituitary-gland|pituitary]]), tentorial artery (Bernasconi-Cassinari), dorsal meningeal artery
- **Inferolateral trunk** — supplies lateral wall, CN III-VI dural blood supply
- **Capsular arteries** — supply pituitary capsule

## Venous Connections

The sinus communicates with:
- Bilateral cavernous sinuses via **intercavernous sinuses** (anterior, posterior)
- **Basilar sinus** — across dorsum sellae posteriorly
- **Superior petrosal sinus** → [[transverse-sinus]]
- **Inferior petrosal sinus** → jugular bulb
- Superior and inferior ophthalmic veins ← [[orbit]]
- Superficial sylvian veins ← [[sylvian-fissure]]

## [[surgical-approaches-overview|Surgical Corridors]] to the Cavernous Sinus

| Triangle | Boundaries | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Oculomotor | Ant./post. clinoid, [[petrous-apex]] | CN III entry; roof access |
| Supratrochlear (Parkinson) | CN III, CN IV, dura | Lateral wall superior |
| Infratrochlear (lateral) | CN IV, V1, dura | Lateral wall inferior |
| Anteromedial (Dolenc) | V1, V2, ICA | Medial to V1 |
| Anterolateral | V1, V2, lateral dura | Between V1 and V2 |
| Posterolateral (Glasscock) | V2, V3, [[greater-petrosal-nerve|GSPN]] | Below V2 |
