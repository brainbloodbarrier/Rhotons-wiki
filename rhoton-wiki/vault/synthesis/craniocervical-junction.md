# Craniocervical Junction

The craniocervical junction (CVJ) is the transitional zone between the skull base and the upper cervical spine, encompassing the [[foramen-magnum]], the atlas (C1), and the axis (C2). It houses the cervicomedullary junction of the [[brainstem|medulla-spinal cord transition]], the [[vertebral-artery]] V3-V4 segments, and the upper cervical nerve roots.

## Osseous Framework

| Structure | Key Features |
|---|---|
| **Occipital bone** (basiocciput) | [[foramen-magnum]], occipital condyles, jugular tubercle |
| **Atlas (C1)** | Ring vertebra, no body; lateral masses articulate with occipital condyles (atlantooccipital joint) |
| **Axis (C2)** | Odontoid process (dens) projects superiorly through C1 ring; atlantoaxial joint allows rotation |

### Occipital Condyles
The occipital condyles flank the [[foramen-magnum]] anterolaterally. They articulate with the C1 lateral masses and are:
- The **lateral limits** of the [[far-lateral-approach]] (partial condylectomy extends ventral exposure)
- Drilled in the **transcondylar approach** (posterior third can be removed without entering the hypoglossal canal)
- The hypoglossal canal passes through/above the condyle

## Ligaments

| Ligament | Connects | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Tectorial membrane** | Posterior body of C2 → anterior foramen magnum (clivus) | Continuation of posterior longitudinal ligament; deepest layer |
| **Cruciate (cruciform) ligament** | Transverse ligament of atlas + vertical bands | Holds odontoid against anterior C1 arch |
| **Alar ligaments** | Odontoid apex → medial occipital condyles | Limit rotation; check ligaments |
| **Apical ligament** | Odontoid tip → anterior foramen magnum | Vestigial; minor stabilizer |
| **Anterior atlantooccipital membrane** | Anterior C1 arch → anterior foramen magnum | Continuation of anterior longitudinal ligament |
| **Posterior atlantooccipital membrane** | Posterior C1 arch → posterior foramen magnum | Pierced by [[vertebral-artery]] V3 segment |
| **[[dentate-ligament]]** | Spinal cord (pia) → dura laterally | Divides anterior/posterior compartments; surgical landmark |

## Neurovascular Contents

### Vertebral Artery
The [[vertebral-artery]] V3 segment courses through the transverse foramen of C1, turns medially behind the atlantooccipital joint on the upper surface of the posterior C1 arch, then pierces the posterior atlantooccipital membrane and dura to become the V4 (intradural) segment.

- The V3 segment courses within the [[suboccipital-triangle]]
- The vertebral venous plexus surrounds V3
- The C1 nerve root passes between V3 and the posterior C1 arch

### Spinal Cord / Medulla
The cervicomedullary junction is the transition from medulla to spinal cord, occurring at approximately the level of the foramen magnum. The [[anterior-spinal-artery]] and [[posterior-spinal-artery]] supply this region.

### Cranial/Spinal Nerves
- **[[lower-cranial-nerves|CN IX-XII]]** — exit the brainstem and pass through the [[jugular-foramen]] (IX-XI) and hypoglossal canal (XII)
- **C1 nerve root** — exits between the vertebral artery and the posterior C1 arch
- **C2 nerve root** — gives rise to the greater occipital nerve (dorsal ramus)

## Surgical Approaches

| Approach | Access | Indication |
|---|---|---|
| [[far-lateral-approach]] | Posterolateral | Ventral foramen magnum lesions, VA-PICA aneurysms |
| Transcondylar | Extension of far-lateral | Greater ventral exposure; partial condylectomy |
| Posterior midline (suboccipital) | Posterior | Foramen magnum meningiomas (dorsal), Chiari malformation |
| Transoral/transpalatal | Ventral | Odontoid pannus, ventral compression, [[clivus|lower clival]] lesions |
| [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach]] (transodontoid) | Ventral endonasal | Odontoid invagination, ventral CVJ compression |

## Clinical Significance

### Chiari Malformation
Cerebellar tonsillar herniation through the [[foramen-magnum]] into the cervical canal, compressing the cervicomedullary junction and obstructing CSF flow at the foramen of Magendie.

### Basilar Invagination
Upward migration of the odontoid process into the foramen magnum, compressing the ventral brainstem. Requires ventral decompression (transoral or endonasal) ± posterior fusion.

### Foramen Magnum Meningiomas
Meningiomas arising from the dura of the [[foramen-magnum]]. Classified by position (anterior, lateral, posterior) relative to the [[dentate-ligament]]. The [[far-lateral-approach]] is preferred for anterolateral tumors.

## Connections

- Osseous: [[foramen-magnum]], occipital condyles, atlas (C1), axis (C2)
- Arterial: [[vertebral-artery]] (V3-V4), [[anterior-spinal-artery]], [[posterior-spinal-artery]]
- Neural: cervicomedullary junction of [[brainstem]], [[lower-cranial-nerves]], C1-C2 roots
- Ligaments: [[dentate-ligament]], cruciate ligament, alar ligaments
- Approaches: [[far-lateral-approach]], transoral, [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach]]
- Landmark: [[suboccipital-triangle]] (V3 segment)
