# Cavernous Sinus Triangles

The surgical triangles of the [[cavernous-sinus]] are defined by the cranial nerves in the lateral wall and the bony landmarks of the middle fossa floor. These triangles provide windows for surgical access to the intracavernous [[internal-carotid-artery]], the [[sellar-region]], and the [[clivus|petroclival region]]. They are essential for planning approaches to cavernous sinus meningiomas, ICA aneurysms, and trigeminal schwannomas.

## Triangle Map

The triangles are organized from superior to inferior along the lateral wall of the [[cavernous-sinus]]:

### Clinoidal Triangle (Dolenc Triangle)
- **Boundaries**: [[optic-nerve]] (medial), [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] (inferolateral), dura between [[optic-canal|optic foramen]] and [[anterior-clinoid-process]] (anterior)
- **Contents**: [[anterior-clinoid-process]], clinoid segment of [[internal-carotid-artery]]
- **Access**: exposed by [[anterior-clinoid-process|clinoidectomy]]; provides access to the clinoid segment of the ICA
- **Key risk**: [[ophthalmic-artery]] origin, upper/lower dural rings

### Oculomotor Triangle (Supratrochlear Triangle)
- **Boundaries**: [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] (anterolateral), [[trochlear-nerve|CN IV]] (posterolateral), dura of posterior clinoid/tentorial edge (posterior)
- **Contents**: roof of cavernous sinus; porus of CN III
- **Access**: opening the roof of the cavernous sinus between CN III and IV
- **Key risk**: [[oculomotor-nerve]], meningohypophyseal trunk

### Parkinson Triangle (Infratrochlear Triangle)
- **Boundaries**: [[trochlear-nerve|CN IV]] (superior), [[trigeminal-nerve|V1 (ophthalmic nerve)]] (inferior), tentorial edge (posterior)
- **Contents**: intracavernous ICA (posterior vertical/genu segments), CN VI
- **Access**: primary window to the intracavernous ICA
- **Key risk**: [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]] crossing the lateral ICA surface

### Anterolateral Triangle (Mullan Triangle)
- **Boundaries**: [[trigeminal-nerve|V1]] (superior), [[trigeminal-nerve|V2 (maxillary nerve)]] (inferior), dura between V1 and V2
- **Contents**: intracavernous ICA (horizontal segment), [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]]
- **Access**: lateral approach to the horizontal ICA
- **Key risk**: CN VI adherent to ICA, pterygoid venous plexus

### Anteroinferior (Lateral) Triangle
- **Boundaries**: [[trigeminal-nerve|V2]] (superior), [[trigeminal-nerve|V3 (mandibular nerve)]] (inferior), line between foramen rotundum and foramen ovale
- **Contents**: floor of middle fossa lateral to cavernous sinus; [[greater-petrosal-nerve|greater superficial petrosal nerve]]
- **Access**: extradural middle fossa approach to cavernous sinus floor
- **Key risk**: [[middle-meningeal-artery]] at foramen spinosum

### Posteromedial (Kawase) Triangle
- **Boundaries**: greater superficial petrosal nerve (lateral), [[trigeminal-nerve|V3 at foramen ovale]] (anterior), arcuate eminence/petrous ridge (posterior)
- **Contents**: [[petrous-apex]] bone covering the [[internal-carotid-artery|petrous ICA]]
- **Access**: [[anterior-petrosectomy]] (Kawase approach) — drilling this triangle exposes the [[posterior-fossa]] and lateral [[clivus]]
- **Key risk**: [[internal-carotid-artery|petrous ICA]], cochlea, [[facial-nerve|geniculate ganglion]]

### Posterolateral (Glasscock) Triangle
- **Boundaries**: greater superficial petrosal nerve (medial), line from arcuate eminence to foramen spinosum (lateral), mandibular nerve/foramen ovale (anterior)
- **Contents**: petrous ICA horizontal segment
- **Access**: exposure of the petrous ICA for bypass or proximal control
- **Key risk**: tensor tympani, eustachian tube, [[middle-meningeal-artery]]

## Approach Application

| Approach | Primary Triangles Used |
|---|---|
| [[pterional-approach]] + clinoidectomy | Clinoidal, oculomotor |
| [[orbitozygomatic-approach]] | Clinoidal, oculomotor, Parkinson |
| [[subtemporal-approach]] | Parkinson, anterolateral |
| [[anterior-petrosectomy]] (Kawase) | Posteromedial (Kawase) triangle |
| [[middle-fossa-approach]] | Posterolateral (Glasscock) |
| Direct cavernous sinus approach | Parkinson (primary surgical window) |

## Lateral Wall Nerve Order

From superior to inferior within the lateral wall of the [[cavernous-sinus]] (see [[cavernous-sinus-contents]]):

1. [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]]
2. [[trochlear-nerve|CN IV]]
3. [[trigeminal-nerve|V1]]
4. [[trigeminal-nerve|V2]]

The [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]] courses **within** the sinus itself (not in the wall), adherent to the lateral surface of the intracavernous ICA.
