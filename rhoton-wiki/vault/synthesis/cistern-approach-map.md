# Cistern-Approach Map

Every neurosurgical approach reaches its target through one or more subarachnoid cisterns. The cisterns are the natural corridors of the brain — they contain CSF that buffers the brain, carry the major neurovascular structures, and provide pre-formed dissection planes. This synthesis maps each cistern to the approaches that traverse it, the structures encountered, and the pathology accessed.

## Supratentorial Cisterns

### Sylvian Cistern
- **Approaches:** [[pterional-approach]], [[orbitozygomatic-approach]]
- **Key structures:** [[middle-cerebral-artery|MCA]] M1-M3, [[sylvian-fissure|sylvian veins]], [[lenticulostriate-arteries]]
- **Targets:** MCA aneurysms, [[insula|insular tumors]], [[anterior-communicating-artery|ACoA]] complex (via medial sylvian)
- **Dissection principle:** Open the fissure proximal-to-distal (cisternal to opercular)

### Carotid Cistern
- **Approaches:** [[pterional-approach]], [[orbitozygomatic-approach]]
- **Key structures:** [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]], [[optic-nerve|CN II]], [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]], [[posterior-communicating-artery|PCoA]], [[anterior-choroidal-artery|AChA]]
- **Targets:** [[paraclinoid-ica|Paraclinoid aneurysms]], ICA bifurcation aneurysms, [[optic-chiasm|chiasmatic tumors]]
- **Dissection principle:** Open the opticocarotid triangle and carotid-oculomotor triangle

### Chiasmatic Cistern
- **Approaches:** [[pterional-approach]], [[interhemispheric-approach]], [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach]]
- **Key structures:** [[optic-chiasm]], [[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]] A1, [[lamina-terminalis]], [[superior-hypophyseal-artery]]
- **Targets:** Craniopharyngiomas, [[anterior-communicating-artery|ACoA]] aneurysms, third ventricle (via lamina terminalis)
- **Dissection principle:** Open the lamina terminalis for CSF drainage and third ventricle access

### Callosal Cistern
- **Approaches:** [[interhemispheric-approach]]
- **Key structures:** [[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]] A2-A5, [[corpus-callosum]], pericallosal arteries, [[bridging-veins|bridging veins]]
- **Targets:** [[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]] aneurysms, [[corpus-callosum|callosal tumors]], lateral ventricle (transcallosal)
- **Dissection principle:** Stay in the interhemispheric fissure; identify both pericallosal arteries before callosotomy

### Velum Interpositum
- **Approaches:** [[interhemispheric-approach]] transchoroidal, transcallosal
- **Key structures:** [[internal-cerebral-veins]], [[posterior-choroidal-arteries|MPChA]], [[fornix]], [[choroidal-fissure]]
- **Targets:** [[pineal-region]] tumors, posterior third ventricle, colloid cysts
- **Dissection principle:** Open [[choroidal-fissure]] along the forniceal lip; retract ICVs one at a time

## Incisural Cisterns

### Crural Cistern (Anterior Incisural Space)
- **Approaches:** [[pterional-approach]], [[orbitozygomatic-approach]], [[subtemporal-approach]]
- **Key structures:** [[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] P2A, [[anterior-choroidal-artery|AChA]], [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]], [[uncus]], [[brainstem|cerebral peduncle]]
- **Targets:** [[basilar-artery]] apex aneurysms, peduncular lesions, [[uncus|uncal tumors]]
- **Dissection principle:** Follow the ICA → PCoA → P1 to reach the basilar apex

### Ambient Cistern (Middle Incisural Space)
- **Approaches:** [[subtemporal-approach]], [[supracerebellar-infratentorial-approach]]
- **Key structures:** [[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] P2, [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal]], [[trochlear-nerve|CN IV]], [[thalamogeniculate-arteries]], [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]]
- **Targets:** P2 aneurysms, tentorial meningiomas, [[thalamus|lateral thalamic lesions]]
- **Dissection principle:** Divide the [[tentorium-cerebelli|tentorium]] to widen the corridor

### Quadrigeminal Cistern (Posterior Incisural Space)
- **Approaches:** [[supracerebellar-infratentorial-approach]], occipital transtentorial
- **Key structures:** [[vein-of-galen]], [[internal-cerebral-veins]], [[pineal-region|pineal gland]], [[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] P3
- **Targets:** [[pineal-region]] tumors, tectal gliomas, [[vein-of-galen|galenic malformations]]
- **Dissection principle:** Gravity-dependent position (sitting/prone); cerebellum falls from tentorium

## Infratentorial Cisterns

### Cerebellopontine Cistern
- **Approaches:** [[retrosigmoid-approach]], [[middle-fossa-approach]]
- **Key structures:** [[trigeminal-nerve|CN V]], [[facial-nerve|CN VII]], [[vestibulocochlear-nerve|CN VIII]], [[aica]], [[superior-petrosal-vein]]
- **Targets:** Vestibular schwannomas, [[cerebellopontine-cistern|CP angle]] meningiomas, [[neurovascular-compression-syndromes|MVD]]
- **Dissection principle:** Open the cistern early for CSF drainage; identify [[superior-petrosal-vein]] before dividing

### Cerebellomedullary Cistern (Cisterna Magna)
- **Approaches:** [[telovelar-approach]], [[far-lateral-approach]], midline suboccipital
- **Key structures:** [[pica]], [[vertebral-artery]], [[lower-cranial-nerves|CN IX-XII]], [[cerebellomedullary-fissure]]
- **Targets:** [[fourth-ventricle]] tumors, [[foramen-magnum]] lesions, Chiari decompression
- **Dissection principle:** Follow [[pica]] through the [[cerebellomedullary-fissure]] to reach the fourth ventricle floor

### Prepontine Cistern
- **Approaches:** [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach]], [[retrosigmoid-approach]]
- **Key structures:** [[basilar-artery]], [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]], [[liliequist-membrane]], pontine perforators
- **Targets:** [[clivus|Clival tumors]], petroclival meningiomas, endoscopic third ventriculostomy
- **Dissection principle:** Open [[liliequist-membrane]] to pass from prepontine to [[interpeduncular-cistern]]

### Interpeduncular Cistern
- **Approaches:** [[pterional-approach]], [[orbitozygomatic-approach]], [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach]]
- **Key structures:** [[basilar-artery]] apex, [[thalamoperforating-arteries]], [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]], [[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] P1
- **Targets:** [[basilar-artery]] apex aneurysms, [[hypothalamus|hypothalamic lesions]]
- **Dissection principle:** Preserve [[thalamoperforating-arteries]] behind the basilar apex at all costs
