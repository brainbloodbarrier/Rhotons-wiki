# Cranial Nerves Overview

Twelve paired cranial nerves emerge from the brain and [[brainstem]], traverse the subarachnoid cisterns ([[basal-cisterns]]), and exit through foramina of the [[cranial-base]]. Ten of the twelve pairs (CN III–XII) have segments within the [[posterior-fossa]]. Their intimate relations to major arteries, dural sinuses, and skull base structures make them central to every surgical approach.

## Brainstem Organization

Cranial nerves emerge from the [[brainstem]] in a predictable pattern:

### Midbrain
- **CN III** — ventral (interpeduncular fossa) → passes between [[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] and [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]]
- **CN IV** — dorsal (below inferior colliculus) → only CN with dorsal exit and contralateral supply

### Pons
- **CN V** — lateral mid-pons → between [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]] above and [[aica|AICA]] below
- **CN VI** — ventral pontomedullary junction → longest subarachnoid course along [[clivus]]
- **CN VII + VIII** — lateral pontomedullary junction → intimately related to [[aica|AICA]] meatal loop

### Medulla
- **CN IX, X, XI (cranial)** — postolivary sulcus → pass between [[pica|PICA]] rootlets to jugular foramen
- **CN XI (spinal)** — cervical cord → ascends through [[foramen-magnum]] behind [[vertebral-artery]]
- **CN XII** — preolivary sulcus → rootlets pass behind [[vertebral-artery]]

## Cisternal Course

Each cranial nerve traverses specific [[basal-cisterns]]:

- **[[interpeduncular-cistern]]** — CN III origin
- **[[ambient-cistern]]** — CN III, IV course with [[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] and [[superior-cerebellar-artery|SCA]]
- **[[prepontine-cistern]]** — CN VI ascending on clivus with [[basilar-artery]]
- **[[cerebellopontine-cistern]]** — CN V, VII, VIII with [[aica|AICA]]
- **Cerebellomedullary cistern** — CN IX, X, XI with [[pica|PICA]]
- **Premedullary cistern** — CN XII with [[vertebral-artery|vertebral arteries]]

## Cavernous Sinus Transit

Four cranial nerves traverse the [[cavernous-sinus]]:
- **Lateral wall** (superior to inferior): [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]], [[trochlear-nerve|CN IV]], [[trigeminal-nerve|V1]]
- **Within the sinus**: [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]] (medial to V1, lateral to [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]])

See [[cavernous-sinus-contents]] for detailed surgical triangles.

## Relations

- All CNs III–XII arise from or traverse the [[brainstem]]
- Pass through [[basal-cisterns]] to reach [[cranial-base]] foramina
- CN III–VI traverse the [[cavernous-sinus]] or its wall
- CN VII enters the [[orbit]] via superior orbital fissure (III, IV, V1, VI)
- [[posterior-fossa]] contains CN III–XII segments
- [[vertebral-artery]] and [[basilar-artery]] are the principal arterial relations for CN III–XII
- CN IX–XII concentrated at [[foramen-magnum]] and jugular foramen
- [[dural-venous-sinuses]] (superior petrosal, sigmoid) cross CN transit paths
- [[cerebellum]] is related to CN V–XII via the three neurovascular complexes
- See [[surgical-approaches-overview]] for approach-specific CN vulnerability
