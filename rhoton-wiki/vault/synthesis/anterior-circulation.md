# Anterior Circulation

The anterior (carotid) circulation supplies the frontal, parietal, and temporal lobes, the basal ganglia, and the anterior limb of the [[circle-of-willis]]. It originates from the paired [[internal-carotid-artery|internal carotid arteries]], which bifurcate into the [[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]] and [[middle-cerebral-artery|MCA]] — the two largest cerebral arteries.

## Arterial Hierarchy

```
Common carotid artery
  └── Internal carotid artery (C1–C4)
        ├── [[ophthalmic-artery]] → orbit, optic nerve
        ├── Posterior communicating artery → circle of Willis
        ├── Anterior choroidal artery → [[choroidal-fissure]], temporal horn
        ├── Anterior cerebral artery (A1–A5)
        │     ├── [[recurrent-artery-of-heubner]] → caudate head, anterior limb IC
        │     ├── ACoA complex → anterior circle of Willis
        │     ├── Callosomarginal artery → cingulate sulcus
        │     └── Pericallosal artery → [[corpus-callosum]]
        └── Middle cerebral artery (M1–M4)
              ├── [[lenticulostriate-arteries]] → basal ganglia, [[internal-capsule]]
              ├── Temporal branches → lateral [[cerebral-lobes|temporal lobe]]
              ├── Frontal branches → lateral frontal lobe
              └── Parietal branches → lateral parietal lobe
```

## Internal Carotid Artery ([[internal-carotid-artery]])

Four segments in the Bouthillier classification:
- **C1 (cervical)** — from common carotid bifurcation to carotid canal
- **C2 (petrous)** — through the [[temporal-bone|petrous temporal bone]]
- **C3 (cavernous)** — through the [[cavernous-sinus]]; gives rise to the meningohypophyseal trunk and inferolateral trunk
- **C4 (supraclinoid)** — from the [[anterior-clinoid-process]] to the bifurcation; gives rise to the ophthalmic artery, [[posterior-communicating-artery|PCoA]], and [[anterior-choroidal-artery|AChA]]

The ICA terminates by bifurcating into the ACA (A1) and MCA (M1).

## Anterior Cerebral Artery ([[anterior-cerebral-artery]])

- **A1** — from ICA bifurcation to [[anterior-communicating-artery|ACoA]]; crosses above the [[optic-chiasm]]/[[optic-nerve]]
- **A2 (infracallosal)** — ascends in the interhemispheric fissure anterior to the [[lamina-terminalis]]
- **A3 (precallosal)** — curves around the genu of the corpus callosum
- **A4–A5 (supracallosal/postcallosal)** — course above and behind the corpus callosum

**Key branches:** Recurrent artery of Heubner (largest perforator, from proximal A2), frontopolar, callosomarginal, pericallosal arteries.

**Supply territory:** Medial hemispheric surface (leg motor/sensory cortex), corpus callosum, caudate head, anterior limb of internal capsule.

## Middle Cerebral Artery ([[middle-cerebral-artery]])

- **M1 (sphenoidal)** — courses laterally in the [[sylvian-fissure]] (sphenoidal compartment)
- **M2 (insular)** — courses on the insular surface within the operculoinsular compartment
- **M3 (opercular)** — courses along the inner surface of the frontoparietal and temporal opercula
- **M4 (cortical)** — emerges from the sylvian fissure to supply the lateral hemispheric surface

**Key branches:** Lenticulostriate arteries (from M1 — critical perforators to the [[basal-ganglia|basal ganglia and internal capsule]]), temporal, frontal, and parietal cortical branches.

**Supply territory:** Lateral hemispheric surface (face and arm motor/sensory, Broca's and Wernicke's areas), [[insula]], basal ganglia (via lenticulostriates).

## Anterior Communicating Artery ([[anterior-communicating-artery]])

Connects bilateral A1 segments, completing the anterior [[circle-of-willis]]. The **most common site of intracranial aneurysms** (25–30%). Its perforating branches supply the [[hypothalamus]], anterior commissure, and columns of the [[fornix]] — injury during ACoA [[aneurysm-surgery]] causes memory deficits.

## Relations

- Originates from [[internal-carotid-artery]] bifurcation
- Connects to [[posterior-circulation]] via [[posterior-communicating-artery]] → [[circle-of-willis]]
- [[optic-nerve]] and [[optic-chiasm]] are the principal neural landmarks
- [[sylvian-fissure]] is the primary surgical corridor for MCA territory
- [[basal-cisterns]] (sylvian, carotid, chiasmatic cisterns) contain the anterior circulation
- Drains via [[cerebral-veins]] → [[dural-venous-sinuses]] (superior sagittal, sphenoparietal)
- See [[cranial-nerves-overview]] — [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] at the ICA-PCoA junction
- [[cerebrum]] — the supply territory encompasses most of the hemispheric surface
