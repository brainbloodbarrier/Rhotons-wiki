# Cerebral Lobes

The [[cerebrum]] is divided into four lobes — frontal, parietal, temporal, and occipital — plus the deep [[insula|insular lobe]] hidden within the [[sylvian-fissure]]. Each lobe has distinct functional areas, vascular supply territories, and surgical landmarks. Rhoton's surface anatomy descriptions provide the microsurgical framework for cortical mapping and approach planning.

## Frontal Lobe

The largest lobe, anterior to the central sulcus and superior to the [[sylvian-fissure]].

### Key gyri and sulci
- **Precentral gyrus** — primary motor cortex (homunculus: leg medially, face laterally)
- **Superior, middle, inferior frontal gyri** — premotor and prefrontal areas
- **Inferior frontal gyrus (pars opercularis + triangularis)** — **Broca's area** (dominant hemisphere) for speech production
- **Gyrus rectus** — medial orbital surface; related to [[olfactory-nerve|olfactory tract]] and [[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]] A1

### Vascular supply
- **Lateral surface** — [[middle-cerebral-artery|MCA]] (frontal branches: orbitofrontal, prefrontal, precentral) — see [[anterior-circulation]]
- **Medial surface** — [[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]] (callosomarginal, pericallosal branches)
- **Orbital surface** — [[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]] + [[middle-cerebral-artery|MCA]] overlap

### Surgical relevance
- [[pterional-approach]] accesses the frontal lobe via the [[sylvian-fissure]]
- [[interhemispheric-approach]] accesses the medial frontal surface
- Frontal lobe retraction in subfrontal approaches to the [[sellar-region]] and [[optic-chiasm]]
- Broca's area at risk in dominant hemisphere sylvian fissure surgery

## Temporal Lobe

Below the [[sylvian-fissure]], anterior to the occipital lobe, lateral to the [[brainstem]].

### Key gyri and sulci
- **Superior temporal gyrus** — primary auditory cortex (Heschl's gyrus), **Wernicke's area** (posterior portion, dominant hemisphere)
- **Middle temporal gyrus** — association cortex; common site for cortical incision in temporal lobectomy
- **Inferior temporal gyrus** — visual association
- **Fusiform gyrus** — inferior surface; face recognition
- **Parahippocampal gyrus and [[uncus]]** — medial surface; [[hippocampus]] lies deep to it

### Vascular supply
- **Lateral surface** — [[middle-cerebral-artery|MCA]] (temporal branches: anterior, middle, posterior temporal)
- **Inferior/medial surface** — [[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] (inferior temporal, hippocampal arteries)
- **Deep (hippocampal)** — [[anterior-choroidal-artery]], [[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] choroidal branches

### Surgical relevance
- [[subtemporal-approach]] elevates the temporal lobe for [[tentorial-incisura]] access
- Anterior temporal lobectomy for epilepsy (amygdalohippocampectomy — see [[hippocampus]])
- The uncus herniates through the [[tentorial-incisura]], compressing [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]]
- Vein of Labbé drains the temporal lobe to the [[dural-venous-sinuses|transverse sinus]] — must be preserved

## Parietal Lobe

Between the central sulcus (anterior), parieto-occipital sulcus (posterior), and [[sylvian-fissure]] (inferior).

### Key gyri and sulci
- **Postcentral gyrus** — primary somatosensory cortex (homunculus mirrors precentral gyrus)
- **Superior parietal lobule** — proprioception, spatial orientation
- **Inferior parietal lobule** — supramarginal gyrus (phonological processing) and angular gyrus (reading, calculation)
- **Intraparietal sulcus** — divides superior from inferior lobule

### Vascular supply
- **Lateral surface** — [[middle-cerebral-artery|MCA]] (central, parietal branches)
- **Medial surface** — [[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]] (posterior branches, precuneal artery)

### Surgical relevance
- Parietal cortex incision for transcortical approach to the [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|atrium]] of the lateral ventricle
- Superior parietal lobule approach preferred for atrial lesions (avoids angular gyrus → alexia)
- [[bridging-veins]] to the [[dural-venous-sinuses|superior sagittal sinus]] constrain parasagittal approaches

## Occipital Lobe

The most posterior lobe, behind the parieto-occipital sulcus.

### Key gyri and sulci
- **Calcarine sulcus** — primary visual cortex (V1) on the medial surface; upper bank = inferior visual field, lower bank = superior visual field
- **Cuneus** — between the parieto-occipital and calcarine sulci
- **Lingual gyrus** — below the calcarine sulcus

### Vascular supply
- **Medial surface** — [[posterior-cerebral-artery|PCA]] (calcarine and parieto-occipital arteries) — see [[posterior-circulation]]
- **Lateral surface** — [[middle-cerebral-artery|MCA]] (angular artery) + PCA overlap

### Surgical relevance
- Occipital transtentorial approach retracts the occipital lobe medially for [[pineal-region]] access (alternative to [[supracerebellar-infratentorial-approach]])
- Calcarine artery injury causes contralateral homonymous hemianopia
- The occipital pole overlies the [[dural-venous-sinuses|confluence of sinuses]]

## Insular Lobe

See [[insula]] — deep cortex hidden within the [[sylvian-fissure]], covered by opercula, intimately related to [[middle-cerebral-artery|MCA]] M2 segments and [[basal-ganglia|lenticulostriate arteries]].

## Functional-Vascular Correlation

| Function | Cortical area | Vascular supply | Deficit if injured |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motor (leg) | Medial precentral | [[anterior-cerebral-artery\|ACA]] | Contralateral leg weakness |
| Motor (face/arm) | Lateral precentral | [[middle-cerebral-artery\|MCA]] | Contralateral face/arm weakness |
| Speech production | Broca's (dominant IF gyrus) | [[middle-cerebral-artery\|MCA]] frontal | Expressive aphasia |
| Speech comprehension | Wernicke's (dominant STG) | [[middle-cerebral-artery\|MCA]] temporal | Receptive aphasia |
| Somatosensory | Postcentral gyrus | [[middle-cerebral-artery\|MCA]] / [[anterior-cerebral-artery\|ACA]] | Contralateral numbness |
| Vision | Calcarine cortex | [[posterior-cerebral-artery\|PCA]] | Contralateral hemianopia |
| Memory | [[hippocampus]] | [[anterior-choroidal-artery\|AChA]] / [[posterior-cerebral-artery\|PCA]] | Amnesia |

## Relations

- [[cerebrum]] = the two hemispheres composed of these four lobes + [[insula]]
- [[middle-cerebral-artery]] supplies the lateral surfaces — see [[anterior-circulation]]
- [[anterior-cerebral-artery]] supplies the medial surfaces
- [[posterior-cerebral-artery]] supplies occipital and inferior temporal — see [[posterior-circulation]]
- [[sylvian-fissure]] separates frontal from temporal lobes
- [[corpus-callosum]] connects homologous lobe areas via commissural fibers — see [[white-matter-tracts]]
- [[basal-ganglia]] and [[thalamus]] are deep to the lobes
- [[lateral-and-third-ventricles]] embedded within the lobes
- Bridging veins from all lobes drain to [[dural-venous-sinuses]]
- See [[surgical-approaches-overview]] and [[perforating-arteries]]
