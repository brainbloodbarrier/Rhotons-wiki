# Deep Venous System

The deep venous system of the brain drains the deep white and gray matter, ventricular walls, and cisternal structures through three principal channels — the [[internal-cerebral-veins]], the [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal|basal veins of Rosenthal]], and the [[vein-of-galen]] — before emptying into the straight sinus. Injury to this system during deep-seated surgery can produce catastrophic venous infarction.

## Architecture — Three Converging Channels

```
Ventricular veins ──→ Internal Cerebral Veins ──┐
                                                  ├──→ Vein of Galen ──→ Straight Sinus
Cisternal veins ───→ Basal Veins of Rosenthal ──┘
```

### [[internal-cerebral-veins]]
- **Formation**: behind the foramen of Monro (thalamostriate + superior choroidal veins → venous angle)
- **Course**: velum interpositum in roof of [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|third ventricle]]
- **Territory**: frontal horn, body, atrium walls; [[thalamus]], [[basal-ganglia|caudate]], septum, fornix
- **Termination**: unite above pineal to form [[vein-of-galen]]

### [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal]]
- **Formation**: [[anterior-perforated-substance]] (anterior cerebral + deep sylvian + olfactory veins)
- **Course**: encircles [[brainstem]] through crural → ambient → quadrigeminal cisterns
- **Territory**: basal frontal surface, medial temporal lobe, [[hippocampus]], cerebral peduncle, lateral midbrain
- **Termination**: joins [[vein-of-galen]] in quadrigeminal cistern

### [[vein-of-galen]]
- **Formation**: union of paired [[internal-cerebral-veins]] ± [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal|basal veins]] above/behind pineal
- **Course**: short (1–2 cm), quadrigeminal cistern below splenium of [[corpus-callosum]]
- **Receives**: superior vermian veins, tectal veins, posterior pericallosal veins
- **Termination**: joins inferior sagittal sinus → straight sinus

## Ventricular Veins — Medial and Lateral Groups

The ventricular veins arise from the walls of the lateral ventricles and converge on the [[choroid-plexus|choroidal fissure]]:

| Group | Route | Drains Into |
|---|---|---|
| **Lateral group** | Through thalamic side of choroidal fissure | [[internal-cerebral-veins]], [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal\|basal vein]], [[vein-of-galen]] |
| **Medial group** | Pierces fornix to enter velum interpositum | [[internal-cerebral-veins]] |

The lateral group drains the lateral wall (caudate), and the medial group drains the medial wall (septum, fornix) and opposite surfaces.

## Cisternal Veins — Three Incisural Regions

The cisternal group of deep veins drains the walls of the cisterns surrounding the [[brainstem]]:

| Incisural Region | Location | Drainage |
|---|---|---|
| **Anterior** | In front of brainstem (chiasmatic, interpeduncular cisterns) | → anterior segment of [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal\|basal vein]] |
| **Middle** | Lateral to brainstem (crural, ambient cisterns) | → middle segment of [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal\|basal vein]] |
| **Posterior** | Behind brainstem (quadrigeminal cistern) | → [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal\|basal vein]], [[internal-cerebral-veins]], or [[vein-of-galen]] |

## Surgical Obstacle Map

| Approach | Deep Venous Obstacle | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| [[supracerebellar-infratentorial-approach]] | [[vein-of-galen]], terminal basal veins, superior vermian veins | [[pineal-region]] surgery |
| [[interhemispheric-approach]] (posterior) | [[internal-cerebral-veins]] in velum interpositum | Third ventricle/pineal tumors |
| [[subtemporal-approach]] | Middle segment of [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal\|basal vein]] | Temporal lobe elevation |
| [[pterional-approach]] | Anterior segment of [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal\|basal vein]] | APS, deep sylvian veins |
| Transchoroidal | [[internal-cerebral-veins]], [[posterior-choroidal-arteries\|MPChA]] | Choroidal fissure opening |
| Transcallosal | [[internal-cerebral-veins]] | Velum interpositum entry |

## Consequences of Deep Venous Injury

The deep venous system has diffuse anastomoses that sometimes protect against unilateral injury. However, sacrifice of major deep venous trunks can produce:

- **Bilateral thalamic infarction** — coma, memory loss (ICV injury)
- **Basal ganglia hemorrhagic infarction** — hemiplegia (basal vein injury)
- **Temporal lobe venous infarction** — inferior ventricular vein sacrifice
- **Death** — complete galenic system occlusion

The variability of anastomotic channels makes it difficult to predict which patients will tolerate sacrifice. **Preservation of all deep veins is the surgical principle.**

## Relationship to Superficial Venous System

The [[bridging-veins]] form the superficial venous system, draining cortical surfaces into the [[dural-venous-sinuses]]. The two systems interconnect at:

- **[[anterior-perforated-substance]]** — where deep sylvian veins join the basal vein
- **Choroidal fissure** — where ventricular veins exit to reach cisternal channels
- **Quadrigeminal cistern** — where cortical calcarine/pericallosal veins may join the deep system

## Connections

- Channels: [[internal-cerebral-veins]], [[basal-vein-of-rosenthal]], [[vein-of-galen]]
- Drains into: straight sinus → [[dural-venous-sinuses|torcular Herophili]]
- Tributaries from: [[thalamus]], [[basal-ganglia]], [[hippocampus]], [[choroid-plexus]], [[brainstem]]
- Obstacle for: [[pineal-region]], [[tentorial-incisura]], [[lateral-and-third-ventricles|third ventricle]] surgery
- Complemented by: [[bridging-veins]] (superficial system), [[cerebral-veins]]
