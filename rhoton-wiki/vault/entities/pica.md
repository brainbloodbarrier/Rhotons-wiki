# Posterior Inferior Cerebellar Artery

The posterior inferior cerebellar artery (PICA) is the largest branch of the vertebral artery and the most variable of the cerebellar arteries. It belongs to the **lower neurovascular complex** of the [[posterior-fossa]], associated with the medulla, inferior cerebellar peduncle, cerebellomedullary fissure, suboccipital surface, and cranial nerves IX–XII.

## Course

1. **Anterior medullary segment** — arises from vertebral artery, courses along anterior medulla near CN IX–XII
2. **Lateral medullary segment** — courses around the lateral medulla, closely related to CN IX, X, XI
3. **Tonsillomedullary segment** — loops around the cerebellar tonsil in the cerebellomedullary fissure
4. **Telovelotonsillar segment** — courses in relation to the tela choroidea and inferior medullary velum (roof of fourth ventricle)
5. **Cortical segment** — distributes to the suboccipital cerebellar surface

## Supply Territory

- Suboccipital cerebellar surface (inferior vermis, tonsils, inferior hemispheres)
- Choroid plexus of fourth ventricle (via choroidal branches)
- Lateral medulla (via perforating branches)

## Surgical Relevance

- PICA aneurysms arise near vertebral artery origin — approached via [[far-lateral-approach]]
- The **telovelar approach** follows the PICA through the cerebellomedullary fissure to access the fourth ventricle floor without splitting the vermis
- PICA may compress CN IX (glossopharyngeal neuralgia) or CN X — treated by microvascular decompression
- Lateral medullary syndrome (Wallenberg) results from PICA or vertebral artery occlusion

## Relations

- Arises from [[vertebral-artery]] in [[posterior-fossa]]
- Courses near [[lower-cranial-nerves|CN IX, X, XI, XII]] at the medulla
- Supplies suboccipital cerebellar surface
- Courses through cerebellomedullary fissure (telovelar approach corridor)
- Part of the lower neurovascular complex with [[posterior-fossa-veins]]
- Origin related to [[foramen-magnum]] (may arise above or below)
- Sister cerebellar arteries: [[aica]] (middle), [[superior-cerebellar-artery]] (upper) — all branch from [[basilar-artery]] or [[vertebral-artery]]
