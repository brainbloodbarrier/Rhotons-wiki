# Maxillary Artery

The maxillary artery is the larger terminal branch of the external carotid artery, coursing through the [[infratemporal-fossa]] and [[pterygopalatine-fossa]] to supply the deep face, nasal cavity, palate, dura, and upper teeth. It is the dominant extracranial artery encountered in lateral [[cranial-base|skull base]] surgery and the source of the [[middle-meningeal-artery]], [[sphenopalatine-artery]], and the descending palatine artery. In [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach|endoscopic endonasal surgery]], the maxillary artery is a critical landmark and potential source of catastrophic hemorrhage.

## Anatomy

### Three Parts (relation to lateral pterygoid muscle)

#### First Part (Mandibular/Retromandibular)
- From the ECA bifurcation (behind the mandibular neck) to the lower border of the lateral pterygoid
- **Branches:**
  - **[[middle-meningeal-artery]]** — enters foramen spinosum; the most important dural artery
  - **Inferior alveolar artery** — enters the mandibular foramen; supplies teeth and chin
  - **Deep auricular and anterior tympanic arteries** — supply the ear canal and tympanic membrane
  - **Accessory meningeal artery** — enters foramen ovale alongside V3

#### Second Part (Pterygoid/Muscular)
- Courses superficial (75%) or deep (25%) to the lateral pterygoid muscle in the [[infratemporal-fossa]]
- **Branches:**
  - **Deep temporal arteries** — supply temporalis muscle; encountered in [[pterional-approach]] myocutaneous flap
  - **Pterygoid branches** — supply lateral and medial pterygoid muscles
  - **Masseteric artery** — exits through the mandibular notch
  - **Buccal artery** — supplies the buccinator and cheek

#### Third Part (Pterygopalatine)
- Enters the [[pterygopalatine-fossa]] through the pterygomaxillary fissure
- **Terminal branches:**
  - **[[sphenopalatine-artery]]** — enters the nasal cavity through the sphenopalatine foramen; the dominant nasal blood supply and nasoseptal flap pedicle
  - **Descending palatine artery** — descends through the greater palatine canal to the palate
  - **Infraorbital artery** — courses through the infraorbital canal to the face
  - **Posterior superior alveolar artery** — supplies upper molars
  - **Artery of the pterygoid canal (Vidian artery)** — courses through the Vidian canal alongside the Vidian nerve to the nasopharynx; landmark for the petrous [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]]

## Surgical Significance

### Endoscopic Endonasal Surgery
- The [[sphenopalatine-artery]] (terminal branch) is the pedicle of the **nasoseptal flap** — the workhorse flap for [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach|endonasal skull base reconstruction]]
- The **Vidian artery** leads directly to the petrous [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]] — following it is the safest way to identify the ICA during transpterygoid approaches
- The descending palatine artery is a secondary flap pedicle option
- Uncontrolled [[sphenopalatine-artery]] bleeding is the most common cause of serious endonasal hemorrhage

### Lateral Skull Base Surgery
- The maxillary artery is the dominant artery in the [[infratemporal-fossa]] — it must be controlled or transposed during infratemporal approaches (Fisch type B/C)
- The [[middle-meningeal-artery]] (first part branch) is divided during all [[pterional-approach|pterional]] and [[orbitozygomatic-approach|orbitozygomatic]] craniotomies when it tethers the dura
- Juvenile nasopharyngeal angiofibromas arise near the sphenopalatine foramen and are supplied by maxillary artery branches — preoperative embolization is standard

### Epistaxis
- The [[sphenopalatine-artery]] is the source of **posterior epistaxis** — ligation or cauterization via transnasal endoscopic approach is definitive treatment
- The descending palatine artery contributes to the Kiesselbach plexus on the anterior septum (Little's area)

### EC-IC Bypass
- The maxillary artery can serve as a **high-flow bypass donor** ([[superficial-temporal-artery]] being insufficient)
- Interposition graft from maxillary artery to [[middle-cerebral-artery|MCA]] for flow replacement after ICA sacrifice

## Relationships
- [[infratemporal-fossa]] — second part courses through
- [[pterygopalatine-fossa]] — third part terminates in
- [[middle-meningeal-artery]] — first part branch, enters foramen spinosum
- [[sphenopalatine-artery]] — terminal branch, nasoseptal flap pedicle
- [[internal-carotid-artery]] — Vidian artery leads to petrous ICA
- [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach]] — maxillary artery territory in endonasal corridor
- [[pterional-approach]] — deep temporal branches in temporalis muscle
- [[skull-base-foramina]] — branches enter foramen spinosum, ovale, rotundum, pterygoid canal
