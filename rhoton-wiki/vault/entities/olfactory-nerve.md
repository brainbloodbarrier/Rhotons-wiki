# Olfactory Nerve and Tract

The olfactory nerve (CN I) is the only cranial nerve that bypasses the [[thalamus]] and the only one with direct cortical projections. The olfactory filaments (true CN I) pass through the cribriform plate of the ethmoid bone to reach the olfactory bulb on the anterior cranial fossa floor. The olfactory tract then courses posteriorly along the gyrus rectus to the [[anterior-perforated-substance]], where it splits into medial and lateral olfactory striae.

## Anatomy

### Olfactory filaments (CN I proper)
15–20 bundles of unmyelinated axons from the olfactory epithelium in the nasal cavity roof. They pass through the foramina of the cribriform plate ([[skull-base-foramina]]) — the thinnest part of the anterior [[cranial-base]] floor.

### Olfactory bulb
A flattened ovoid structure resting on the cribriform plate. Receives the olfactory filaments and processes olfactory information through glomerular synapses.

### Olfactory tract
A white band coursing posteriorly in the olfactory sulcus on the inferior surface of the frontal lobe (between the gyrus rectus medially and the orbital gyri laterally). Length ~30 mm.

### Olfactory trigone and striae
At the posterior end of the tract, it expands into the olfactory trigone and divides into:
- **Lateral olfactory stria** — courses to the piriform cortex (primary olfactory cortex) in the temporal lobe
- **Medial olfactory stria** — courses toward the septal area and subcallosal gyrus
- The striae border the [[anterior-perforated-substance]] anteriorly

## Vascular Supply

- **[[ethmoidal-arteries|Anterior ethmoidal artery]]** ([[ophthalmic-artery]]) — supplies the olfactory mucosa and cribriform plate region
- **Olfactory artery** — a small branch from the [[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]] A2 segment; supplies the olfactory bulb and tract
- **Frontopolar artery** — from the [[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]]; supplies the adjacent gyrus rectus

## Surgical Significance

### Olfactory groove meningiomas
Arise from the dura over the cribriform plate and planum sphenoidale ([[sphenoid-bone]]). They grow between the frontal lobes, displacing the olfactory tracts laterally. Often large at diagnosis due to "silent" location. Approaches:
- **Bifrontal [[surgical-approaches-overview|craniotomy]]** — bilateral subfrontal exposure; the olfactory tracts are usually sacrificed (bilateral anosmia)
- **[[pterional-approach]]** — unilateral subfrontal exposure; may preserve contralateral olfaction
- **Endoscopic endonasal** ([[transsphenoidal-approach]] extended) — approaching from below through the [[paranasal-sinuses]] and cribriform plate

### Anterior cranial fossa floor repair
CSF leaks through the cribriform plate (traumatic or spontaneous) are the most common anterior skull base CSF rhinorrhea. The anterior ethmoidal artery ([[ophthalmic-artery]]) is the landmark for the cribriform plate level during endoscopic repair.

### Esthesioneuroblastoma (olfactory neuroblastoma)
Malignant tumor arising from the olfactory neuroepithelium. Extends through the cribriform plate into the anterior cranial fossa. Requires combined craniofacial resection or [[endoscopic-endonasal-approach]].

### Interhemispheric approach ([[interhemispheric-approach]])
The anterior variant accesses the midline structures below the frontal lobes. The olfactory tracts may be encountered at the depth of the exposure, near the [[anterior-communicating-artery|ACoA]] and [[lamina-terminalis]]. CN I is the cranial nerve most at risk in the anterior [[interhemispheric-approach]].

### Frontal lobe retraction
Subfrontal approaches to the [[sellar-region]], [[optic-chiasm]], and [[anterior-communicating-artery|ACoA]] require elevating the frontal lobe off the anterior fossa floor. The olfactory tract may be avulsed from the bulb during retraction → unilateral anosmia.

## Relations

- CN I filaments pass through cribriform plate ([[cranial-base]], [[skull-base-foramina]])
- Olfactory tract courses on the inferior frontal lobe ([[cerebrum]], [[cerebral-lobes|frontal lobe]])
- Olfactory trigone borders the [[anterior-perforated-substance]] anteriorly
- [[anterior-cerebral-artery|ACA]] A1 courses above the [[optic-chiasm]] near the olfactory tract — see [[anterior-circulation]]
- [[ophthalmic-artery]] (anterior ethmoidal artery) supplies the cribriform region
- [[paranasal-sinuses]] (ethmoid) below the cribriform plate
- At risk in [[pterional-approach]] (subfrontal retraction), [[interhemispheric-approach]] (anterior variant)
- Related to [[sphenoid-bone]] (planum sphenoidale) and [[meninges]] (anterior fossa dura)
