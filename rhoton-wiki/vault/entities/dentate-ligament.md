# Dentate Ligament

The dentate ligament is a white fibrous sheet attached to the spinal cord medially and to the dura mater laterally via a series of triangular toothlike processes. At the [[foramen-magnum]], it is a critical landmark separating the [[vertebral-artery]] and ventral roots (anteriorly) from the [[posterior-spinal-artery]] and [[lower-cranial-nerves|accessory nerve (CN XI)]] (posteriorly).

## Anatomy

- **Medial attachment** — pia mater of the lateral spinal cord, between the dorsal and ventral rootlets along the entire cord length
- **Lateral attachment** — triangular processes (teeth) attach at intervals to the inner surface of the dura mater
- **Most rostral attachment** — at the level of the [[foramen-magnum]], above where the [[vertebral-artery]] pierces the dura
- The second triangular process attaches below the site where the [[vertebral-artery]] and C1 roots pierce the dura

## Relations at the Foramen Magnum

The dentate ligament divides the subarachnoid space into anterior and posterior compartments at the craniocervical junction:

| Anterior to dentate | Posterior to dentate |
|---|---|
| [[vertebral-artery]] (V4 segment) | [[posterior-spinal-artery]] |
| Ventral roots of C1 | [[lower-cranial-nerves\|CN XI]] (accessory nerve, spinal portion) |
| [[pica]] (when arising at this level) | Dorsal roots of C1 (when present) |
| [[anterior-spinal-artery]] | — |

The dentate ligament is often incorporated into the **dural cuff** around the [[vertebral-artery]] at the site of dural penetration. It may pass between the [[vertebral-artery]] and [[pica]] to reach its dural attachment.

## Surgical Significance

### Far-lateral approach ([[far-lateral-approach]])
The dentate ligament is a key orientation landmark after dural opening:
- Structures **anterior** to the ligament = [[vertebral-artery]], ventral roots → surgical target zone
- Structures **posterior** to the ligament = [[lower-cranial-nerves|CN XI]], [[posterior-spinal-artery]] → preserved zone
- **Sectioning** the upper two triangular processes increases access anterior to the spinal cord for ventral [[foramen-magnum]] lesions

### Identifying the accessory nerve
The spinal portion of [[lower-cranial-nerves|CN XI]] ascends through the [[foramen-magnum]] **posterior** to the dentate ligament at upper levels (but anterior to it at lower cervical levels). The ligament helps locate CN XI during surgery.

### Cord tethering
The dentate ligament tethers the spinal cord within the dural sac. In Chiari malformation surgery, some surgeons section the ligament to allow the cord to shift posteriorly within the canal.

## Relations

- Separates anterior from posterior structures at the [[foramen-magnum]]
- [[vertebral-artery]] anterior — often incorporated into the dural cuff
- [[lower-cranial-nerves|CN XI]] (spinal portion) posterior — ascends behind the ligament
- [[posterior-spinal-artery]] posterior — courses between ligament and CN XI
- [[pica]] may course anterior or between ligament processes
- [[anterior-spinal-artery]] anterior — on the ventral cord surface
- [[brainstem]] (cervicomedullary junction) medial
- Related to [[suboccipital-triangle]] (V3 segment approaches the dural entry nearby)
- Exposed by [[far-lateral-approach]] — see [[surgical-approaches-overview]]
- Related to [[meninges]] (pia and dura mater)
