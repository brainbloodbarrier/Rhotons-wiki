# Ascending Pharyngeal Artery

The ascending pharyngeal artery (APhA) is the smallest named branch of the [[external-carotid-artery]], arising from its medial or posterior surface near the origin. Despite its small caliber, it has outsized surgical importance: its meningeal branches are the dominant [[external-carotid-artery|ECA]] supply to the [[posterior-fossa]] dura, [[lower-cranial-nerves|CN IX-XII]] vasa nervorum, and [[jugular-foramen]] region. It is the primary feeder of paragangliomas (glomus tumors) and a key target for preoperative embolization.

## Course and Branches

### Pharyngeal Trunk
- Ascends between the [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]] and the pharyngeal constrictor muscles
- Supplies the pharyngeal wall, soft palate, and torus tubarius

### Neuromeningeal Trunk
- **Jugular branch** — enters the [[jugular-foramen]] to supply dura, CN IX-XI vasa nervorum, and the jugular bulb wall
- **Hypoglossal branch** — enters the hypoglossal canal alongside CN XII
- **Clival branches** — supply [[clivus|clival]] and [[foramen-magnum|foramen magnum]] dura

### Inferior Tympanic Artery
- Enters the inferior tympanic canaliculus
- Supplies the medial wall of the middle ear (promontory)
- Feeds glomus tympanicum tumors

## Surgical Significance

### Paraganglioma Surgery
- **Glomus jugulare** — dominant feeder is the ascending pharyngeal artery (neuromeningeal trunk → jugular branch)
- **Glomus tympanicum** — fed by inferior tympanic artery
- **Carotid body tumor** — fed by ascending pharyngeal + other ECA branches
- Preoperative embolization via APhA dramatically reduces intraoperative blood loss

### Posterior Fossa Dural Supply
- The neuromeningeal trunk supplies the [[posterior-fossa]] dura around the [[foramen-magnum]] and [[clivus]]
- Posterior fossa meningiomas may receive ECA supply via APhA branches
- Knowledge of APhA anatomy is essential for complete embolization

### Dangerous Anastomoses
- The neuromeningeal trunk can anastomose with the [[vertebral-artery]] (odontoid arcade) and [[internal-carotid-artery|ICA]] (caroticotympanic)
- Embolization must avoid reflux into these anastomoses → stroke risk
- Superselective catheterization is mandatory
