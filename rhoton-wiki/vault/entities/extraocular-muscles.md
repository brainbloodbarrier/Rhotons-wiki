# Extraocular Muscles

The six extraocular muscles control eye movements and are innervated by three cranial nerves ([[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]], [[trochlear-nerve|CN IV]], [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]]). They arise from the annular tendon (annulus of Zinn) at the orbital apex and insert on the sclera. Their anatomy is critical for understanding orbital surgery, cranial nerve palsies, and the superior orbital fissure.

## Muscles and Innervation

| Muscle | Origin | Insertion | Action | Innervation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Superior rectus** | Annular tendon (superior) | Superior sclera | Elevation, intorsion, adduction | [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] (superior division) |
| **Inferior rectus** | Annular tendon (inferior) | Inferior sclera | Depression, extorsion, adduction | [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] (inferior division) |
| **Medial rectus** | Annular tendon (medial) | Medial sclera | Adduction | [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] (inferior division) |
| **Lateral rectus** | Annular tendon (lateral) | Lateral sclera | Abduction | [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]] (sole supply) |
| **Superior oblique** | Sphenoid body (superomedial apex) | Superior sclera (via trochlea) | Depression, intorsion, abduction | [[trochlear-nerve|CN IV]] (sole supply) |
| **Inferior oblique** | Maxilla (orbital floor, anteromedial) | Inferior sclera (posterior) | Elevation, extorsion, abduction | [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] (inferior division) |
| **Levator palpebrae superioris** | Lesser wing of [[sphenoid-bone]] | Upper eyelid | Eyelid elevation | [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] (superior division) + sympathetic (Müller's muscle) |

**Mnemonic: LR6SO4** — lateral rectus = CN VI, superior oblique = CN IV, all others = CN III.

## Annular Tendon (Annulus of Zinn)

The common tendinous ring from which the four rectus muscles originate. It encircles the [[optic-canal]] and the central part of the superior orbital fissure (the oculomotor foramen):

### Structures passing THROUGH the annulus
- [[optic-nerve|CN II]] and [[ophthalmic-artery]] (via optic canal)
- [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] (both divisions)
- [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]]
- Nasociliary nerve (from [[trigeminal-nerve|V1]])
- Sympathetic root of the ciliary ganglion

### Structures passing OUTSIDE the annulus (through the lateral part of the SOF)
- [[trochlear-nerve|CN IV]] — passes above the annulus (unique: enters the [[orbit]] outside the annular tendon)
- Frontal nerve (from [[trigeminal-nerve|V1]])
- Lacrimal nerve (from [[trigeminal-nerve|V1]])
- Superior ophthalmic vein

## Orbital Relationships

The rectus muscles form a **muscle cone** that divides the [[orbit]] into intraconal and extraconal spaces:
- **Intraconal space** — contains the [[optic-nerve]], [[ophthalmic-artery]], ciliary ganglion, and orbital fat
- **Extraconal space** — contains the lacrimal gland, orbital fat, and periorbita

The intermuscular [[septum-pellucidum|septum]] connects the rectus muscles, defining the cone boundary. Intraconal tumors displace the eye forward (proptosis) while preserving motility until late.

## Surgical Significance

### Superior orbital fissure syndrome
Complete palsy of CN III, IV, V1, and VI from lesions at the SOF. All eye movements lost + forehead numbness + ptosis + fixed dilated pupil.

### Orbital apex syndrome
SOF syndrome + [[optic-nerve]] involvement (vision loss). Caused by tumors, inflammation, or infection at the orbital apex.

### Cranial nerve palsies
- **[[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]] palsy** — ptosis + eye deviated "down and out" (lateral rectus and superior oblique unopposed) + dilated pupil
- **[[trochlear-nerve|CN IV]] palsy** — head tilt away from affected side, difficulty looking down and medially (reading, stairs)
- **[[abducens-nerve|CN VI]] palsy** — esotropia (eye turned inward), inability to abduct

### Approaches to the orbit
- **Lateral orbitotomy** — through the lateral orbital wall ([[sphenoid-bone]] greater wing)
- **Transcranial** — [[pterional-approach]] or [[orbitozygomatic-approach]] with orbital roof removal
- **Endoscopic endonasal** — medial orbital wall (lamina papyracea) for medial lesions
- **Transconjunctival** — anterior orbital lesions

## Relations

- Arise from annular tendon at the [[orbit]] apex
- Innervated by [[oculomotor-nerve|CN III]], [[trochlear-nerve|CN IV]], [[abducens-nerve|CN VI]] — see [[cranial-nerves-overview]]
- [[optic-nerve]] within the muscle cone (intraconal)
- [[ophthalmic-artery]] and its branches supply the muscles
- [[trigeminal-nerve|V1]] branches (frontal, lacrimal, nasociliary) course among the muscles
- Pass through the superior orbital fissure ([[sphenoid-bone]])
- Related to [[cavernous-sinus]] (CN III, IV, VI traverse cavernous sinus before entering orbit)
- See [[skull-base-foramina]] for SOF contents
