## Overall verdict

C2 (last 20 iters) is **~35% pure signal, ~45% mixed, ~20% padding**. Score
gains from padding iters (2–3 lines × word-count component) are cheap wins that
inflate `v1` metric without adding retrievable knowledge. Breadcrumb adoption is
inconsistent (7/20 pages). Source specificity is nominally present (18/20 `yes`)
but often just "NSAtlas — Cohen-Gadol" without chapter/page — the `sources_specific`
column should tighten in future audits.

**Decision:** continue C2 only after quality gates (plan T3/T5/T6) are in place.
Historical padding iters are not reverted (append-only TSV invariant); they are
flagged in `revise-queue.md` for fix-forward on next pass.
